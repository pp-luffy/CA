100 Years of UPSC 🏛️

Historical Context

Merit-based ICS entry began in London in 1855. Satyendranath Tagore became the first Indian in the ICS in 1864. The exam age was progressively reduced from 23 to 19, intentionally disadvantaging Indians.

Revision Hooks

  • Mains The shift to conduct exams in India (Allahabad, 1922) was enabled by the Govt. of India Act 1919 (Montagu-Chelmsford).
  • Trap Alert: Sir Ross Barker was the *first* chairman of the Public Service Commission (1926).

India's Path to Blue Skies 💨
  • NCAP The National Clean Air Program targets 130 specific cities.
  • Pollutants PM2.5 (≤2.5 micrometers) easily bypasses lung filters causing severe damage.
🔓 Unlock UPSC Background & Revision Data

AQI & Measurement

The Air Quality Index (AQI) simplifies pollution data into 6 categories: Good (0-50), Satisfactory (51-100), Moderate (101-200), Poor (201-300), Very Poor (301-400), and Severe (401-500).

Revision Hooks

  • Mains Progress: 72 cities achieved a >20% reduction in particulate matter during FY 25-26.
  • Trap Alert: AQI is based on exactly 8 pollutants: PM10, PM2.5, NO2, SO2, CO, O3, NH3, Pb. Carbon Dioxide (CO2) is NOT one of them!

PM Khanij Kshetra Kalyan Yojana ⛏️
  • DMF Introduced via the 2015 amendment to the MMDR Act 1957.
  • Objective Implements welfare projects via District Mineral Foundations (DMFs) for mining-affected areas.
🔓 Unlock UPSC Background & Revision Data

District Mineral Foundations

DMFs are non-profit trusts established across 656 districts in 23 states to ensure that communities suffering the environmental/social externalities of mining receive financial remediation.

Revision Hooks

  • Mains Funds collected at the district level minimize the adverse effects on health, environment, and long-term socio-economic livelihoods.
  • Trap Alert: DMF operations fall entirely under the jurisdiction of the relevant STATE government, which frames its own distinct rules.

Clean Energy Reliability

Formulas & Mnemonics 🧠

Exam Prelims Tricks ⚠️

  • AQI Trick: Carbon Dioxide (CO2) is NEVER measured in the standard AQI.
  • Literacy Trick: ULLAS = Adults (15+). NIPUN = Children (3-8).
  • Border Trick: Longest boundary is Bangladesh, not China.
