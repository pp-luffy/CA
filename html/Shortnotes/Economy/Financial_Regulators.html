📌 Core Regulators & Exam Perspectives

Central Bank

1. Reserve Bank of India (RBI)

Est: Apr 1, 1935 HQ: Mumbai (shifted from Kolkata 1937) 26th Gov: Sanjay Malhotra
  • Origin: Recommended by Hilton Young Commission (1926); established under RBI Act, 1934.
  • Nationalization: Jan 1, 1949. First Governor: Sir Osborne Smith; 1st Indian Governor: C.D. Deshmukh.
  • Core Functions: Monetary Policy, Issuer of Currency (except ₹1 note & coins — Ministry of Finance), Banker to Govt & Banks, Lender of Last Resort, Forex Custodian (FEMA 1999), Banking & NBFC Supervision.
  • Monetary Policy Committee (MPC): Statutory (2016, Sec 45ZB of RBI Act). 6 Members (3 RBI + 3 Govt), chaired by RBI Governor (casting vote). Inflation target: 4% ± 2% (CPI).
  • Payment Systems (PSS Act 2007): Regulates RTGS (Min ₹2 Lakh, No upper limit), NEFT (No min/max), UPI, IMPS.
  • 4 Wholly-Owned Subsidiaries: DICGC (Deposit insurance up to ₹5 Lakh/depositor/bank), BRBNMPL (Note printing), ReBIT (IT/Cyber), IFTAS, plus RBIH (Innovation Hub, Bengaluru).
🔥 Exam Trap: RBI regulates Money Market (short-term, <1 yr), NOT Capital Market. Also, RBI has 4 Deputy Governors max.
Capital Market

2. SEBI (Securities & Exchange Board of India)

Est: 1988 | Statutory: 1992 HQ: Mumbai Chair: Tuhin Kanta Pandey
  • Statutory Status: Granted on Jan 30, 1992 via SEBI Act, 1992 (Narasimham Committee).
  • Domain: Regulates Stock Exchanges (NSE, BSE), Mutual Funds (OPSC PYQ!), Merchant Bankers, Credit Rating Agencies, FPIs, Venture Capital, REITs/InvITs.
  • Commodity Derivatives: Forward Markets Commission (FMC) was merged into SEBI in 2015.
  • Quasi-Powers: Quasi-legislative (regulations), Quasi-executive (investigations), Quasi-judicial (orders).
  • Appellate Body: SAT (Securities Appellate Tribunal), Mumbai — also hears appeals against IRDAI & PFRDA!
  • Key Portals: SCORES (Investor grievance redressal), SMART ODR, T+1 / T+0 settlement cycle.
🔥 Exam Trap: Mutual Funds & Credit Rating Agencies are regulated by SEBI (not RBI). Tuhin Kanta Pandey is a 1987-batch Odisha cadre IAS officer!
Insurance

3. IRDAI (Insurance Regulatory & Development Authority)

Est: 1999 | Statutory: Apr 2000 HQ: Hyderabad Chair: Ajay Seth
  • Origin: Based on Malhotra Committee (1994) recommendations; established under IRDA Act, 1999 (renamed IRDAI in 2014).
  • Composition: 10 Members — 1 Chairperson + 5 Whole-Time + 4 Part-Time members (appointed by Govt).
  • Domain: Regulates Life Insurance (LIC & private), General Insurance, Health Insurance, Reinsurance (GIC Re), Insurance Brokers & Agents.
  • Vision 2047: "Insurance for All by 2047" via Bima Trinity — Bima Sugam (marketplace), Bima Vistaar (all-in-one policy), Bima Vahak (women-led distribution).
  • FDI Limit: Increased to 74% (automatic route; Budget 2025 proposed raising to 100% for domestic investment).
🔥 Exam Trap: HQ shifted from New Delhi to Hyderabad (2001) — favorite SSC/OPSC HQ question!
Pensions

4. PFRDA (Pension Fund Regulatory & Dev. Authority)

Est: 2003 | Statutory: 2013 HQ: New Delhi Chair: Sivasubramanian Ramann
  • Origin: Set up by executive order in 2003; statutory status via PFRDA Act, 2013 (Ministry of Finance).
  • Composition: 1 Chairperson + up to 6 members (at least 3 whole-time).
  • Flagship Schemes Regulated:
    • NPS (National Pension System) — for Govt & all citizens (18–70 yrs).
    • APY (Atal Pension Yojana) — unorganized sector (18–40 yrs; ₹1,000–₹5,000 guaranteed pension at 60).
    • NPS Vatsalya (for minors) & UPS (Unified Pension Scheme).
  • Intermediaries: Regulates Pension Funds, Central Recordkeeping Agencies (CRAs like Protean/KFintech), Points of Presence (PoPs).
🔥 Exam Trap: EPFO (Employees' Provident Fund) is NOT under PFRDA — it is under Ministry of Labour & Employment!
Unified IFSC

5. IFSCA (Int'l Financial Services Centres Authority)

Est: Apr 27, 2020 HQ: GIFT City, Gandhinagar Chair: K. Rajaraman
  • Act: IFSCA Act, 2019 (Statutory body under Ministry of Finance).
  • Why Unique? India's first unified financial regulator. Inside GIFT City (Gujarat), it exercises the combined powers of RBI + SEBI + IRDAI + PFRDA.
  • Composition: 9 Members — Chairperson + 1 nominee each from RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA + 2 MoF officials + 2 whole-time/part-time members.
  • Key Initiatives: India International Bullion Exchange (IIBX), Global FinTech Hub, Foreign Universities in GIFT City, Aircraft/Ship leasing.
🔥 Exam Trap: Only major financial regulator headquartered in Gandhinagar (Gujarat).
Insolvency

6. IBBI (Insolvency & Bankruptcy Board of India)

Est: Oct 1, 2016 HQ: New Delhi Chair (Addl.): Jayanti Prasad
  • Act: Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016 (Ministry of Corporate Affairs).
  • Unique Role: Regulates both Profession (Insolvency Professionals, IP Agencies, Information Utilities like NeSL) and Processes (CIRP, Liquidation, Pre-Packs).
  • Adjudicating Authorities:
    • NCLT (National Company Law Tribunal) → Companies & LLPs.
    • DRT (Debt Recovery Tribunal) → Individuals & Partnerships.
  • Timeline: Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) must conclude within 180 days (+90 days extension; max 330 days including litigation).
🔥 Exam Trap: IBBI falls under Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), NOT Ministry of Finance!
Agri & Rural

7. NABARD (National Bank for Agri & Rural Dev.)

Est: July 12, 1982 HQ: Mumbai Chair: Shaji K.V.
  • Origin: Recommended by B. Sivaraman Committee (CRAFICARD) during the 6th Five-Year Plan; under NABARD Act, 1981.
  • Ownership: 100% Government of India (RBI sold its remaining stake in 2019).
  • Supervisory Role: Supervises RRBs (Regional Rural Banks) & Rural Cooperative Banks (State & District Co-op Banks). Note: Banking license still comes from RBI!
  • Key Funds & Schemes: RIDF (Rural Infrastructure Development Fund — funded by banks' PSL shortfalls), SHG-Bank Linkage, Kisan Credit Card (KCC), E-Shakti.
MSME Apex

8. SIDBI (Small Industries Dev. Bank of India)

Est: Apr 2, 1990 HQ: Lucknow CMD: Manoj Mittal
  • Act: SIDBI Act, 1989 (All-India Financial Institution — AIFI regulated by RBI).
  • Role: Principal financial institution for promotion, financing & development of the MSME sector.
  • Key Subsidiaries / Initiatives:
    • MUDRA Bank (Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency — Shishu, Kishore, Tarun, Tarun Plus).
    • CGTMSE (Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro & Small Enterprises — joint with MSME Ministry).
    • UDYAMI Helpline & PSB Loans in 59 Minutes.
🔥 Exam Trap: HQ is in Lucknow (UP) — frequently tested in Banking & OPSC exams!
AIFIs & Housing

9. NHB, EXIM Bank & NaBFID

  • NHB (National Housing Bank):
    • Est: July 9, 1988 (NHB Act 1987) | HQ: New Delhi.
    • 100% Govt owned (transferred from RBI in 2019).
    • Trap: Regulation of Housing Finance Companies (HFCs) shifted from NHB to RBI in Aug 2019! NHB now focuses on refinancing & supervision. Publishes RESIDEX.
  • EXIM Bank (Export-Import Bank): Est: Jan 1, 1982 (EXIM Bank Act 1981) | HQ: Mumbai. Finances foreign trade.
  • NaBFID (2021): 5th AIFI (with NABARD, SIDBI, NHB, EXIM). HQ: Mumbai. Long-term Infrastructure financing.
Coordination & Payments

10. FSDC & NPCI (High-Yield Extras)

  • FSDC (Financial Stability & Development Council):
    • Est: Dec 2010 (Raghuram Rajan Committee). Non-statutory body.
    • Chairperson: Union Finance Minister.
    • Members: Heads of RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA, IBBI, IFSCA + Finance Secy + CEA.
    • FSDC Sub-Committee: Chaired by RBI Governor.
  • NPCI (National Payments Corp. of India):
    • Est: 2008 (Not-for-profit Sec 8 company; initiative of RBI & IBA). HQ: Mumbai.
    • Operates: UPI, RuPay, IMPS, AePS, FASTag (NETC), NACH, BBPS.
