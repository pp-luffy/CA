UPSC / OPSC ASO: Economic Planning in India

1st Plan (51-56) Harrod-Domar. Agriculture & Dams (Hirakud). Successful.
2nd Plan (56-61) Mahalanobis. Heavy Industry (Steel plants).
3rd Plan (61-66) Gadgil Yojana. Failed (Wars + Drought).
Plan Holiday (66-69) Green Revolution started. Rupee Devalued.
4th Plan (69-74) 14 Banks Nationalized. Smiling Buddha (74).
5th Plan (74-78) Garibi Hatao. Terminated early by Janata Govt.
Rolling Plan (78-80) Janata Govt. Discarded by Indira Gandhi.
6th Plan (80-85) NABARD (82) & EXIM established.
7th Plan (85-90) Private sector priority. Broke 'Hindu Rate of Growth'.
Annual Plans (90-92) 1991 LPG Reforms (Rao-Manmohan).
8th Plan (92-97) HRD focus. WTO joined (95). Panchayat Acts (73rd/74th).
11th Plan (07-12) Highest growth (8%). RTE Act (2009).
NITI Aayog (2015) Think Tank. Bottom-up approach. Cooperative Federalism.
Foundation

Pre-Independence Plans

  • 1934 Visvesvaraya Plan: 1st blueprint (Planned Economy).
  • 1938 National Planning Comm: Set by SC Bose, chaired by Nehru.
  • 1944 Bombay Plan: By 8 Industrialists (Tata, Birla).
  • 1944 Gandhian Plan: S.N. Agarwal (Agri/Cottage).
  • 1945 People's Plan: M.N. Roy (Marxist).
  • 1950 Sarvodaya Plan: JP Narayan.

Planning Commission (1950): Non-constitutional, advisory body.
NDC (1952): Final approval body, included CMs.

FYP 1

1st Five-Year Plan

1951–1956

Model: Harrod-Domar

Focus: Agriculture, price stability, power.

  • Massive projects: Hirakud Dam (Odisha), Bhakra Nangal.
  • 5 IITs & UGC established.
  • Community Development Prog (1952).

Result: Highly successful (Achieved 3.6%).

FYP 2

2nd Five-Year Plan

1956–1961

Model: P.C. Mahalanobis

Focus: Rapid Industrialization (Heavy).

  • Steel plants: Rourkela (Odisha - German help), Bhilai (Russian help), Durgapur (UK help).
  • Atomic Energy Commission (1957).

Result: Moderate success (Forex crisis).

FYP 3

3rd Five-Year Plan

1961–1966

Model: Gadgil Yojana

Focus: Self-reliance, Agriculture.

  • FCI and Agri Prices Commission established (1965).

Result: Massive Failure due to Sino-Indian War (62), Indo-Pak War (65), & severe drought.

Break

Plan Holiday (3 Annual Plans)

1966–1969

Context: Triggered by 3rd Plan failure & lack of resources.

  • Devaluation of Rupee (1966).
  • Implementation of Green Revolution (led by M.S. Swaminathan).
FYP 4

4th Five-Year Plan

1969–1974

Model: Gadgil Formula

Focus: Growth with stability.

  • Nationalization of 14 Banks (1969).
  • 1971 Indo-Pak War (Bangladesh creation).
  • Smiling Buddha (1974): 1st Nuclear test.

Result: Failed targets (Oil Crisis '73).

FYP 5 & Break

5th Plan & Rolling Plan

1974–1978 & 1978-1980

5th Plan Focus: Garibi Hatao (Poverty Eradication). Drafted by D.P. Dhar. Minimum Needs Prog.

Result: Terminated early (1978) by Morarji Desai.

Rolling Plan (78-80): Introduced by Janata Party. Discarded by Indira Gandhi in 1980.

FYP 6

6th Five-Year Plan

1980–1985

Focus: Tech self-reliance, poverty.

  • Beginning of economic lib (End of Nehruvian socialism).
  • 6 more banks nationalized (1980).
  • NABARD established (1982).

Result: Successful (Achieved 5.7%).

FYP 7 & Crisis

7th Plan & The Crisis

1985–1990 & 1990-1992

7th Plan: Pvt sector prioritized. Jawahar Rozgar Yojana. SEBI established (88). Broke 'Hindu Rate of Growth'.

Annual Plans (90-92): Delayed 8th Plan due to Forex crisis.

  • 1991 LPG Reforms: Lib, Priv, Glob (Rao-Manmohan).
FYP 8 & 9

8th & 9th Plans

1992–1997 & 1997-2002

8th Plan (Rao-Manmohan): HRD focus. Joined WTO (1995). 73rd/74th Amendments. Highly successful.

9th Plan: "Growth with Social Justice". Hit by Asian Financial Crisis (97), Pokhran-II, Kargil. Failed targets.

FYP 10 & 11

10th & 11th Plans

2002–2007 & 2007-2012

10th Plan: Target to double per capita income in 10yrs. High growth.

11th Plan (C. Rangarajan): "Faster and More Inclusive Growth".

  • RTE Act enacted (2009).
  • Achieved 8% growth (highest ever actual FYP rate).
FYP 12

12th Five-Year Plan

2012–2017

Theme: "Faster, More Inclusive and Sustainable Growth".

  • 8% GDP target.
  • Reduce IMR to 25, MMR to 1.

Note: The Final Five-Year Plan.

Modern Era

NITI Aayog

Jan 1, 2015

National Institution for Transforming India.

  • Role: Premier "Think Tank" of Govt.
  • Philosophy: Cooperative Federalism (States are equal partners).
  • Approach: Bottom-Up (village to center).

Has NO power to allocate funds (shifted to Finance Ministry). Governing Council includes all CMs.
