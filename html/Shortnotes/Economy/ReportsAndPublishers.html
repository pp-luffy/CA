🌍 Part 1: International Reports & Publishers

Bretton Woods

1. World Bank

  • World Development Report (WDR) — Annual flagship on global development.
  • Global Economic Prospects (GEP) — Semi-annual economic growth forecasts.
  • South Asia Economic Focus (now South Asia Development Update) — OPSC ASO 2021 PYQ!
  • Logistics Performance Index (LPI) — Trade logistics efficiency (India: 38th).
  • Business Ready (B-READY) — New flagship replacing Ease of Doing Business (discontinued in 2021).
  • International Debt Report & Migration & Development Brief (Global Remittances — India #1 recipient).
  • Human Capital Index (HCI) & Service Trade Restriction Index.
🔥 Exam Trap: "World Development Report" = World Bank; "Human Development Report" = UNDP.
Bretton Woods

2. International Monetary Fund (IMF)

  • World Economic Outlook (WEO) — Published twice a year (April & October); global GDP growth & inflation forecasts.
  • Global Financial Stability Report (GFSR) — Assesses systemic risks to global financial markets.
  • Fiscal Monitor — Tracks public finance, government debt & deficits worldwide.
  • External Sector Report — Current account balances & exchange rates.
🔥 Exam Trap: Global Financial Stability Report = IMF; Indian Financial Stability Report = RBI!
Non-UN • Davos

3. World Economic Forum (WEF)

  • Global Gender Gap Report — 4 pillars (Eco, Edu, Health, Politics); India: 131st of 148 (2025).
  • Global Risks Report — 2026 edition: Geoeconomic confrontation = top short-term global risk; Cyber insecurity = major risk for India.
  • Energy Transition Index (ETI) — Clean energy readiness (India: 71st, 2025).
  • Travel & Tourism Development Index (TTDI) — India: 39th of 119 (2024).
  • Global Competitiveness Report, Future of Jobs Report, Global Social Mobility Index, Global IT Report, Enabling Trade Report.
🔥 Exam Trap: WEF is NOT a UN agency (it's a Swiss NGO). Also, World Competitiveness Index is by IMD (not WEF)!
UN Programme

4. UNDP (UN Development Programme)

  • Human Development Report (HDR) & HDI — Since 1990 (Mahbub ul Haq & Amartya Sen). OPSC ASO 2015 PYQ!
    • 3 Dimensions: Health (Life expectancy), Education (Mean & Expected yrs), Income (GNI per capita PPP).
    • Latest (2025): India 130th of 193 (HDI 0.685).
  • Gender Inequality Index (GII) — India: 108th.
  • Gender Development Index (GDI) & Inequality-Adjusted HDI (IHDI).
  • Global Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI) — Jointly with OPHI (Oxford University).
Trade & Policy

5. UNCTAD, WTO, OECD & BIS

  • UNCTAD (UN Trade & Dev.):
    • World Investment Report (Global FDI inflows/outflows).
    • Trade and Development Report & Review of Maritime Transport.
  • WTO (World Trade Org.): World Trade Report, World Trade Statistical Review, World Trade Outlook Indicator.
  • OECD (Paris): PISA (Programme for International Student Assessment), OECD Economic Outlook, Better Life Index.
  • BIS (Basel): Global Financial System Report.
  • FATF: Global Money Laundering Report (Grey & Black Lists).
UN Agencies

6. Key UN Agencies (One-Liner Goldmine)

  • WIPO: Global Innovation Index (GII) (India: 38th, 2025) & World IP Indicators.
  • UN SDSN: World Happiness Report (India: 118th, 2025).
  • UNEP: Emissions Gap Report, Adaptation Gap Report, Global Environment Outlook (GEO).
  • FAO: State of Food Security & Nutrition (SOFI), Food Price Index, State of World's Forests (SOFO).
  • ILO: World Employment & Social Outlook (WESO), World Social Protection Report, Global Wage Report.
  • UN-DESA: World Economic Situation & Prospects (WESP), World Social Report, World's Women Report.
  • UNFPA: State of World Population Report.
  • UNESCO: Global Education Monitoring (GEM) Report, World Water Development Report (UN-Water).
  • UN-Habitat: World Cities Report | WHO: World Health Statistics, Global TB Report.
  • WMO: State of Global Climate Report, Greenhouse Gas Bulletin.
  • IEA (Energy): World Energy Outlook & Global Energy Review.
Non-UN NGOs

7. Global Watchdogs & Think Tanks

  • Global Hunger Index (GHI) → Concern Worldwide & Welthungerhilfe (India: 102nd, 2025; 4 indicators: undernourishment, child stunting, wasting, mortality).
  • Corruption Perceptions Index (CPI) → Transparency International (India: 91st, score 39; Denmark #1).
  • World Press Freedom Index → Reporters Without Borders (RSF) (India: 151st; Norway #1).
  • Global Peace Index (GPI) & Global Terrorism Index (GTI) → Institute for Economics & Peace (IEP), Australia (GPI 115th; GTI 14th).
  • Climate Change Performance Index (CCPI) → Germanwatch, NewClimate Inst. & CAN (India: 10th).
  • Global Climate Risk Index (CRI) → Germanwatch.
  • Living Planet Report → WWF | Rule of Law Index → World Justice Project (86th).
  • Henley Passport Index → Henley & Partners (Singapore #1).
  • Democracy Index & Global Liveability Index → Economist Intelligence Unit (EIU).
  • International IP Index → US Chamber of Commerce (38th).
  • World Competitiveness Index → IMD (41st) | Index of Economic Freedom → Heritage Foundation.
  • World Air Quality Report → IQAir | Air Quality Life Index → EPIC (Univ. of Chicago).
  • Arms Transfers Report → SIPRI | Global Financial Centres Index → Z/Yen Group (GIFT City 43rd).
  • Global AI Vibrancy Index → Stanford HAI (India: 3rd).

🇮🇳 Part 2: Indian National Reports, Indices & Publishers

Think Tank

8. NITI Aayog Indices

  • SDG India Index — Tracks UN Sustainable Development Goals across states/UTs.
  • National Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI) — Official poverty assessment body (OPSC ASO 2021 PYQ!).
  • Fiscal Health Index (FHI) — Evaluates state fiscal sustainability & debt management.
  • Export Preparedness Index (EPI) — Maharashtra topped large states (2024).
  • India Innovation Index (with Institute for Competitiveness).
  • Composite Water Management Index (CWMI).
  • State Health Index & School Education Quality Index (SEQI).
  • State Energy & Climate Index (SECI).
🌟 OPSC ASO Special: In NITI Aayog's Fiscal Health Index (FY 2023–24), Odisha ranked #1 across India!
Macro & Banking

9. RBI, NSO & DPIIT (Macro Indices)

  • Reserve Bank of India (RBI):
    • Financial Stability Report (FSR) — Bi-annual.
    • Financial Inclusion Index (FI-Index) — Scale 0–100 (67 in Mar 2025; Usage has max 45% weight).
    • Digital Payments Index (DPI) — Tracks digital payment adoption (Base: Mar 2018 = 100).
    • Report on Trend & Progress of Banking in India.
  • NSO (MoSPI):
    • CPI (Consumer Price Index) — Retail inflation (RBI's MPC target).
    • IIP (Index of Industrial Production) — Industrial activity.
    • PLFS (Periodic Labour Force Survey) & GDP Estimates.
  • DPIIT (Min. of Commerce & Industry):
    • WPI (Wholesale Price Index) — Goods only (no services!).
    • Index of Eight Core Industries (ICI) — 40.27% weight in IIP (Refinery products has max weight).
  • Labour Bureau (Min. of Labour): CPI-IW (Industrial Workers), CPI-AL (Agri), CPI-RL (Rural).
Must-Know India

10. Other High-Yield Indian Reports

  • ASER (Annual Status of Education Report) → Pratham NGO (NOT Ministry of Education!).
  • India State of Forest Report (ISFR) → Forest Survey of India (FSI), Dehradun (Biennial).
  • Economic Survey of India → Ministry of Finance (DEA), authored by Chief Economic Adviser.
  • RESIDEX (Housing prices) → National Housing Bank (NHB).
  • Swachh Survekshan → Min. of Housing & Urban Affairs (MoHUA).
  • National Air Quality Index (AQI) → CPCB (8 pollutants).
  • Crime in India Report → NCRB (Ministry of Home Affairs).
  • Good Governance Index (GGI) → DARPG (Min. of Personnel).
  • AISHE (Higher Education Survey) → Ministry of Education.
  • National Family Health Survey (NFHS) → MoHFW (Nodal: IIPS Mumbai).
