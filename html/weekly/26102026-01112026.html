12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum 2026

Background Context

Held in New Delhi, the theme was "Fostering parliaments for people-centric development." Focuses on SDGs, poverty eradication, climate finance, and safe AI public infrastructure.

⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert

CCIT (Comprehensive Convention on International Terrorism) is a proposed international framework—it is called for early adoption, not yet fully enforced universally.

✍️ Mains Analytical Hook

BRICS acts as the voice of the Global South, directly demanding reforms in the United Nations Security Council (UNSC) for equitable representation.

Drishti IAS Diwali Sale

Background Context

A twin celebration for Diwali and Drishti IAS Foundation Day, providing massive discounts on test series, books, and courses up to 60% off.

⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert

Ensure you know the deadline—November 6, 2026 is the last day to avail the foundation and flagship course discounts.

✍️ Mains Analytical Hook

Includes access to comprehensive essay writing classes and personal mentorship, crucial for scaling the UPSC Mains examination.

India at CBD COP 17

Background Context

Conference of Parties 17 for the Convention on Biological Diversity took place in Yerevan, Armenia. Focuses on the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework.

⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert

The Kunming-Montreal Framework aims to halt/reverse biodiversity loss by 2030 and achieve a world living in harmony with nature by 2050.

🗺️ Statutory Anchors

India highlighted its Cali Fund support for sharing benefits from Digital Sequence Information (DSI) on genetic resources. India operates 1,134 protected areas, 103 Ramsar sites, and 18 Biosphere Reserves.

Pradhan Mantri Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana

Background Context

A centrally sponsored scheme (2026-27 to 2030-31) to empower 2.55 lakh Gram Panchayats as centers of effective grassroot governance.

⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert

The scheme introduces the Pancham platform, which is an AI-enabled multilingual tool for spreading awareness and connecting national priorities with local needs.

✍️ Mains Analytical Hook

Focuses on financial accountability by helping Panchayats generate Own Source Revenue and utilizing e-GramSwaraj for digital governance.

Committee on Banking for Viksit Bharat

Background Context

A High-Level Committee chaired by former CVC Suresh N Patel with a 6-month tenure to review India's banking system for 2047 readiness.

⚖️ Statutory Anchors

The committee's terms of reference include reviewing banking rules for Commercial Banks, Regional Rural Banks (RRBs), and Cooperative Banks, as well as Priority Sector Lending.

✍️ Mains Analytical Hook

Balancing AI and digital banking expansion while strictly protecting against cyber threats, ensuring banks are globally competitive yet financially inclusive.

ASUSE Data (2027-28)

Background Context

Annual Survey of Unincorporated Sector Enterprises conducted by the NSO (MOSPI) to collect data on India's non-agricultural enterprises.

⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert

The survey cycle has officially shifted from the calendar year (Jan-Dec) to the Financial Year (April-March).

✍️ Mains Analytical Hook

Newly incorporates questionnaires to capture gender-specific challenges faced by female entrepreneurs, alongside tracking e-commerce, digital payments (UPI), and traditional sectors like handlooms.

Delhi Declaration 2026 on Local Climate Action

Background Context

Adopted at the ARISE Cities Forum in New Delhi. Aims to strengthen the role of cities and local governments in global climate governance, particularly in developing economies.

⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert

The theme is "Bharat to Antalya: Commit, Implement, and Deliver," indicating the declaration will be carried forward to COP 31 in Turkey.

✍️ Mains Analytical Hook

Emphasizes a circular economy and zero-waste initiatives while demanding that climate finance must bypass bureaucratic bottlenecks to directly reach local community governments.

Flex Fuel Rules (E85 / E100)

Background Context

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways updated the Central Motor Vehicle Rules to include provisions for high-ethanol blends (E85 and E100).

⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert

A vehicle certified for E10 or E20 is NOT automatically compatible with E85. There is no mandatory switch; E85 must only be used in certified Flex-Fuel engines.

✍️ Mains Analytical Hook

Flex-fuel vehicles automatically adjust engine operations based on the ethanol-petrol mixture, drastically lowering dependence on imported crude oil and reducing greenhouse emissions.

GSLV F18 & NVS-03 Mission

Background Context

ISRO launched the NVS-03 navigation satellite using the GSLV F18 rocket from Satish Dhawan Space Centre to a Geosynchronous Transfer Orbit.

⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert

NavIC provides two services: Standard Positioning Service (open to civilians) and Restricted Service (encrypted, strictly for authorized strategic users only).

🗺️ Statutory Anchors

The NVS-03 carries a highly critical indigenously developed atomic clock. The GSLV utilizes a domestic cryogenic upper stage (liquid hydrogen/oxygen), reducing foreign reliance.
