Age

🎂 Minimum Age & Retirement

Minimum Qualification

  • 21 Years: Panchayat, Municipality
  • 25 Years: Lok Sabha, MLA, PM, CM
  • 30 Years: Rajya Sabha, MLC
  • 35 Years: President, VP, Governor

Retirement / Max Age

  • 62 Years: HC Judges, SPSC (Chair/Members)
  • 65 Years: SC Judges, UPSC, CAG, CEC, CVC
  • 70 Years: Lokpal, NHRC/SHRC (Chair/Members)
Days

📅 Days & Time Limits

Dual Membership (10/14/60 Rule)

  • 10 Days: Elected to BOTH LS & RS (inform EC, else RS vacant).
  • 14 Days: Elected to BOTH Parl & State Leg (resign State, else Parl vacant).
  • 60 Days: Absent from all meetings (seat vacant).

Notices & Delays

  • 14 Days Notice: Impeach Pres / Remove VP, Speaker, Dy Speaker.
  • 2 Months Notice: Civil suit vs Pres/Gov for personal acts.
  • 14 Days Delay: Money Bill (RS/Council).
  • 4 Months Delay: Ordinary Bill in State Council (3m + 1m).
  • 6 Months Delay: Ordinary Bill in RS = Deadlock / Joint Sitting.

Detention (Art 22)

  • 24 Hours: Present to Magistrate (Normal arrest).
  • 3 Months: Max detention without trial (Preventive).
Months

📆 Months & Weeks

Emergency Rule (1 / 2)

  • 1 Month: Parl approval for National Emergency (Art 352).
  • 2 Months: Parl approval for Pres Rule (356) & Financial (360).

The "6" Rule (Sessions/Ordinances)

  • 6 Months: Max gap between sessions.
  • 6 Months: Hold elections for Pres/VP/Local vacancies.
  • 6 Months: Max time non-member can be Minister/PM/CM.
  • 6 Months: Nominated MP window to join a party safely.
  • 6 Weeks: Limit to approve Ordinance once reassembled.
    (Max Ordinance life: 6m + 6w)
House

🏛️ House Strengths, Quorum & Majorities

Maximum / Minimum Limits

  • Lok Sabha: 550 Max (Anglo-Indian abolished).
  • Rajya Sabha: 250 Max (238 Elec + 12 Nom).
  • State Assembly: 500 Max, 60 Min.
  • State Council: Max = 1/3rd of Assembly. Min = 40.
  • Quorum: 1/10th of total membership.

Majorities & The 15% Rule

  • Cabinet (91st Amend): Max 15% of LS/Assembly. State minimum is 12.
  • Simple: >50% present & voting.
  • Absolute: >50% of total membership.
  • Effective: >50% of (Total - Vacancies).
  • Special (368): 2/3rd P&V + Absolute Maj.
  • Special (61 Impeach): 2/3rd of Total Membership.

🗳️ Elections, Population & Citizenship

Elections

  • GST Council: 50% Quorum. 75% weighted maj to decide (Centre 1/3rd, States 2/3rd).
  • Deposit Loss: If votes < 1/6th.
  • President: 50 Proposers + 50 Seconders (₹15k).
  • Vice-President: 20 Proposers + 20 Seconders (₹15k).
  • SC/ST Parliament Resv: valid till Jan 25, 2030.

Population Limits (Local)

  • 20 Lakhs: Min pop for Block Panchayat.
  • 3 Lakhs: Min pop for Wards Committee.
  • 10 Lakhs: Min pop for Metropolitan Area.

Citizenship Limits

  • Registration: 7 years residence.
  • Naturalization: 12 years (11 + 1).
    *Reduced to 5 years for CAA 2019 groups.
