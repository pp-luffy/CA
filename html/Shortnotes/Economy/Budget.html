01 • Context & Vision

🏛️ Why in News & The Three Kartavyas

  • Presented in Parliament by the Union Minister of Finance and Corporate Affairs — marks the first Budget prepared in the newly inaugurated Kartavya Bhawan.
  • Framed as a Yuva Shakti-driven Budget, anchored in the vision of Viksit Bharat.
  • Three Guiding Principles:
    • Action over Ambivalence
    • Reform over Rhetoric
    • People over Populism
  • Guided by Three Kartavyas (Duties):
    • 1. Sustain Economic Growth: Enhance productivity, competitiveness, and build resilience against volatile global dynamics.
    • 2. Fulfill Aspirations: Build the capacity of youth and citizens, making them strong partners in India's prosperity.
    • 3. Sabka Saath, Sabka Vikas: Ensure every family, community, region, and sector has access to resources and opportunities, focusing on the "Last Mile".

Fiscal Prudence + Capex Push
03 • Kartavya 1 (Part A)

🏭 7 Strategic & Frontier Sectors

  • 1. Biopharma SHAKTI (Strategy for Healthcare Advancement through Knowledge, Technology and Innovation):
    • Outlay: ₹10,000 crore over 5 years to develop India as a global biopharma manufacturing centre.
    • Focus on biologics & biosimilars; 3 new NIPERs, upgradation of 7 existing institutes, strengthening CDSCO to global standards.
  • 2. India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) 2.0:
    • Builds on ISM 1.0 for technological sovereignty.
    • Focus on manufacturing semiconductor equipment & materials, resilient supply chains, industry-led R&D and training centres for skilled workforce (economic & national security).
  • 3. Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme:
    • Outlay hiked from ₹22,919 crore → ₹40,000 crore to deepen domestic value chains and boost electronics manufacturing.
  • 4. Rare Earth Corridors & Chemical Parks:
    • Rare Earth Corridors in Odisha, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, and Tamil Nadu for mining, processing, and manufacturing of Rare Earth Permanent Magnets (REPM).
    • 3 Chemical Parks under a cluster-based, plug-and-play model to reduce import dependence.
  • 5. Capital Goods & Container Manufacturing:
    • Hi-Tech Tool Rooms by CPSEs; Construction & Infrastructure Equipment (CIE) Scheme; ₹10,000 crore Container Manufacturing Scheme.
  • 6. Textile Sector Push:
    • Integrated Textile Programme: National Fibre Scheme, Samarth 2.0, Tex-Eco Initiative, cluster modernisation + Mega Textile Parks (technical textiles & value addition).
  • 7. Gram Swaraj & Sports Goods:
    • Mahatma Gandhi Gram Swaraj initiative (khadi, handloom, handicrafts) + Sports goods manufacturing initiative (global hub for affordable, high-quality sports equipment).
Global Manufacturing Hub Strategy
04 • Kartavya 1 (Part B)

🚄 Legacy Clusters, MSMEs & Growth Connectors

  • Rejuvenating Legacy Industrial Sectors: Scheme to revive 200 legacy industrial clusters (cost competitiveness & efficiency via infra and tech upgradation).
  • Champion MSMEs:
    • Dedicated ₹10,000 crore "SME Growth Fund" to incentivize high-potential firms and create globally competitive "Champion MSMEs".
    • Self-Reliant India Fund gets additional ₹2,000 crore (micro enterprises, steady risk capital).
    • 'Corporate Mitras' envisioned as key enablers to mentor, guide, and integrate enterprises into larger value chains.
  • Infrastructure as "Growth Connectors":
    • 7 High-Speed Rail Corridors: Mumbai–Pune, Pune–Hyderabad, Hyderabad–Bengaluru, Hyderabad–Chennai, Chennai–Bengaluru, Delhi–Varanasi, Varanasi–Siliguri.
    • Sustainable Cargo Movement: New Dedicated Freight Corridor (Dankuni to Surat) + operationalisation of 20 National Waterways over next 5 years.
    • Coastal Cargo Promotion Scheme: Shift cargo from road/rail to waterways & coastal shipping to raise share from 6% to 12% by 2047.
    • Seaplane VGF Scheme: Indigenised seaplane manufacturing & operations for last-mile connectivity and tourism.
    • Infrastructure Risk Guarantee Fund: Prudently calibrated partial credit guarantees to lenders during infra development and construction phase.
  • City Economic Regions (CER): Map cities based on specific growth drivers; ₹5,000 crore per CER over 5 years via "challenge mode".
  • Carbon Capture (CCUS): Carbon Capture, Utilization, and Storage scheme to decarbonize hard-to-abate sectors (Steel, Cement).
Connectivity, Urban Hubs & Decarbonisation
05 • Kartavya 2

🚀 Fulfill Aspirations & Build Capacity

  • AVGC Content Creator Labs ("Orange Economy"):
    • Support Indian Institute of Creative Technologies, Mumbai to set up Animation, Visual Effects, Gaming and Comics (AVGC) Content Creator Labs in 15,000 secondary schools and 500 colleges.
  • National Institute of Hospitality:
    • Proposed by upgrading existing National Council for Hotel Management and Catering Technology — bridges gap between academia and tourism industry.
  • Khelo India Mission:
    • Builds on Khelo India programme to transform sports via integrated talent development, coach capacity building, sports science integration, competitive leagues, and expanded sports infrastructure.
  • Medical Value Tourism:
    • 5 Regional Medical Hubs in partnership with private sector — integrated facilities including AYUSH Centres, diagnostics, post-care, and rehabilitation services.
  • Women in STEM:
    • One girls' hostel in every district through Viability Gap Funding (VGF) or capital support.
Youth, Orange Economy, Sports & STEM
06 • Kartavya 3

🤝 Sabka Saath, Sabka Vikas (Last Mile)

  • Bharat-VISTAAR (Virtually Integrated System to Access Agricultural Resources):
    • Multilingual AI platform integrating AgriStack and ICAR data for customized farmer advisory.
  • SHE Marts (Self-Help Entrepreneur Marts):
    • Builds on success of "Lakhpati Didis" — community-owned retail outlets set up within cluster federations.
  • Mental Health Infrastructure:
    • Setting up "NIMHANS-2" and upgrading institutes in Ranchi and Tezpur to "Regional Apex Institutions".
  • Purvodaya & North-East:
    • Buddhist Circuits: Specific scheme in North East Region (Arunachal Pradesh, Sikkim, Assam, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura).
    • East Coast Industrial Corridor: Well-connected node at Durgapur (West Bengal) + creation of 5 tourism destinations in 5 Purvodaya States.
  • Divyangjan Support:
    • Targeted empowerment of differently-abled through schemes like "Divyang Sahara Yojana".
Inclusive Growth & Regional Balance
07 • Tax Reforms (Direct)

💼 Direct Tax & Compliance Overhaul

  • New Income Tax Act, 2025: Replaces Income Tax Act, 1961 — effective from 1st April 2026.
  • Tax Rates: No changes to tax slabs for FY 2026–27; stability maintained.
  • TCS Rationalization: TCS on overseas tour packages and LRS remittances for education/medical purposes reduced to a uniform 2% (without any threshold).
  • TDS Rationalization:
    • TDS on supply of manpower services fixed at 1% (Individuals/HUF) or 2% (others).
    • Decriminalized: Non-production of books of accounts & failure to pay TDS (where payment is made in kind).
  • Securities Transaction Tax (STT): Marginally increased (0.1% → 0.15% in certain segments) to curb excessive equity speculation.
  • Minimum Alternate Tax (MAT): Exemption to all non-residents paying tax on presumptive basis.
  • Tax Administration: Joint Committee of MCA & CBDT to incorporate ICDS into IndAS (eliminating separate ICDS accounting from tax year 2027–28); rationalising definition of accountant under Safe Harbour Rules.
  • Immunity from Prosecution: Non-disclosure of foreign assets worth less than ₹20 lakh granted immunity (retrospective from 1st October 2024).
  • Buyback Tax Shift: Share buybacks taxed as Capital Gains in hands of shareholder (burden shifted from company to recipient).
Simplification & Certainty
08 • Tax Reforms (Customs & Incentives)

🌐 Strategic Tax Holidays & Customs Modernisation

  • Customs Duty Rationalization:
    • Tariff on goods imported for personal use cut from 20% → 10%.
    • Full exemption on 17 cancer drugs and medicines/foods for 7 rare diseases.
  • Strategic Incentives for Investment & Industry:
    • Data Centers Tax Holiday: Foreign companies providing global cloud services via Indian data centers get tax holiday until 2047.
    • IFSC (GIFT City): Tax holiday extended from 10 → 20 years for Offshore Banking Units.
    • Critical Minerals: Customs duty exemptions on capital goods for processing critical minerals (Lithium, Cobalt, etc.) & manufacturing Lithium-ion cells.
    • IT Sector Safe Harbour: Threshold enhanced to ₹2,000 crore; unified category for software development and KPO services.
  • Customs Modernization & Export Promotion:
    • Sectoral Relief: Duty-free import limits increased for inputs in Marine, Leather, and Textile sectors.
    • Aviation & Defence: Duty exemptions for parts/components used in aircraft manufacture & MRO (Maintenance, Repair, Overhaul).
  • Ease of Doing Business:
    • Single digital window for cargo clearance, instant customs clearance for non-compliant goods, rollout of Customs Integrated System (CIS), expanded AI-based container scanning.
    • EEZ / High Seas fish catch made duty-free; revised duty-free baggage allowances; dispute settlement for honest taxpayers with reduced penalty.
Global Capital & Trade Facilitation
09 • Critical Analysis

⚠️ Concerns Regarding Union Budget 2026–27

  • Global Headwinds: Assumes 10.0% nominal GDP growth (First Advance Estimates FY 2025–26), tested by global slowdown, geopolitical conflicts, and trade disruptions.
  • Revenue Buoyancy: Large shortfalls in income tax & GST collections sharply reduced fiscal space → across-the-board expenditure cuts (capex & key social sectors). Heavy bet on Supply-Side Economics (roads, factories, railways), while Private Consumption (~60% of GDP) remains limited, especially in rural areas.
  • Implementation Lag: High-tech schemes (Bharat-VISTAAR, Biopharma SHAKTI) require advanced institutional capacity and face bureaucratic/execution bottlenecks; infra projects still face land acquisition challenges.
  • Job Creation Gap: Capex-heavy focus (semiconductors, biopharma) risks jobless or K-shaped growth due to limited labour absorption amid widening education–employment skill mismatch.
  • Green Transition & Resource Constraints: Shift to green tech increases demand for water, energy, critical minerals → import dependence & greenflation elevating costs for MSMEs & manufacturing.
  • Uncertain Capital Flows: Persistent FPI outflows and unclear FDI outlook raise external financing stability & investor confidence concerns.
  • External Aid Prioritisation Challenge: Foreign grants-in-aid make Bhutan the largest beneficiary, while no funds for Chabahar Port (Iran) raise regional connectivity and strategic outreach concerns.
Key Risks & Structural Bottlenecks
10 • Way Forward & Conclusion

🧭 Strengthening India's Economy Beyond 2026–27

  • Reviving Twin Engines of Demand: Both investment and consumption must fire together. Faster rollout of SHE Marts & Bharat-VISTAAR can raise rural incomes and boost demand (higher marginal propensity to consume).
  • Securing Strategic Autonomy in Critical Resources: Critical minerals = "new oil" of green economy. Combine Rare Earth Corridors with overseas mineral security partnerships + boost R&D spending beyond current low share of GDP (semiconductors & biopharma).
  • Skilling for New Sectors: Match AVGC & Semiconductor push with aggressive skill development (Skill India 2.0) to prevent a talent crunch.
  • Quality of Expenditure: Shift from "Outlays" to "Outcomes" — audit every Rupee (e.g., Mahatma Gandhi Gram Swaraj) for tangible asset creation & income generation, not just fund utilization.
  • Correcting "Inverted Duty Structures": In textiles & electronics, raw materials taxed higher than finished imports hurt domestic manufacturing → need sector-wise duty correction so "Made in India" is cheaper than imports at the tax level.
  • Conclusion: Strategically balances fiscal prudence with high-tech industrialization & inclusive welfare for "Viksit Bharat". Ultimate success hinges on effective implementation to bridge the "Jobless Growth" gap and revive private consumption to reach the "Last Mile".
