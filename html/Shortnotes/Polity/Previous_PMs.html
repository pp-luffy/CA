🇮🇳 Prime Ministers of India

⚡ Quick Revision Cheat Sheet

Nehru: 1st & Longest (17y), IITs, NAM Nanda: 1st Acting PM (twice) Shastri: "Jai Jawan, Jai Kisan", '65 War Indira: Only female, '71 War, Emergency Desai: 1st Non-Congress Charan Singh: Farmers' Champion, 0 Days in Parliament Rajiv: Youngest (40), IT Rev, Voting Age 18 VP Singh: Mandal Commission (OBCs) Chandra Shekhar: '91 BoP Crisis Narasimha Rao: 1st from South, '91 Economic Liberalization Vajpayee: 1st Non-Congress full term, Pokhran-II Deve Gowda: United Front Govt Gujral: Gujral Doctrine (Foreign Policy) Manmohan: 1st Sikh, RTI, MGNREGA, US Nuke Deal Modi: 1st born post-independence, GST, Digital India
1947–1964

1. Jawaharlal Nehru

  • Tenure: India’s first & longest-serving PM (nearly 17 years).
  • Notable Works: Established the Planning Commission (Five-Year Plans).
  • Championed the Non-Aligned Movement in foreign policy.
  • Founded premier institutions like the IITs.
Acting: 1964 & 1966

2. Gulzarilal Nanda

  • Tenure: India's first Acting Prime Minister.
  • History: Took charge briefly for 13 days each following the sudden deaths of Jawaharlal Nehru and Lal Bahadur Shastri to ensure a smooth transition.
1964–1966

3. Lal Bahadur Shastri

  • Notable Works: Led the country during the 1965 India-Pakistan War.
  • Coined the iconic morale-boosting slogan "Jai Jawan, Jai Kisan".
  • Previous Roles: Minister of Home Affairs & Minister of Railways.
1966–1977, 1980–1984

4. Indira Gandhi

  • Tenure: First and only female Prime Minister of India.
  • Notable Works: Nationalized major banks and led India to victory in the 1971 war (which resulted in the creation of Bangladesh).
  • Controversially imposed the 1975–1977 State of Emergency.
  • End of Term: Assassinated by her bodyguards while in office in 1984.
1977–1979

5. Morarji Desai

  • Tenure: First non-Congress Prime Minister (Janata Party).
  • Notable Works: Worked to reverse Emergency-era constitutional changes and normalized relations with Pakistan.
  • Previous Roles: Chief Minister of Bombay State & Union Finance Minister.
1979–1980

6. Charan Singh

  • Tenure: The only Prime Minister who never faced Parliament, as his government collapsed before a floor test.
  • Notable Works: Widely regarded as a champion for farmers and rural rights.
  • Previous Roles: Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh & Union Finance Minister.
1984–1989

7. Rajiv Gandhi

  • Tenure: Youngest Prime Minister, taking office at age 40.
  • Notable Works: Pioneered India's IT and telecom revolution.
  • Introduced the anti-defection law & lowered the voting age to 18.
  • End of Term: Assassinated by a suicide bomber in 1991 while campaigning.
1989–1990

8. V.P. Singh

  • Notable Works: Implemented the landmark Mandal Commission report.
  • Introduced job reservations for Other Backward Classes (OBCs).
  • Previous Roles: Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh & Union Finance Minister.
1990–1991

9. Chandra Shekhar

  • Tenure: Headed a minority Samajwadi Janata Party government backed by Congress.
  • History: Navigated the early stages of India's severe 1991 balance-of-payments economic crisis.
1991–1996

10. P.V. Narasimha Rao

  • Tenure: First Prime Minister from South India.
  • Notable Works: Primary architect of the historic 1991 economic liberalization.
  • Dismantled the License Raj and opened the Indian economy to global trade.
  • Previous Roles: Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh.
1996, 1998–2004

11. Atal Bihari Vajpayee

  • Tenure: First non-Congress PM to complete a full 5-year term. (Initial 1996 stint lasted only 13 days).
  • Notable Works: Conducted the 1998 Pokhran-II nuclear tests.
  • Launched the Golden Quadrilateral highway project and championed universal primary education.
1996–1997

12. H.D. Deve Gowda

  • Tenure: Headed the United Front, a diverse coalition government.
  • Previous Roles: Chief Minister of Karnataka.
1997–1998

13. I.K. Gujral

  • Notable Works: Formulated the "Gujral Doctrine".
  • A milestone 5-point framework for maintaining peaceful, non-reciprocal, and cooperative foreign relations with India's immediate neighbors.
2004–2014

14. Dr. Manmohan Singh

  • Tenure: First Sikh Prime Minister, serving two full terms.
  • Notable Works: Championed the Indo-US civil nuclear agreement, the Right to Information (RTI) Act, and the MGNREGA rural employment guarantee scheme.
  • Previous Roles: Union Finance Minister (driving force behind the 1991 reforms) and Governor of the Reserve Bank of India.
2014–Present

15. Narendra Modi

  • Tenure: First Prime Minister born after India's independence, currently serving his third consecutive term.
  • Notable Works: Rolled out the Goods and Services Tax (GST), Swachh Bharat Abhiyan (Clean India Mission), Make in India, and the Digital India infrastructure.
  • Previous Roles: Chief Minister of Gujarat.
