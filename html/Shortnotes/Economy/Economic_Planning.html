Foundation

Pre-Independence Plans & Basics

  • 1934 Visvesvaraya Plan: 1st blueprint (Planned Economy for India).
  • 1938 National Planning Comm: Set by S.C. Bose, chaired by J.L. Nehru.
  • 1944 Bombay Plan: By 8 Industrialists (Tata, Birla, etc.).
  • 1944 Gandhian Plan: S.N. Agarwal (Agri/Cottage).
  • 1945 People's Plan: M.N. Roy (Marxist).
  • 1950 Sarvodaya Plan: Jayaprakash Narayan.

Economic Planning: Concurrent List (Entry 20, 7th Schedule).
Mixed Economy Model: Adopted via 1948 Industrial Policy Resolution.
Planning Commission (Mar 15, 1950): Non-constitutional, advisory body (PM = Chairman).
NDC (Aug 6, 1952): Final approval body, included all CMs.

FYP 1

1st Five-Year Plan

1951–1956

Govt: Jawaharlal Nehru (INC)

Model: Harrod-Domar

Focus: Agriculture, price stability, irrigation & power.

  • Massive projects: Hirakud Dam (Odisha), Bhakra Nangal, Damodar Valley.
  • 5 IITs & UGC (1953) established.
  • Community Development Programme (1952).

Result: Highly successful (Target 2.1% → Achieved 3.6%).

FYP 2

2nd Five-Year Plan

1956–1961

Govt: Jawaharlal Nehru (INC)

Model: P.C. Mahalanobis (Nehru-Mahalanobis)

Focus: Rapid Industrialization (Heavy & Basic Industries).

  • Steel plants: Rourkela (Odisha – West Germany), Bhilai (USSR), Durgapur (UK).
  • Industrial Policy Resolution 1956 ("Socialistic Pattern of Society").
  • Atomic Energy Commission (1957).

Result: Moderate success (Target 4.5% → Achieved 4.27%; Forex crisis).

FYP 3

3rd Five-Year Plan

1961–1966

Govt: J.L. Nehru (till '64) → L.B. Shastri ('64–'66) → Indira Gandhi (INC)

Model: Gadgil Yojana (John Sandy & Sukhamoy Chakravarty)

Focus: Self-reliant & self-generating economy, Agriculture.

  • FCI and Agricultural Prices Commission (now CACP) established (1965).
  • IDBI & UTI set up (1964).

Result: Massive Failure (Target 5.6% → Achieved 2.8%) due to Sino-Indian War (62), Indo-Pak War (65) & severe drought.

Break

Plan Holiday (3 Annual Plans)

1966–1969

Govt: Indira Gandhi (INC)

Context: Triggered by 3rd Plan failure, war expenses & lack of resources.

  • Devaluation of Rupee (1966) to boost exports.
  • Implementation of Green Revolution (HYV seeds; M.S. Swaminathan).
FYP 4

4th Five-Year Plan

1969–1974

Govt: Indira Gandhi (INC)

Model: Gadgil Formula (Central assistance to states)

Focus: Growth with stability & progressive self-reliance.

  • Nationalization of 14 Banks (July 1969).
  • 1971 Indo-Pak War (Bangladesh creation).
  • Smiling Buddha (1974): 1st Nuclear test (Pokhran).
  • Family Planning & Drought Prone Area Programme (DPAP).

Result: Failed targets (Target 5.7% → Achieved 3.3%; 1973 Oil Crisis).

FYP 5 & Break

5th Plan & Rolling Plan

1974–1978 & 1978–1980

5th Plan: Indira Gandhi (INC) | Rolling Plan: Morarji Desai (Janata Party)

5th Plan Focus: Garibi Hatao (Poverty Eradication) & Self-reliance. Drafted by D.P. Dhar. Minimum Needs Programme (MNP), 20-Point Programme (1975).

Result: Successful (4.8% achieved), but terminated 1 year early (1978) by Janata Govt.

Rolling Plan (1978–80): Concept by Gunnar Myrdal; adopted by Morarji Desai. Annual revision of targets/allocations. Discarded by Indira Gandhi in 1980.

FYP 6

6th Five-Year Plan

1980–1985

Govt: Indira Gandhi ('80–'84) → Rajiv Gandhi ('84–'85) (INC)

Focus: Poverty eradication, tech self-reliance, economic liberalization.

  • End of strict Nehruvian socialism; price controls eased.
  • 6 more banks nationalized (1980).
  • NABARD (1982) (Sivaraman Committee) & EXIM Bank (1982).
  • IRDP, NREP, TRYSEM launched.

Result: Successful (Target 5.2% → Achieved 5.7%).

FYP 7 & Crisis

7th Plan & Annual Plans

1985–1990 & 1990–1992

7th Plan: Rajiv Gandhi (INC) → V.P. Singh | Annual Plans: V.P. Singh → Chandra Shekhar → P.V. Narasimha Rao

7th Plan: "Food, Work & Productivity". Pvt sector prioritized. Jawahar Rozgar Yojana (1989). SEBI established (1988, non-statutory). Broke 'Hindu Rate of Growth' (achieved 6.0%).

Annual Plans (90–92): Delayed 8th Plan due to political instability & BoP/Forex crisis.

  • 1991 LPG Reforms: Liberalisation, Privatisation, Globalisation (PM P.V. Narasimha Rao & FM Manmohan Singh).
FYP 8 & 9

8th & 9th Plans

1992–1997 & 1997–2002

8th: P.V. Narasimha Rao (INC) → Deve Gowda (UF)

8th Plan (Rao–Manmohan Model): Indicative Planning; HRD focus. Joined WTO (Jan 1, 1995). 73rd/74th Amendments. PMRY (1993). Highly successful (6.8%).

9th: I.K. Gujral (UF) → Atal Bihari Vajpayee (NDA)

9th Plan: "Growth with Social Justice & Equality". Hit by Asian Financial Crisis (1997), Pokhran-II (1998), Kargil War (1999). Failed targets (5.4%).

FYP 10 & 11

10th & 11th Plans

2002–2007 & 2007–2012

10th: A.B. Vajpayee (NDA, '02–'04) → Manmohan Singh (UPA-I, '04–'07)

10th Plan: Target to double per capita income in 10 yrs. Monitorable state-wise targets. High growth (7.6%).

11th: Dr. Manmohan Singh (UPA-I & UPA-II)

11th Plan (C. Rangarajan): "Faster and More Inclusive Growth".

  • First FYP to explicitly include Financial Inclusion.
  • RTE Act enacted (2009); Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana (2007).
  • Achieved 8% growth (highest ever actual FYP rate).
FYP 12

12th Five-Year Plan

2012–2017

Govt: Dr. Manmohan Singh (UPA-II, '12–'14) → Narendra Modi (NDA, '14–'17)

Theme: "Faster, More Inclusive and Sustainable Growth".

  • 8% GDP growth target; 4% Agriculture growth; 10% Manufacturing growth.
  • Reduce IMR to 25, MMR to 1 per 1,000 live births; TFR to 2.1.
  • 10% reduction in headcount poverty; electricity to all villages.

Note: The Final Five-Year Plan (replaced by NITI Aayog's 3-tier framework in 2017).

Modern Era

NITI Aayog

Established: Jan 1, 2015

Govt: Narendra Modi (NDA)

Full Form: National Institution for Transforming India.

  • Status: Extra-constitutional & Non-statutory (Cabinet Resolution).
  • Role: Premier Policy "Think Tank" & Poverty Assessment body.
  • Philosophy: Cooperative & Competitive Federalism.
  • Approach: Bottom-Up (village to centre).

NO fund allocation power (shifted to Ministry of Finance). See complete deep-dive below ↓

🏛️ NITI Aayog: Complete Exam Deep-Dive

1. Structure & Composition (2026)

  • Chairperson: Prime Minister (Ex-officio) — Shri Narendra Modi.
  • Vice-Chairperson: Appointed by PM (Cabinet Minister rank) — Shri Ashok Kumar Lahiri (Apr 2026).
    Past VCs: Arvind Panagariya (1st) → Rajiv Kumar → Suman Bery.
  • CEO: Appointed by PM (Secretary rank, fixed tenure) — Shri Anurag Jain.
    1st CEO: Sindhushree Khullar.
  • Governing Council: All State CMs + CMs of UTs with Legislatures (Delhi, Puducherry, J&K) + Lt. Governors of other UTs.
  • Regional Councils: Formed for specific regional issues; chaired by PM or nominee.
  • Full-Time Members (2026): Rajiv Gauba, Prof. K.V. Raju, Prof. Gobardhan Das, Dr. M. Srinivas, Prof. Abhay Karandikar, Dr. R. Balasubramaniam, Dr. Joram Aniya (1st woman from NE India).
  • Ex-Officio Members: Max 4 Union Cabinet Ministers nominated by PM.
  • Part-Time Members: Max 2 (from universities/research bodies, rotational).
  • Special Invitees: Domain experts nominated by PM.

2. Hubs, Planning Docs & 7 Pillars

Two Specialized Hubs:

  • Team India Hub: Leads State–Centre engagement & cooperative federalism.
  • Knowledge & Innovation Hub: Builds NITI Aayog's think-tank & research capacity.

3-Tier Planning Framework (Replaced FYPs):

  • 3-Year Action Agenda (Short-term tactical goals).
  • 7-Year Strategy (Medium-term sectoral roadmap).
  • 15-Year Vision Document (Long-term; now aligned with Viksit Bharat @2047).

7 Pillars of Effective Governance:

1. Pro-People 2. Pro-Activity 3. Participation 4. Empowering 5. Inclusion of All 6. Equality 7. Transparency

3. Flagship Initiatives & Major Indices

Key Initiatives & Attached Bodies:

  • Aspirational Districts (2018) & Aspirational Blocks (2023): Rapid transformation of India's most underdeveloped districts/blocks (Delta ranking).
  • Atal Innovation Mission (AIM): Atal Tinkering Labs (schools) & Atal Incubation Centres.
  • DMEO: Development Monitoring & Evaluation Office (tracks govt schemes).
  • Women Entrepreneurship Platform (WEP) & State Support Mission.
  • SATH (Sustainable Action for Transforming Human Capital — Education & Health).
  • National Monetisation Pipeline (NMP), Shoonya (Zero-pollution EVs), e-Amrit, LiFE (Lifestyle for Environment).

High-Yield Indices Published by NITI Aayog:

  • SDG India Index (Nodal agency for UN SDGs in India)
  • National Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI)
  • Composite Water Management Index (CWMI)
  • India Innovation Index | State Health Index | Export Preparedness Index | School Education Quality Index (SEQI) | Fiscal Health Index.
