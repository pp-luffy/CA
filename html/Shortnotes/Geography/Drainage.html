Drainage System of India

⚡ Quick Revision Sheet ⚡

Himalayan Rivers Perennial (snow & rain). V-shaped valleys (youth), ox-bow lakes, deltas (plains). Indus, Ganga, Brahmaputra.
Peninsular Rivers Non-perennial. Fixed course, no meanders. Older than Himalayan. Western Ghats = water divide.
West Flowing Narmada (largest) & Tapi. Flow in rift valleys (Vindhya/Satpura). Amarkantak & Betul origins.
East Flowing Mahanadi, Godavari (Dakshin Ganga), Krishna, Kaveri. Drain into Bay of Bengal.
Patterns Dendritic (Tree), Trellis (Folds/90°), Parallel (Steep slope), Rectangular (Faults/Joints), Radial (Dome), Centripetal (Depression).
Tectonics Folding = Flexible crust (Mountains, Anticline/Syncline). Faulting = Brittle crust (Earthquakes).
💦 Channels & Networks

Drainage & Drainage Pattern

Drainage: Flow of water through well-defined channels. Network of channels = drainage system.

Drainage Pattern: System of flow of surface water mainly through rivers and basins.

  • Depends on: slope of land, geological structure, volume of water, and velocity of water.
Concepts
🌳 Tree Roots Branching

Dendritic Pattern

  • Most common form; resembles branching tree roots.
  • Develops where channel follows terrain slope.
  • Rock beneath has no particular structure; eroded equally in all directions.
  • Tributaries join at acute angles (<90°).
  • Ex: Northern plains rivers (Indus, Ganga, Brahmaputra).
Geo-Pattern
⏸️ Elongated Slopes

Parallel Pattern

  • Develops in regions of parallel, elongated landforms with pronounced surface slope.
  • Tributary streams stretch out in parallel-like fashion following the slope.
  • Ex: Rivers originating in Western Ghats (Godavari, Kaveri, Krishna, Tungabhadra).
Geo-Pattern
📐 Folded Topography (90°)

Trellis Pattern

  • Develops in folded topography where hard & soft rocks exist parallel.
  • Down-turned folds (synclines) form valleys for main channel.
  • Primary tributaries flow parallel; secondary join them at right angles.
  • Ex: Upper part of Himalayan region (Indus, Ganga, Brahmaputra).
Geo-Pattern
🪨 Faulted & Jointed Terrain

Rectangular Pattern

  • Found in regions that have undergone faulting.
  • Develops on strongly joined rocky terrain.
  • Streams follow paths of least resistance (weakest exposed rock).
  • Tributary streams make sharp bends, entering main stream at high angles.
  • Ex: Vindhya mountain range streams (Chambal, Betwa, Ken).
Geo-Pattern
⚠️ Compression Forces

Folding and Faulting

Earth's crust pushed together via compression.

  • Folding: Crust bends away from flat surface. Flexible material. Forms mountains.
    • Bend upward = Anticline
    • Bend downward = Syncline
  • Faulting: Crust completely breaks & slides. Brittle material. Causes earthquakes.
Tectonics
🌋 Outward from Center

Radial Pattern

  • Develops around a central elevated point.
  • Common to conically shaped features like volcanoes or hills.
  • Rivers originate from the hill and flow in all directions.
  • Ex: Rivers from Amarkantak range (Narmada and Son - tributary of Ganga).
Geo-Pattern
🎯 Inward to Depression

Centripetal Pattern

  • Opposite of radial; streams flow toward a central depression.
  • During wet periods, streams feed ephemeral lakes.
  • Lakes evaporate in dry periods.
  • Salt dissolved in water precipitates, creating salt flats.
  • Ex: Loktak lake in Manipur.
Geo-Pattern
🏔️ Melting Snow & Rain

Himalayan Drainage System

  • Fed by melting snow and precipitation; hence perennial.
  • Mountain course: Form V-shaped valleys, rapids, and waterfalls.
  • Plains course (Depositional): Form flat valleys, ox-bow lakes, flood plains, braided channels, and deltas near river mouth.
Himalayan

Indus River System

  • One of the largest basins worldwide. Westernmost Himalayan river, aka Sindhu.
  • Origin: Glacier near Bokhar Chu (Kailash range, Tibet). Known as 'Singi Khamban' (Lion’s mouth).
  • Flows in India only through Leh district (Ladakh UT).
  • Tributaries: Sutlej, Ravi, Jhelum, Chenab (largest tributary), Beas.
Himalayan

Ganga River System

  • Origin: Gangotri glacier near Gaumukh (3,900m, UK) as Bhagirathi.
  • Devprayag: Bhagirathi meets Alaknanda = Ganga. Enters plains at Haridwar.
  • States: UK, UP, Bihar, WB. Into Bay of Bengal (Sagar Island).
  • Right Bank Tribs: Son, Yamuna (westernmost, longest, from Yamunotri).
  • Left Bank Tribs: Ramganga, Gomati, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi, Mahananda.
Himalayan

Brahmaputra River System

  • Origin: Chemayungdung glacier (Kailash range) near Mansarovar lake.
  • Tibet: Tsangpo ('the purifier').
  • Emerges as Siang/Dihang; enters India west of Sadiya (Arunachal Pradesh).
  • Left Bank Tribs: Dibang/Sikang, Lohit, Burhi Dihing, Dhansari.
  • Right Bank Tribs: Subansiri, Kameng, Manas, Sankosh.
  • Merges with Padma in BD, falls in Bay of Bengal.
Himalayan

Peninsular Drainage System

  • Characterised by fixed course, absence of meanders, and nonperennial flow.
  • Older than the Himalayan drainage system.
  • Water Divide: Western Ghats running close to west coast.
  • Most major rivers (Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri) flow West to East.
  • Exceptions: Narmada and Tapi (Westward).
Peninsular
⬅️ West Flowing (Rift)

Narmada

  • Largest west flowing river of peninsular region.
  • Flows through a rift valley between Vindhya (north) and Satpura Range (south).
  • Origin: Maikala range near Amarkantak (MP).
  • Tribs: Hiran, Orsang, Barna, Kolar.
  • Basin: MP, Maha, Gujarat. Hosts Sardar Sarovar Project.
Peninsular
⬅️ West Flowing (Parallel)

Tapi

  • Another important westward flowing river.
  • Origin: Betul district of Madhya Pradesh in Satpura ranges.
  • Flows in a rift valley parallel to the Narmada but is much shorter in length.
  • Basin covers parts of Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, and Maharashtra.
Peninsular
➡️ East to Bay of Bengal

Mahanadi

  • Origin: Raipur district (Chhattisgarh), runs through Odisha to BoB.
  • Basin: 53% in MP & CG, 47% in Odisha.
  • Tribs: Seonath, Hasdeo, Mand, Ib, Jonking, Tel.
  • Bounded by Central India hills (N), Eastern Ghats (S, E), Maikala range (W).
Peninsular
👑 "Dakshin Ganga"

Godavari

  • Largest Peninsular river system.
  • Origin: Nasik district of Maharashtra. Discharges to BoB.
  • Tributaries run through: Maha, MP, CG, Odisha, AP.
  • Principal Tribs: Penganga, Indravati, Pranhita, Manjra.
Peninsular
🌊 2nd Largest East-Flowing

Krishna

  • Second largest east flowing Peninsular river.
  • Origin: Near Mahabaleshwar in Sahyadri.
  • Major Tribs: Koyna, Tungbhadra, Bhima.
  • Flows through: Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana, and Andhra Pradesh before BoB.
Peninsular
🛕 Sacred Southern River

Kaveri

  • Origin: Brahmagiri hills of Kodagu district (Karnataka).
  • Sacred river of southern India.
  • Tribs: Arkavathi, Hemavathi, Bhavani, Kabini, Amravati.
  • Flows southeasterly through Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu.
  • Drains into Bay of Bengal through Pondicherry.
