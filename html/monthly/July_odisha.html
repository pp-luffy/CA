Ancy Sojan Breaks 22-Year-Old National Long Jump Record
  • Event: National Inter-State Senior Athletics Championships 2026 at Kalinga Stadium, Bhubaneswar (June 2026).
  • Record: 25-year-old Ancy recorded a jump of 6.88 m, breaking Anju Bobby George's 22-year record of 6.83 m (standing since 2004 Athens Olympics).
  • Adversity: Competed while battling Polycystic Ovarian Disease (PCOD) & hormonal imbalances.
  • Qualification: Secured spot for 2026 Asian Games (Aichi-Nagoya, Japan).
  • Aim: Diamond League & 2027 World Athletics Championships (Beijing).

Skilling
Odisha ITI Cluster Approved Under PM-SETU Scheme
  • Approval: 4th National Steering Committee approved the SIP for an Odisha ITI cluster.
  • Investment & Anchor: ₹240.21 crore proposed. Jindal Naveen Avsar Limited selected as Anchor Industry Partner.
  • Structure: Govt ITI Barbil acts as the Hub. Govt ITI Anandapur, Koira, Karanjia, and Barkote will function as Spoke ITIs.
PM-SETU Details
  • Full Form: Pradhan Mantri Skilling and Employability Transformation through Upgraded ITIs.
  • Model: Central scheme, Hub-and-Spoke. Nationwide rollout across 200 Hub ITIs supporting 800 Spoke ITIs.
  • Outlay: ₹60,000 crore over 5 years. Nodal: MSDE.
Agriculture
Union Minister Reviews Fisheries Development
  • Meeting: Chaired by Union Minister Rajiv Ranjan Singh in Bhubaneswar (July 2026).
  • Stats (2025-26): Odisha recorded 12.70 lakh MT fish production, supporting over 16 lakh fishers, generating ₹5,429 crore in seafood exports.
  • PMMSY Support: Projects worth over ₹1,301 crore approved for Odisha.
  • Focus: Diversification into high-value species: scampi, seabass, pompano, and tilapia. Collaborations with FFPOs.
PMMSY
  • Launched: Sep 10, 2020 (Period: 2020-21 to 2024-25).
  • Nodal Agency: National Fisheries Development Board.
Environment
VP Launches LoA for Sustainable High-Seas Fisheries
  • Launch: By Vice President C.P. Radhakrishnan in Bhubaneswar (July 2026). Launched the LoA framework and Odisha Deep Sea Fishing Mission Document.
  • Legal Framework: Mandatory under the Guidelines for Sustainable Harnessing of Fisheries in the High Seas (2025).
  • Tech: Integrated with ReALCraft Fishing Vessel Registration Portal.
  • Compliance: Strict adherence to RFMOs rules (catch limits, bycatch, Fish Aggregating Device (FAD) management).
  • Priority: Fisheries Cooperatives and FFPOs.
Infrastructure
Cabinet Approves Two Railway Multitracking Projects
  • Approval: CCEA chaired by PM Modi approved ₹3,907 crore investment aligned with PM Gati Shakti.
  • Projects: Paradeep-Haridaspur (Doubling) & Rajkharsawan-Dangoaposi (Fourth Line, Odisha-Jharkhand).
  • Timeline & Cargo: Target 2030-31. Transporting coal, iron ore, dolomite, limestone, gypsum.
  • Tourism Boost: Enhances connectivity to Lalitgiri Buddhist Complex, Shree Baladevjew Temple, Meghahatuburu Hills.
  • Eco-Benefit: Saves 6 crore litres of oil, cuts 29 crore kg of CO2 (= planting 1 crore trees).
Culture & Religion
World-Famous Lord Jagannath Rath Yatra
  • Event: Annual festival in Puri. Deities: Lord Jagannath, Lord Balabhadra, Devi Subhadra.
  • Journey: Travel nearly 3 km from Shree Jagannath Temple to Shree Gundicha Temple (Mausi Bari). Deities stay for 9 days.
  • Return: Known as Bahuda Yatra.
Shree Jagannath Temple Factfile
  • Known as the "White Pagoda". One of the Char Dham sites (Badrinath, Dwarka, Puri, Rameswaram).
  • Architecture: Masterpiece of Kalinga architecture with 4 gates.
  • Structures: Vimana (sanctum), Jagamohana (assembly), Nata Mandira (festival), Bhoga Mandapa (offering).
Digital Govt
High Court Digitises 42.96 Lakh Legacy Case Records
  • Achievement: Orissa High Court digitised 42.96 lakh legacy court records via e-Courts Project.
  • Execution: By Record Room Digitisation Centres (RRDCs) active across all 30 judicial districts.
  • Format: Bulky physical files and historical land archives transformed to encrypted PDF/A format.
  • Integration: Integrated with court workflows and the e-Filing 3.0 portal.
  • Impact: Enabled Virtual High Court Benches (litigants needn't travel to Cuttack) & real-time updates on National Judicial Data Grid (NJDG).
Defence Tech
DRDO Maiden Flight-Test of 'Kusha' Air Defence Missile
  • Test: Successful maiden flight-test of 'Kusha' Long-Range Surface-to-Air Missile (LRSAM) by DRDO (July 2026).
  • Location: APJ Abdul Kalam Island off the Odisha coast.
  • Capability: Intercepted a high-speed, high-altitude simulated target. Can neutralize fighter jets, UAVs, missiles, and large aircraft.
DRDO Basics
  • Established: 1958 (Foundation Day: Jan 1).
  • Principle: "Balasya Mulam Vigyanam" (Strength lies in science).
