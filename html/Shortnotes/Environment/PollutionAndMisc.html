💨 Air Pollution, GHGs, Acid Rain & Water Indicators

Kyoto Basket of Greenhouse Gases (7)

CO₂ CH₄ N₂O HFCs PFCs SF₆ NF₃

  • Original 6 (Kyoto 1997): CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, HFCs, PFCs, SF₆.
  • 7th Gas (Doha 2012): Nitrogen Trifluoride (NF₃) added in 2nd commitment period.
  • GWP (Global Warming Potential) Order: SF₆ (highest, ~23,500×) > PFCs > HFCs > N₂O > CH₄ > CO₂ (baseline = 1).

National Air Quality Index (AQI) & Acid Rain

  • 8 AQI Pollutants: PM₁₀, PM₂.₅, NO₂, SO₂, CO, O₃ (Ozone), NH₃ (Ammonia), Pb (Lead).
  • Classic Exam Trap: CO₂ (Carbon Dioxide) is NOT part of AQI!
  • Acid Rain: Caused by SO₂ and NOₓ reacting with water vapour to form Sulphuric Acid (H₂SO₄) and Nitric Acid (HNO₃).
  • pH Threshold: Rainwater with pH < 5.6 is Acid Rain (normal rain is slightly acidic ~5.6 due to dissolved CO₂).

Water Indicators & Bio-Processes

  • BOD (Biochemical Oxygen Demand): Oxygen required by aerobic bacteria to decompose organic waste. High BOD = High organic pollution = Low Dissolved Oxygen (DO).
  • COD (Chemical Oxygen Demand): Measures both biodegradable & non-biodegradable matter; COD is always ≥ BOD.
  • Eutrophication: Excess nutrients (Nitrates & Phosphates from fertilisers/sewage) trigger Algal Blooms → depletes DO → kills aquatic life.
  • Bioaccumulation vs. Biomagnification:
    • Bioaccumulation: Pollutant build-up inside a single organism over its lifetime.
    • Biomagnification: Pollutant concentration increases up trophic levels (e.g., DDT, Mercury).
🩺 Pollution-Induced Diseases & Odisha Exam Edge

5 High-Yield Pollution Diseases

  • Minamata Disease: Caused by Mercury (Hg) poisoning (neurological damage; first seen in Japan).
  • Itai-Itai Disease: Caused by Cadmium (Cd) poisoning (severe bone/joint pain & kidney damage).
  • Blue Baby Syndrome (Methemoglobinemia): Caused by excess Nitrates (NO₃⁻) in drinking water (reduces oxygen-carrying capacity of infant blood).
  • Blackfoot Disease: Caused by chronic Arsenic (As) groundwater contamination (gangrene of feet).
  • Fluorosis: Caused by excess Fluoride (F⁻) in water (dental mottling & skeletal deformity / knock-knee).

🌿 Odisha-Specific Exam Edge (OPSC ASO)

6 Ramsar Wetland Sites in Odisha:

  • 1. Chilika Lake: Asia's largest brackish water lagoon & India's 1st Ramsar Site (1981) (along with Keoladeo).
  • 2. Bhitarkanika Mangroves: Kendrapara; famous for Saltwater Crocodiles & Olive Ridley turtles (Gahirmatha).
  • 3. Satkosia Gorge: Angul / Nayagarh; Mahanadi river gorge & Gharials.
  • 4. Tampara Lake: Ganjam district (freshwater lake).
  • 5. Hirakud Reservoir: Sambalpur (longest earthen dam in the world).
  • 6. Ansupa Lake: Cuttack district (Odisha's largest freshwater horseshoe-shaped oxbow lake).

2 National Parks in Odisha: Bhitarkanika NP & Simlipal NP (Mayurbhanj — also a Biosphere Reserve & Tiger Reserve).
