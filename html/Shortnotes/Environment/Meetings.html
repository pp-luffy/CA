⏳ Timeline 1: Major UN Environmental Summits & Rio Milestones
Stockholm Conference (UNCHE) 1972 • Sweden
  • First major UN conference on the human environment.
  • Led to creation of UNEP (HQ: Nairobi) and World Environment Day (June 5).
Brundtland Commission Report 1987
  • Report titled "Our Common Future"; formally coined and defined Sustainable Development.
Rio Earth Summit (UNCED) 1992 • Rio de Janeiro
  • Produced the 3 Rio Conventions: UNFCCC (1992/1994), CBD (1992/1993), and UNCCD (1994/1996 — only legally binding framework to combat desertification & drought).
  • Adopted Rio Declaration, Forest Principles, and Agenda 21 (non-binding action plan for sustainable development in the 21st century).
Rio+10 (WSSD — World Summit on Sustainable Development) 2002 • Johannesburg
  • Focused on full implementation of Agenda 21 and poverty eradication.
Rio+20 (UNCSD) 2012 • Rio de Janeiro
  • Outcome document: "The Future We Want".
  • Initiated the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and PAGE (Partnership for Action on Green Economy).
UN Climate Action Summit 2019 • New York
  • Convened by UNSG António Guterres to boost climate ambition.
  • India launched CDRI (Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure) and LeadIT (Leadership Group for Industry Transition, with Sweden).
📅 Timeline 2: Landmark UNFCCC COPs (COP 1 to COP 32)
COP 1 (Berlin) & COP 3 (Kyoto) 1995 & 1997
  • COP 1 (1995, Berlin): First UNFCCC COP; adopted the Berlin Mandate.
  • COP 3 (1997, Kyoto): Adopted the Kyoto Protocol (in force 2005) — legally binding GHG cuts for developed (Annex I) countries; introduced 3 Market Mechanisms: Clean Development Mechanism (CDM), Joint Implementation (JI), and Emissions Trading.
COP 8 (New Delhi) & COP 13 (Bali) 2002 & 2007
  • COP 8 (2002, New Delhi): Only UNFCCC COP hosted by India; adopted Delhi Ministerial Declaration.
  • COP 13 (2007, Bali): Adopted Bali Action Plan; formally introduced REDD+.
COP 16 (Cancun), COP 18 (Doha) & COP 19 (Warsaw) 2010 – 2013
  • COP 16 (2010, Cancun): Cancun Agreements — formally established the Green Climate Fund (GCF) and Technology Mechanism.
  • COP 18 (2012, Doha): Doha Amendment — established the 2nd commitment period (2013–2020) of the Kyoto Protocol.
  • COP 19 (2013, Warsaw): Created the Warsaw International Mechanism (WIM) — first formal UNFCCC mechanism for Loss and Damage.
COP 21 (Paris) & COP 24 (Katowice) 2015 & 2018
  • COP 21 (2015, Paris): Adopted the Paris Agreement (in force 2016) — limit global warming well below 2°C (pursue 1.5°C), uses NDCs & 5-year Global Stocktake. India & France launched the International Solar Alliance (ISA).
  • COP 24 (2018, Katowice, Poland): Finalised the Katowice Rulebook (operational rules to implement Paris Agreement).
COP 26 (Glasgow) & COP 27 (Sharm el-Sheikh) 2021 & 2022
  • COP 26 (2021, Glasgow): Glasgow Climate Pact (first explicit mention of "phasing down" unabated coal). India announced Panchamrit (Net Zero by 2070, 500 GW non-fossil by 2030) and Mission LiFE. Also launched Global Methane Pledge.
  • COP 27 (2022, Sharm el-Sheikh, Egypt): Historic agreement to establish the Loss and Damage Fund for vulnerable developing countries.
COP 28 (Dubai), COP 29 (Baku) & COP 30 (Belém) 2023 – 2025
  • COP 28 (2023, Dubai): First Global Stocktake; operationalised Loss & Damage Fund; historic call for "transitioning away from fossil fuels"; target to triple renewable energy & double energy efficiency by 2030.
  • COP 29 (2024, Baku): Adopted NCQG (New Collective Quantified Goal): $300 billion/year by 2035 for developing nations; finalised Article 6 carbon market rules.
  • COP 30 (2025, Belém, Brazil): Adopted the 59 Belém Adaptation Indicators and decided to develop a Just Transition Mechanism.
★ COP 31 (Antalya, Türkiye) & Upcoming COP 32 Nov 2026 & Nov 2027
  • COP 31 (Nov 2026, Antalya, Türkiye): Hosted by Türkiye (negotiations presided over by Australia). Failed to adopt the overarching "Antalya Pact" due to deadlock over fossil fuel phase-out and adaptation finance.
  • Separate Decisions Adopted at COP 31: UAE Just Transition Work Programme (extended 3 years), Adaptation Fund (exclusively serving Paris Agreement), and GGA; voluntary "35-by-35" Electrification Target (35% final energy from electricity by 2035) and Wildlife for Climate Declaration.
  • COP 32 (Upcoming — Nov 2027, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia): Unresolved COP 31 items automatically carry over under Rule 16.
🏦 Climate Bodies, Forest Carbon Funds & Key Mechanisms

IPCC & Climate Science

  • IPCC (1988): Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change — jointly created by WMO + UNEP.
  • Assesses global climate science through Assessment Reports (AR6, etc.).
  • Exam Trap: IPCC does NOT conduct its own original research or monitor climate data directly.

World Bank Forest & Carbon Funds

  • FCPF (Forest Carbon Partnership Facility): World Bank global partnership helping developing countries reduce emissions from deforestation/degradation (REDD+ readiness & carbon funds).
  • BioCarbon Fund ISFL: World Bank's Initiative for Sustainable Forest Landscapes — reduces GHG emissions from the land sector (agriculture, forestry, land use).
  • UN-REDD / REDD+ (2008): Jointly run by FAO, UNDP, UNEP for Reducing Emissions from Deforestation and Forest Degradation.

Global Climate Finance: GEF vs. GCF

  • GEF (Global Environment Facility): World Bank acts as Trustee; serves as the financial mechanism for 5 major conventions: CBD, UNFCCC, UNCCD, Stockholm, and Minamata.
  • GCF (Green Climate Fund): Established under UNFCCC (COP 16, Cancun); HQ: Songdo, South Korea. World's largest dedicated climate fund for developing countries' mitigation & adaptation.

Other High-Yield Climate Initiatives

  • Global Methane Pledge (2021, COP 26): Cut global methane emissions by 30% by 2030 (led by US & EU). India has NOT signed it (due to agriculture/livestock concerns).
  • EU CBAM (2023 Transitional): Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism — EU's carbon tariff on carbon-intensive imports (steel, cement, aluminium, etc.).
  • Gothenburg Protocol (1999): Under CLRTAP; abates acidification, eutrophication, and ground-level ozone.
