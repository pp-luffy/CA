🧭 1. Critical Geographic Lines

  • Tropic of Cancer (23.5° N): Passes through 8 States (W to E):
    • Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, Mizoram.
    • Mahi River cuts the line twice.
    • Ranchi (JH) is the closest capital city.
  • Indian Standard Time (82.5° E): Passes through 5 States (N to S):
    • UP, MP, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh.
    • Mirzapur (UP) is the reference point.
    • Intersection: IST & Tropic of Cancer meet in Koriya dist, Chhattisgarh.

🗺️ 3. State Boundaries (Domestic)

  • Max Bordering State: Uttar Pradesh (Touches 8 States + Delhi UT). States: UK, HP, HR, RJ, MP, CG, BR, JH.
  • Min Bordering States:
    • Sikkim: Touches only West Bengal.
    • Meghalaya: Touches only Assam.
  • Landlocked States (5): MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Telangana, Haryana. (Note: Delhi & Chandigarh are UTs).
  • Niche District Fact: Sonbhadra (UP) is the ONLY district touching 4 states (MP, CG, JH, BR).
  • Niche State Facts:
    • Tripura: Bordered by Bangladesh on 3 sides (N, S, W).
    • Assam: Borders ALL other 6 "Seven Sister" states + West Bengal.

🌊 4. Coastline Profile

  • Total Coastline: 7,516.6 km (Mainland: 5,422.6 km + Islands: 2,094 km).
  • Coastal entities: 9 States and 4 UTs.

    • UT: Andaman & Nicobar (~1,962 km).
  • Minimum: State: Goa (~101 km).

⛰️ 8A. Passes (NW Himalayas)

  • Mintaka (Karakoram): Hunza-Xinjiang. Tri-junction (IN, CN, AF).
  • Karakoram (Ladakh): Ladakh-Xinjiang. Highest pass.
  • Khardung La (Ladakh): Leh-Shyok/Nubra. Gateway to Siachen (BRO HIMANK).
  • Chang La (Ladakh): Leh-Pangong Tso.
  • Zoji La (Zanskar): Srinagar-Kargil/Leh. NH 1.
  • Pensi La (Zanskar): Suru-Zanskar. Overlooks Drang Drung Glacier.
  • Bara Lacha La (Zanskar, HP): Lahaul-Leh. Suraj Tal (Bhaga river source).
  • Shipki La (Zanskar, HP): HP-Tibet. Sutlej River enters India.
  • Burzil (Great Him): Srinagar-Gilgit (Closed 1947).
  • Pir Panjal (J&K): Kashmir-Rajouri. Mughal Road.
  • Banihal (J&K): Jammu-Srinagar. NH 44 (Jawahar / Pir Panjal tunnels).
  • Rohtang (Pir Panjal, HP): Kullu-Lahaul/Spiti. Atal Tunnel.

🏔️ 8B. Passes (UK, SK, NE)

  • Lipulekh (Kumaon, UK): UK-Tibet. Tri-junction (IN/CN/NP); Kailash Yatra.
  • Nathu La (Sikkim): Sikkim-Chumbi Valley. Reopened 2006.
  • Jelep La (Sikkim): Sikkim-Lhasa. Teesta river nearby.
  • Bum La (Arunachal): Tawang-Tibet. 14th Dalai Lama entered 1959.
  • Bomdi La (Arunachal): Tawang-Tezpur. Gateway to Tawang.
  • Dihang (Arunachal): AR-Mandalay (Myanmar).
  • Diphu (Arunachal): Tri-junction (IN, CN, MY) on McMahon Line.

🌴 8C. Peninsular Passes (W. Ghats)

  • Thal Ghat (MH): Mumbai-Nashik. Mumbai-Kolkata rail/highway.
  • Bhor Ghat (MH): Mumbai-Pune. Oldest route to Deccan.
  • Pal Ghat (KL): Palakkad (KL)-Coimbatore (TN). Gap between Nilgiri (N) and Anaimalai Hills (S).
  • Shencottah (KL/TN): Kollam (KL)-Madurai (TN). Separates Cardamom Hills.
