T - Territories

Sch 1
  • Content: Names of States and Union Territories and their territorial jurisdiction.
  • Relevant Articles: Articles 1 and 4.

E - Emoluments

Sch 2
  • Content: Salaries, allowances, privileges, and pensions.
  • Officeholders: President, Governors.
  • Speaker/Deputy Speaker (Lok Sabha & State Assemblies).
  • Chairman/Deputy Chairman (Rajya Sabha & State Councils).
  • Judges of the Supreme Court & High Courts.
  • Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG).

A - Affirmations

Sch 3
  • Content: Oaths or affirmations formats.
  • Covers: Union/State Ministers, MP/MLA/MLC Candidates & Members, SC/HC Judges, CAG.
🚨 Exam Trap: The President, Vice-President, and Governors do NOT take their oath under Sch 3! (Their formats are under Articles 60, 69, and 159 respectively).

R - Rajya Sabha

Sch 4
  • Content: Allocation of seats in the Council of States (Rajya Sabha) to States and UTs.
  • Distributed primarily based on Population.
  • Relevant Articles: Articles 4(1) and 80(2).

S - Scheduled Areas

Sch 5
  • Content: Administration & control of Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes.
  • Scope: Covers central/peninsular states (Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, MP, etc.).
  • Exception: Excludes Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram.

O - Other Tribal Areas

Sch 6
  • Content: Admin of tribal areas in 4 North-Eastern states.
  • Mnemonic: AMTM (Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram).
  • Key Feature: Creates Autonomous District Councils (ADCs) with legislative/judicial powers over land, forest, and customs.

F - Federal Provisions

Sch 7
  • Content: Division of powers via Three Lists.
  • Union List: 97 subjects originally (now ~100) — Defense, Banking, Foreign Affairs.
  • State List: 66 subjects originally (now ~61) — Police, Public Health, Agriculture.
  • Concurrent List: 47 originally (now ~52) — Education, Forests, Marriage (added via 42nd Amend 1976).
  • Article: Article 246.

O - Official Languages

Sch 8
  • Evolution: 14 Originally ➔ 22 Currently.
  • 21st Amendment (1967): Added Sindhi.
  • 71st Amendment (1992): Added Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali (KMN).
  • 92nd Amendment (2003): Added Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali (BDMS).
  • Articles: Articles 344(1) and 351.

L - Land Reforms

Sch 9
  • Content: Validation of Acts regarding land tenure & abolition of Zamindari.
  • Addition: 1st Constitutional Amendment Act, 1951.
  • I.R. Coelho Case (2007): Laws placed after April 24, 1973 are open to judicial review if they violate Basic Structure.

D - Defection

Sch 10
  • Content: Anti-Defection Law (Disqualification of MPs/MLAs).
  • Addition: 52nd Amendment Act, 1985.
  • Modification: 91st Amendment Act, 2003 (removed exception for "splits").
  • Articles: Articles 102(2) and 191(2).

P - Panchayats

Sch 11
  • Content: Rural Local Government powers & responsibilities.
  • Contains 29 functional items.
  • Addition: 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992.
  • Article: Article 243-G (Part IX).

M - Municipalities

Sch 12
  • Content: Urban Local Government powers & responsibilities.
  • Contains 18 functional items.
  • Addition: 74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992.
  • Article: Article 243-W (Part IX-A).
