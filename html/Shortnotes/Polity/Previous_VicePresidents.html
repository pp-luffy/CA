🇮🇳 Vice Presidents of India Timeline

⚡ Quick Revision Cheat-Sheet

Dr. S. Radhakrishnan: 1st VP, 2 full terms.
Dr. Zakir Husain: Co-founder Jamia Millia.
V.V. Giri: Resigned VP to win Prez election.
G.S. Pathak: 1st VP who didn't become Prez.
B.D. Jatti: Acting Prez for 5 months (1977).
Justice M. Hidayatullah: CJI, Acting Prez, & VP.
R. Venkataraman: VP for 3 yrs, became 8th Prez.
Dr. S.D. Sharma: Ex-CM of Bhopal.
K.R. Narayanan: Former Diplomat/Ambassador.
Krishan Kant: 1st & only VP to die in office.
B.S. Shekhawat: 3-time CM of Rajasthan.
M.H. Ansari: 2nd VP to serve 2 consecutive full terms.
M. Venkaiah Naidu: Ex-I&B & Urban Dev Minister.
Jagdeep Dhankhar: Current VP, Ex-WB Governor.
1952 - 1962

Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan

The first Vice President of India. He is one of only two Vice Presidents to serve two full terms. He subsequently became the second President of India.
First VP 2 Full Terms
1962 - 1967

Dr. Zakir Husain

A prominent educationist and co-founder of Jamia Millia Islamia. He went on to become the third President of India.
Educationist
1967 - 1969

Varahagiri Venkata Giri (V.V. Giri)

His term was cut short when he assumed the role of Acting President following Dr. Zakir Husain's death. He subsequently resigned from the vice presidency to contest and win the presidential election.
Resigned Acting President
1969 - 1974

Gopal Swarup Pathak

A former judge of the Allahabad High Court. He was the first Vice President who did not subsequently become the President of India.
1st Not to become Prez Former Judge
1974 - 1979

Basappa Danappa Jatti

Former Chief Minister of Mysore. During his tenure as Vice President, he also served as the Acting President for five months in 1977 after the sudden demise of President Fakhruddin Ali Ahmed.
Ex-CM Mysore Acting President
1979 - 1984

Justice Mohammad Hidayatullah

Holds the unique distinction of being the only person to have served as the Chief Justice of India, the Acting President of India, and the Vice President of India.
Only CJI + Acting Prez + VP
1984 - 1987

Ramaswamy Venkataraman

Served as Vice President for three years before being elected as the eighth President of India in 1987.
Became 8th Prez
1987 - 1992

Dr. Shankar Dayal Sharma

Before becoming Vice President, he served as the Chief Minister of Bhopal and held Cabinet positions. He went on to become the ninth President of India.
Ex-CM Bhopal
1992 - 1997

K. R. Narayanan

A former diplomat who served as an ambassador to several countries. He later became the tenth President of India.
Ambassador/Diplomat
1997 - 2002

Krishan Kant

He is the first and only Vice President of India to die while in office. Prior to this role, he was the Governor of Andhra Pradesh.
Died in Office Ex-Governor AP
2002 - 2007

Bhairon Singh Shekhawat

A veteran political leader who served as the Chief Minister of Rajasthan three times before being elected as the Vice President.
3-time CM Rajasthan
2007 - 2017

Mohammad Hamid Ansari

A former diplomat and the second Vice President (after Dr. S. Radhakrishnan) to serve two consecutive full terms in office.
2 Consecutive Terms
2017 - 2022

M. Venkaiah Naidu

A prominent parliamentarian who held several key cabinet portfolios, including Information and Broadcasting as well as Urban Development, before assuming the vice presidency.
Key Cabinet Portfolios
2022 - Present

Jagdeep Dhankhar

The current Vice President of India. Before taking office, he served as the Governor of West Bengal and is a senior advocate by profession.
Ex-WB Governor Senior Advocate
