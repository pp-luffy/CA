Ecology & Tribals

1. Maithilee Bhue: Forest Conservation

  • Maithilee Bhue (40): Tribal woman from Dhodrokusum (near Debrigarh Wildlife Sanctuary, Sambalpur).
  • 1st tribal woman to join Debrigarh eco-tourism (2023). Highlighted by PM Modi in 137th Mann Ki Baat.
  • Encouraged villagers to avoid felling green trees. Made Dhodrokusum a Green Village.
  • Village selected for women-managed tribal homestays. Inspired 20 fringe villages.
  • Debrigarh WS: Bargarh dist, near Hirakud Dam (Mahanadi). Sal, Asana, Bija, Aanla. Fauna: Leopard, chousingha, gaur. Winter ground for migratory birds.
🎯 MCQ Alert: Maithilee Bhue's village (Dhodrokusum) & sanctuary (Debrigarh - Bargarh/Sambalpur border region).
Infrastructure

2. Rameshwar-Paradeep Coastal Corridor

  • NHAI received 17 bids for 160.18 km Access-Controlled Greenfield Coastal NH.
  • Pkg I: Rameshwar-Konark (79.4 km, 4-lane).
  • Pkg II: Konark-Paradeep (80.7 km, 2-lane).
  • Mode: HAM (Hybrid Annuity Mode). Cost: ₹8,300.79 cr. Speed: 100 km/h.
  • Connects: Sri Jagannath Temple, Puri Beach, Konark, Paradeep/Astarang/Subarnarekha ports.
  • Districts: Khurda, Puri, Kendrapada, Jagatsinghpur. Alternate to NH-16 & NH-316.
🎯 MCQ Alert: Project mode is HAM. Length: ~160km. Connects Rameshwar to Paradeep.
Environment

3. ARISE Coastal Resilience Plan

  • ARISE = Adaptive Resilience Investment in Coastal Systems on Economics.
  • Assesses Odisha's 573-km coastline.
  • Joint assessment by ADB (Asian Development Bank) & OSDMA.
  • Focus: Blue Economy, sediment management, real-time data tech.
  • ADB: Est. Dec 1966, HQ Manila (69 members).
🎯 MCQ Alert: Odisha Coastline length (573 km). ARISE partners with ADB.
Polity

4. Odisha to Implement PESA

  • PESA Act, 1996 (Panchayats Extension to Scheduled Areas) proposed for Cabinet approval.
  • Strengthens Gram Sabhas and self-governance in Fifth Schedule areas.
  • Benefits: 9 tribal-dominated districts, 117 blocks, 2,022 GPs.
  • Tribal population: ~22.8% of Odisha's population.
🎯 MCQ Alert: PESA applies to 5th Schedule areas. Odisha tribal pop % is 22.8%.
State Schemes

Governance

7. Paperless Property Deed Registration

  • Amendments to Registration Act, 1908 (8 existing modified, 9 new inserted).
  • Limits property documents to 4 pages.
  • Linked to UPIN (Unique Property Identification Number).
  • Aadhaar consent-based verification; legalizes e-signatures & e-seals.

Culture/Law

11. Shree Jagannath Land Rules 2026

  • Rules for settlement of Shree Jagannath Temple (SJT) land.
  • Valuation: Higher of Benchmark Value (BMV) or Average Land Sale Statistics (ALSS).
  • Concession for 'Bijesthali' of mathas with continuous 30-year possession.
  • NOC issued by SJTMC. Online portal integrated with Bhulekh/Bhunaksha.

Legislature

13. Odisha Jan Vishwas & Stamp Bills

  • Jan Vishwas Bill 2026: Phase 2 of reforms. Amends 62 provisions in 16 Acts to decriminalise minor offences.
  • Indian Stamp Amendment Bill: Uniform 5% stamp duty on apartment units (replacing 3-5% slabs). ₹50,000 nominal duty for common areas transfer. 90% exemption for EWS.
