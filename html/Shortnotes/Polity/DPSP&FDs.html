📜 PART IV: DIRECTIVE PRINCIPLES OF STATE POLICY (Art. 36–51)

📌 DPSP Fundamentals & Quotes

  • Location: Part IV, Articles 36 to 51
  • Borrowed From: Irish Constitution (1937), which in turn borrowed it from the Spanish Constitution.
  • Resembles: Instrument of Instructions in the Government of India Act, 1935 (noted by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar).
  • Nature: Non-justiciable (not enforceable by courts, Art. 37), positive obligations on the State, aimed at establishing a Welfare State (social & economic democracy).
  • Key Quote: Granville Austin described Fundamental Rights and DPSPs together as the "Conscience of the Constitution".

⚖️ Chunk 1: Foundation & Justice (36–39A)

Delhi Always Wants Perfect Lawyers.
  • 36 (D) Definition of State: Carries the same meaning as in Part III (Article 12).
  • 37 (A) Application of Principles: Non-justiciable (not enforceable by courts), yet fundamental in the governance of the country.
  • 38 (W) Welfare of People (Social Order): Secure social, economic & political justice. Clause (2) directs State to minimise inequalities in income, status, facilities & opportunities. 44th (1978)
  • 39 (P) Certain Principles of Policy (6 Clauses):
    (a) Adequate means of livelihood for all citizens
    (b) Equitable distribution of material resources for common good
    (c) Prevention of concentration of wealth & means of production
    (d) Equal pay for equal work for men and women
    (e) Health & strength of workers and children against abuse
    (f) Opportunities for healthy development of children 42nd (1976)
  • 39A (L) Equal Justice & Free Legal Aid: Ensure justice is not denied due to economic or other disabilities. 42nd (1976)

🌾 Chunk 2: Villages & Workers (40–43B)

Professionals Who Master Law Manage Courts.
  • 40 (P) Village Panchayats: Organise village panchayats and endow them with powers to function as units of self-government.
  • 41 (W) Right to Work, Education & Public Assistance: Within limits of economic capacity, in cases of unemployment, old age, sickness & disablement.
  • 42 (M) Just & Humane Work Conditions & Maternity Relief: Provisions for humane working conditions and maternity relief.
  • 43 (L) Living Wage & Cottage Industries: Secure living wage, decent standard of life, and promote cottage industries in rural areas.
  • 43A (M) Workers' Participation in Management: Secure worker participation in management of industries. 42nd (1976)
  • 43B (C) Co-operative Societies: Promote voluntary formation, autonomous functioning & democratic control of co-operative societies. 97th (2011)

🤝 Chunk 3: Society & Welfare (44–47)

Usually Cases Settle Nicely,
  • 44 (U) Uniform Civil Code (UCC): Endeavour to secure a Uniform Civil Code for all citizens throughout the territory of India.
  • 45 (C) Early Childhood Care & Education: Provide early childhood care and education for all children until age 6 years (6–14 yrs moved to Art. 21A). 86th (2002)
  • 46 (S) SCs, STs & Weaker Sections: Promote their educational and economic interests; protect them from social injustice and exploitation.
  • 47 (N) Nutrition, Standard of Living & Public Health: Raise nutrition and public health levels; endeavour to prohibit intoxicating drinks & drugs (except medicinal use).

🌍 Chunk 4: Nature, Heritage & Peace (48–51)

And Everyone Makes Justice Possible.
  • 48 (A) Agriculture & Animal Husbandry: Organise on modern, scientific lines; preserve & improve breeds; prohibit slaughter of cows, calves, and other milch & draught cattle.
  • 48A (E) Environment, Forests & Wildlife: Protect and improve the environment and safeguard forests and wildlife. 42nd (1976)
  • 49 (M) Protection of Monuments: Protect monuments, places & objects of artistic or historic interest declared of national importance.
  • 50 (J) Separation of Judiciary from Executive: Take steps to separate judiciary from executive in public services of the State.
  • 51 (P) International Peace & Security: Promote international peace, maintain just relations between nations, foster respect for international law & treaty obligations, and encourage settlement of disputes by arbitration.

🧩 Ideologies, Amendments & Outside Part IV

  • Ideological Classification (not in Constitution):
    • Socialistic: 38, 39, 39A, 41, 42, 43, 43A, 47
    • Gandhian: 40, 43, 43B, 46, 47, 48
    • Liberal-Intellectual: 44, 45, 48, 48A, 49, 50, 51
  • 4 Key Amendments to DPSPs:
    • 42nd (1976): Added 39A, 43A, 48A; modified 39(f)
    • 44th (1978): Added 38(2)
    • 86th (2002): Substituted Art. 45 (below 6 yrs)
    • 97th (2011): Added 43B (Co-operatives)
  • 3 Directives Outside Part IV:
    • Art. 335 (Part XVI): Claims of SCs/STs to services & posts, consistent with administrative efficiency.
    • Art. 350A (Part XVII): Instruction in mother-tongue at primary stage for linguistic minorities.
    • Art. 351 (Part XVII): Duty of Union to promote spread & development of Hindi language.

🇮🇳 PART IVA: FUNDAMENTAL DUTIES (Article 51A: a to k)

📌 Fundamental Duties Basics

  • Location: Part IVA, Article 51A (Clauses a to k).
  • Original Constitution (1950): Did NOT contain Fundamental Duties.
  • Borrowed From: USSR (Soviet) Constitution (Note: Japan is the only major democratic country with citizen duties in its constitution).
  • Added By: 42nd Amendment (1976) added 10 duties (a–j).
  • 11th Duty Added By: 86th Amendment (2002) added Clause (k) (education for 6–14 yrs).
  • Scope & Nature: Applicable only to Indian citizens (not foreigners); non-justiciable, though Parliament can enforce them via specific laws.

🛡️ Duties Part 1: Clauses (a) to (e)

Citizens Fight United, Defending Borders.
  • (a) C Abide by the Constitution; respect its ideals, institutions, National Flag & National Anthem. 42nd
  • (b) F Cherish & follow the noble ideals that inspired the national Freedom struggle. 42nd
  • (c) U Uphold and protect sovereignty, Unity, and integrity of India. 42nd
  • (d) D Defend the country and render national service when called upon. 42nd
  • (e) B Promote harmony & common Brotherhood; renounce practices derogatory to the dignity of women. 42nd

🌱 Duties Part 2: Clauses (f) to (k)

Country Enjoys Strong Progress, Excellence, Education.
  • (f) C Value and preserve rich heritage of our composite Culture. 42nd
  • (g) E Protect & improve natural Environment (forests, lakes, rivers, wildlife) & compassion for living creatures. 42nd
  • (h) S Develop Scientific temper, humanism, and spirit of inquiry & reform. 42nd
  • (i) P Safeguard Public property and abjure violence. 42nd
  • (j) E Strive towards Excellence in all spheres of individual & collective activity. 42nd
  • (k) E Parent/guardian duty to provide opportunities for Education to child/ward aged 6–14 years. 86th (2002)

🏛️ Key Committees & Clause Anchors

  • Swaran Singh Committee (1976):
    Recommended adding Fundamental Duties during the Emergency. Suggested 8 duties (42nd Amendment added 10).
    2 Rejected Recommendations: (1) Parliament imposing penalties/punishment for non-compliance, (2) Duty to pay taxes.
  • Verma Committee (1999):
    Identified existing legal provisions enforcing several duties:
    • Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act (1971)
    • Wildlife Protection Act (1972)
    • Forest Conservation Act (1980)
    • Protection of Civil Rights Act (1955)
    • Representation of the People Act (1951)
  • Quick Clause-Letter Anchors:
    (a) Anthem | (d) Defend | (g) Green (Env) | (h) Humanism | (k) Kids' Edu (6–14)
