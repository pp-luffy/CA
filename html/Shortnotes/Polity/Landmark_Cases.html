Phase 1

Amendability & Basic Structure

📌 Shankari Prasad Case (1951)
Parliament CAN amend Fundamental Rights (FRs) under Article 368. Constitutional amendments are NOT "laws" under Article 13.
📌 Golaknath Case (1967)
SC reversed stance. Parliament CANNOT abridge/take away FRs. Amendments are treated as "laws" under Article 13.
📌 Kesavananda Bharati (1973)
Monumental judgment! Parliament can amend anything EXCEPT the "Basic Structure". Judicial review became a non-negotiable pillar.
📌 Minerva Mills Case (1980)
Struck down parts of 42nd Amendment giving absolute power. Established that a limited amending power is itself part of the basic structure.
Phase 2

Liberty & Article 21

🛡️ A.K. Gopalan Case (1950)
Narrow interpretation. Allowed preventive detention via "Procedure Established by Law" (minimal judicial safeguards).
🛡️ Maneka Gandhi Case (1978)
Overturned Gopalan! Read "Due Process of Law" into Art 21. Any law curtailing liberty must be "just, fair, and reasonable".
🛡️ ADM Jabalpur (1976)
Emergency-era (Habeas Corpus Case). Majority suspended right to move courts for liberty. Famous for Justice H.R. Khanna’s legendary dissent.
🛡️ K.S. Puttaswamy (2017)
Unanimous ruling. Right to Privacy is inherently protected as a fundamental right under Article 21.
Phase 4

Social Justice & Appointments

🎓 Champakam Dorairajan (1951)
SC struck down communal educational quotas. Prompted the 1st Constitutional Amendment Act and addition of Article 15(4).
🎓 Indra Sawhney [Mandal] (1992)
Upheld 27% OBC reservation. Introduced the "Creamy Layer" concept. Capped total reservations at 50%.
🎓 1st, 2nd, 3rd Judges Cases (1981-98)
Judgments that created and evolved the Collegium System for appointing Supreme Court and High Court judges.
Phase 3

Centre-State & Federalism

🏛️ S.R. Bommai Case (1994)
The ultimate judgment on Article 356 (President's Rule). Federalism is a basic feature. Arbitrary state govt dismissals are unconstitutional. Floor tests MUST happen in the Assembly, not the Governor's bungalow.
Phase 5

Secularism & Criminalization

🛑 Shah Bano Case (1985)
Upheld maintenance rights for Muslim women under Section 125 CrPC, igniting nationwide debates on the Uniform Civil Code (UCC).
🛑 Sarla Mudgal Case (1995)
Hindu husband cannot convert to Islam for a second marriage without dissolving the first. SC called for urgent UCC enactment.
🛑 Lily Thomas v. UoI (2013)
Immediate disqualification for any MP/MLA/MLC convicted of a crime with a sentence of 2 years or more. Struck down Section 8(4) of the RPA, 1951.
