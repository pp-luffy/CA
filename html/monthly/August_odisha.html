Govt

1. Odisha Issues SOP for Rescue and Rehabilitation of Bonded Labourers

  • Legal Action: Stronger investigation & prosecution for strict punishment of traffickers/middlemen; aligns with Central Govt SOP.
  • Repatriation: Will not be delayed due to pending investigation or trials.
  • Escort Provision: DM/SDM ensures representative & police officer accompany rescued worker.
  • Implementation: SOP released in Odia for better departmental coordination.
  • Occasion: UN World Day Against Trafficking in Persons & Golden Jubilee of Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act, 1976.
Environment

2. Catch the Rain in Odisha: From Awareness to Groundwater Recharge

  • Objective: Conserve rainwater by collecting & storing "where it falls, when it falls."
  • Implementation: Recharge structures, renovating traditional water bodies, rooftop harvesting, watershed development.
  • Community: Involves PRIs, ULBs, educational institutions, and locals.
  • Nodal Agency: Led by Ministry of Jal Shakti (National Water Mission) with State support.
  • Significance: Mitigates groundwater depletion & enhances climate resilience.

6. Odisha's PM JANMAN Initiative Benefits Over 3.12 Lakh PVTGs

  • Implementation: Saturation coverage of basic infrastructure in PVTG habitations.
  • Beneficiaries: Over 3.12 lakh covered across the State.
  • Target Communities: Odisha has 13 PVTGs, one of the highest in Indian states.

8. Bhubaneswari OCP Sets Benchmark for Sustainable Coal Mining

  • Location/Scale: Talcher Coalfield; Peak capacity 30 MTPA.
  • Production: FY 25-26 produced 26.477 MT (105.98% of target); 370.4 MT since inception. FY 26-27 target enhanced to 28 MT.
  • Tech Adoption: 100% blast-free Surface Miner tech; CIL's first Automatic Truck Loading System (ATLS); 25 MTPA FMC SILO; >85% dispatched via rail.
  • Environment: 5-hectare Sal nursery, dust suppression, air quality monitoring.
  • Record: Zero-fatality for 5 consecutive years; PBT of ₹1,792.60 crore (FY 25-26).

16. Odisha Constitutes Panel to Probe Village Relocation in Satkosia Tiger Reserve

  • Panel: 5-member committee headed by ACS Revenue & Disaster Mgmt following OHRC directions. Includes former NTCA Member Secy Anup Nayak.
  • Probe: Examine village-wise relocation process (except Raiguda village).
  • Legal Checks: Wildlife Protection Act 1972, Forest Rights Act 2006, NTCA guidelines.
  • OHRC Findings: Forest rights unsettled, Gram Sabha procedures ignored. No relocation from buffer/fringe until report is submitted.
