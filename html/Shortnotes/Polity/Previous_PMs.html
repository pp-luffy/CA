🇮🇳 Prime Ministers of India

Govt

1. Jawaharlal Nehru (1947–1964)

  • Tenure: India’s first and longest-serving Prime Minister (nearly 17 years).
  • Notable Works: Established the Planning Commission (Five-Year Plans), championed the Non-Aligned Movement in foreign policy, and founded premier institutions like the IITs.
Govt

2. Gulzarilal Nanda (Acting: 1964 & 1966)

  • Tenure: India's first Acting Prime Minister.
  • History: Took charge briefly for 13 days each following the sudden deaths of Jawaharlal Nehru and Lal Bahadur Shastri to ensure a smooth transition.
Culture

3. Lal Bahadur Shastri (1964–1966)

  • Notable Works: Led the country during the 1965 India-Pakistan War and coined the iconic morale-boosting slogan "Jai Jawan, Jai Kisan".
  • Previous Roles: Served as Minister of Home Affairs and Minister of Railways.
Govt

4. Indira Gandhi (1966–1977, 1980–1984)

  • Tenure: First and only female Prime Minister of India.
  • Notable Works/History: Nationalized major banks, led India to victory in the 1971 war (which resulted in the creation of Bangladesh), and controversially imposed the 1975–1977 State of Emergency.
  • End of Term: Assassinated by her bodyguards while in office in 1984.
Policy

5. Morarji Desai (1977–1979)

  • Tenure: First non-Congress Prime Minister (Janata Party).
  • Notable Works: Worked to reverse Emergency-era constitutional changes and normalized relations with Pakistan.
  • Previous Roles: Chief Minister of Bombay State and Union Finance Minister.
Agri/Env

6. Charan Singh (1979–1980)

  • Tenure: The only Prime Minister who never faced Parliament, as his government collapsed before a floor test.
  • Notable Works: Widely regarded as a champion for farmers and rural rights.
  • Previous Roles: Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh and Union Finance Minister.
Tech

7. Rajiv Gandhi (1984–1989)

  • Tenure: Youngest Prime Minister, taking office at age 40.
  • Notable Works: Pioneered India's IT and telecom revolution, introduced the anti-defection law, and lowered the voting age to 18.
  • End of Term: Assassinated by a suicide bomber in 1991 while campaigning.
Govt

8. V.P. Singh (1989–1990)

  • Notable Works: Implemented the landmark Mandal Commission report, which introduced job reservations for Other Backward Classes (OBCs).
  • Previous Roles: Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh and Union Finance Minister.
Economy

9. Chandra Shekhar (1990–1991)

  • Tenure: Headed a minority Samajwadi Janata Party government backed by Congress.
  • History: Navigated the early stages of India's severe 1991 balance-of-payments economic crisis.
Economy

10. P.V. Narasimha Rao (1991–1996)

  • Tenure: First Prime Minister from South India.
  • Notable Works: The primary architect of the historic 1991 economic liberalization, which dismantled the License Raj and opened the Indian economy to global trade.
  • Previous Roles: Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh.
Infra/Tech

11. Atal Bihari Vajpayee (1996, 1998–2004)

  • Tenure: First non-Congress Prime Minister to complete a full five-year term. His initial 1996 stint lasted only 13 days.
  • Notable Works: Conducted the 1998 Pokhran-II nuclear tests, launched the Golden Quadrilateral highway project, and championed universal primary education.
Govt

12. H.D. Deve Gowda (1996–1997)

  • Tenure: Headed the United Front, a diverse coalition government.
  • Previous Roles: Chief Minister of Karnataka.
Foreign

13. I.K. Gujral (1997–1998)

  • Notable Works: Formulated the "Gujral Doctrine," a milestone five-point framework for maintaining peaceful, non-reciprocal, and cooperative foreign relations with India's immediate neighbors.
Govt

14. Dr. Manmohan Singh (2004–2014)

  • Tenure: First Sikh Prime Minister, serving two full terms.
  • Notable Works: Championed the Indo-US civil nuclear agreement, the Right to Information (RTI) Act, and the MGNREGA rural employment guarantee scheme.
  • Previous Roles: Union Finance Minister (driving force behind the 1991 reforms) and Governor of the Reserve Bank of India.
Infra/Govt

15. Narendra Modi (2014–Present)

  • Tenure: First Prime Minister born after India's independence, currently serving his third consecutive term.
  • Notable Works: Rolled out the Goods and Services Tax (GST), Swachh Bharat Abhiyan (Clean India Mission), Make in India, and the Digital India infrastructure.
  • Previous Roles: Chief Minister of Gujarat.
