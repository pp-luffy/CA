India-New Zealand FTA

A bilateral trade agreement designed to eliminate customs duties on traded goods, effective from Vijay Dashmi (Oct 20, 2026).

  • New Zealand committed $20B in investments to India.
  • Benefits Indian exports in textiles, footwear, pharma, and gems.
  • Secures access to 118 services sectors, 5,000 temporary employment visas, and 1,000 working holiday visas annually.
  • Indian STEM students get 3-year post-study work visas (4 years for Doctoral scholars).
⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert

Sensitive agricultural sectors like dairy, sugar, and edible oils are explicitly excluded from tariff concessions to protect domestic farmers.

✍️ Mains Analytical Hook

Boosts India's MSME export ecosystem and secures global supply chain integration, perfectly aligning with the "Viksit Bharat 2047" economic vision.

⚖️ Statutory Anchors

Subject to stringent "Rules of Origin" mechanisms to prevent third-party misuse.

Changes in PLFS (Jan 2027)

The primary survey measuring India's employment and unemployment rates is being overhauled to reflect modern labor realities.

  • Introduces a separate sampling frame specifically for gig and platform workers.
  • New 2027-28 sample frame reflects rapid urbanization and redrawn district boundaries.
  • The Jan-Mar 2027 transition period will merge data from both old and new sampling frames.
  • Crucially, new questionnaires will track unpaid household, volunteer, and caregiving work (age 15+).
⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert

The nodal agency conducting PLFS is the National Statistics Office (NSO) under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), NOT the Ministry of Labour.

✍️ Mains Analytical Hook

Measuring unpaid caregiving work represents a watershed moment for recognizing women's invisible economic contributions and framing inclusive GDP metrics.

🗺️ Statutory Anchors

Executed by the National Statistics Office (NSO).

Integrated Mfg Cluster, Gaya

A centralized industrial zone providing shared physical infrastructure to massively reduce setup costs for incoming industries.

  • Spread across 1,670 acres in Dobhi block, Gaya (Bihar).
  • Joint venture executed by BIADA and NICDIT.
  • Targeted focus on agro-based industries, textiles, garments, and engineering fabrication.
  • Provides shared roads, water supply, power, and solid waste management.
⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert

This specific node falls strictly under the Amritsar-Kolkata Industrial Corridor development project.

✍️ Mains Analytical Hook

Shared infrastructure models act as catalysts for MSME and foreign direct investment by mitigating massive initial capital expenditure (CAPEX) hurdles in developing states like Bihar.

⚖️ Statutory Anchors

National Industrial Corridor Development Program.

Farmer IDs (Digital Agriculture)

A unique digital identity assigned to farmers to streamline the delivery of state agriculture benefits without repeated paperwork.

  • Created 11 crore Farmer IDs by Oct 2026, hitting the target 5 months early.
  • Approved in September 2024 under the Digital Agriculture Mission.
  • Simplifies rapid access to Kisan Credit Cards, crop insurance, and disaster relief funds.
  • Over 6 crore farmers are already receiving direct benefits through this ID pipeline.
⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert

Farmer IDs are an initiative under the broader Digital Agriculture Mission by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, acting as a foundational registry, not just a scheme.

✍️ Mains Analytical Hook

Showcases "Cooperative Federalism" in action. By preventing systemic duplication and fraud, it ensures leak-proof Direct Benefit Transfers (DBT) to the most vulnerable.

⚖️ Statutory Anchors

Digital Agriculture Mission rollout.

Agrivoltaics World Conference

The concurrent use of a single parcel of land for both agricultural crop production and solar electricity generation.

  • 7th Edition held at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi (Oct 2026).
  • Organized by Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) with Germany's BMUV.
  • First time ever hosted in the Global South.
  • Aims to transform farmers from food producers (Annadata) into energy producers (Urjadata).
⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert

Agrivoltaics does NOT mean alternating seasons between crops and solar. It explicitly means dual-use of the same land at the same time (e.g., elevated panels over growing crops).

✍️ Mains Analytical Hook

Provides a scalable solution to the historic "Food vs. Fuel/Energy" land debate, simultaneously boosting rural incomes, grid capacity, and climate resilience.

🗺️ Statutory Anchors

MNRE policy frameworks and solar mandates.

Khet Bachao Abhiyan (Rabi 2026)

An awareness campaign urging farmers to test their soil first and apply chemical fertilizers only if scientifically required.

  • Launched from Karnal, Haryana, targeting the Rabi Sowing Season (Oct-Dec).
  • Core focus on balanced fertilizer usage to drastically cut input costs and protect soil health.
  • Promotes crop diversification, natural farming, and crop residue management.
  • Aims to reach 1 crore participants through Kisan Chaupals and ATMA functionaries.
⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert

Be crystal clear on crop classification: Wheat, mustard, gram, barley, and peas are Rabi crops (sown Oct-Dec, harvested Mar-Apr).

✍️ Mains Analytical Hook

Blind fertilizer application degrades soil productivity over time. Rationalizing usage based on lab testing ensures sustainable agriculture, lowers farmer debt, and curtails chemical runoff into water tables.

⚖️ Statutory Anchors

Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare grassroots outreach via ATMA.

ATAL (Air Pollution Lab)

Integrated Atmospheric Chemistry and Aerosol Toxicity Analysis Laboratory, inaugurated to decode the exact chemical threat of Delhi NCR smog.

  • Established under Mission Mausam at IITM’s Delhi branch.
  • Goes beyond standard AQI to measure actual aerosol toxicity (metals, toxic PM2.5 elements).
  • Deploys the Air-wise system: a 400m high-resolution air quality forecast for the next 72 hours.
  • Pinpoints specific sources like stubble burning, vehicular exhaust, and construction dust.
⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert

ATAL does not just spit out a generic numerical AQI. Its primary function is analyzing the chemical composition and biological health impacts of the pollutants.

✍️ Mains Analytical Hook

Provides robust empirical data directly to the Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM), allowing for pinpoint, legally-defensible enforcement of the Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP).

🗺️ Statutory Anchors

Mission Mausam & CAQM jurisdiction.

Pumped Storage Hydropower

Colloquially known as a "Water Battery", it uses excess electricity to pump water uphill, storing gravitational potential energy to be released later.

  • Uses surplus daytime solar energy to push water to an upper reservoir.
  • At night, water is released downhill to spin turbines, supplying electricity when the sun isn't shining.
  • India’s Western region holds the highest potential (129.72 GW).
  • Currently operational: 7.43 GW. Target: 105.56 GW by 2035-36. India is 4th globally.
⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert

Pumped Storage (PSP) is strictly for long-duration energy storage, whereas Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) are preferred for rapid-response, short-duration needs.

✍️ Mains Analytical Hook

As India pivots toward its 500 GW non-fossil fuel target by 2030, PSPs are critical macro-infrastructure to stabilize grids against the intermittent nature of wind and solar power.

⚖️ Statutory Anchors

Central Electricity Authority (CEA) guidelines.

Indigenous AIP System

Air-Independent Propulsion technology allows diesel-electric submarines to operate underwater for extended periods without surfacing for air.

  • Phase-2 (Energy Module) inaugurated by DRDO’s Naval Materials Research Laboratory (NMRL) at L&T Hazira Complex, Gujarat.
  • Drastically reduces a submarine's dependence on atmospheric oxygen for power generation.
  • Slated for integration into the Indian Navy's Kalvari-class submarines by early 2027.
⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert

AIP technology is designed specifically to upgrade diesel-electric submarines. Nuclear submarines already possess limitless underwater endurance and do not require AIP.

✍️ Mains Analytical Hook

A masterclass in DRDO-industry synergy. Mastering AIP domestically shatters import reliance and geometrically increases the stealth and lethality of India's underwater fleet in the Indo-Pacific.

🗺️ Statutory Anchors

Atmanirbhar Bharat defense manufacturing mandate.
