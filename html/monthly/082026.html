🔓 Unlock UPSC Background & Revision Data

Legal Position vs SC Ruling

Legally, neither Ramsar Convention nor Wetland Rules prescribe a fixed buffer. National Parks/Sanctuaries usually have a 1km mining prohibition unless a larger ESZ is notified. The SC enforced a strict 10km buffer for this Conservation Reserve.
