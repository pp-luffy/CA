ICAR & Central Potato Research Institute (CPRI)

Topic: Nutrient-Rich Colored Potato Varieties & Biofortification

What is Hidden Hunger?

Hidden hunger occurs when a person consumes sufficient calories (chapatis, rice) but suffers from chronic micronutrient deficiencies (essential vitamins, minerals, and proteins).

Key Potato Varieties Developed

  • Kufri Neel (2018): Purple-skinned, rich in antioxidants.
  • Kufri Manik (2021): Red potato rich in anthocyanins, zinc, and iron.
  • Kufri Jamuni: Purple-fleshed, high yield (32-35 tons/hectare), excellent storage quality, low rotting, ideal for traditional dishes (aloo paratha) and processing.
⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert: Biofortification is achieved through traditional plant breeding / genetic selection, NOT through genetically modified (GM) transgenic insertion in these specific varieties.

Mahila Kisan Diwas 2026 (15th October)

Organized by: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

Objective & Significance

Recognizes the foundational role of women in agriculture (crop cultivation, animal rearing, seed saving). Venue: Dr. Ambedkar International Center, New Delhi.

Key Highlights & Launches

  • Honoring outstanding women farmers across tribal and rural regions.
  • Release of book: "Women Farmers of India: Journey from Challenges to Success".
  • Focus on affordable credit, farm mechanization, modern equipment access, and rural entrepreneurship.

National Strategic Trade Command Center (NSTCC)

Developed by: Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce & Industry

Core Function

A centralized digital monitoring center tracking India's imports, exports, and international trade activities under one roof using interactive dashboards.

Integration & Key Areas

  • Integrated with DGFT (Directorate General of Foreign Trade) and Trade Connect e-Platform.
  • Covers Importer-Exporter Code (IEC), Advance Authorization, Export Promotion Capital Goods (EPCG), Certificate of Origin (CoO), and SCOMET (Special Chemicals, Organisms, Materials, Equipment, and Technologies).
  • SCOMET regulates sensitive items having both civilian and military utility.

PM GatiShakti National Master Plan (5 Years)

Developed by: BISAG-N (Bhaskaracharya National Institute for Space Applications and Geo-informatics)

Objective & Technology

Launched in October 2021 to integrate infrastructure planning across ministries, enhance multi-modal connectivity, and reduce logistic costs using geospatial technology (GIS).

Key Achievements

  • Integrated over 1,800 data layers (railways, highways, forests, industrial zones).
  • Covers 58 central ministries and all 36 States/UTs.
  • Prevents departmental duplication of work and curtails project delays.

National Social Assistance Program (PMSSY)

Nodal Ministry: Ministry of Rural Development | Funding: 100% Central Assistance

Constitutional Link & Sub-schemes

Backed by Article 41 of the Constitution (DPSP) directing public assistance for old age, unemployment, sickness, and disability. Renamed to Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana (Old Age, Widow, and Divyangjan pensions).

Major Structural Reforms

  • Disability threshold relaxed from 80% down to 40%.
  • Disability pension eligibility now starts from birth.

Headquarters: 55 Lodi Estate, New Delhi, India

The 7 Target Big Cats

Membership & Funding

TB-Free India & JOUSH Campaign

Causative Agent: Mycobacterium tuberculosis (Bacterial)

Key Statistics & BPaL Regimen

  • India's TB incidence declined by 21% (2015–2024), outperforming global averages. Treatment coverage reached 92%.
  • BPaL Regimen: Shorter 6-month treatment course for drug-resistant TB (Bedaquiline, Pretomanid, Linezolid, Moxifloxacin).

Technology & Mobilization

AI-enabled portable X-rays, Kushi AI chatbot for screening, and the JOUSH (Joint Squad for Health) youth mobilization campaign.
