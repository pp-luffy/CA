Tenures

⏳ Tenures & Terms of Office ("Whichever is earlier")

Constitutional Bodies (The 6-Year Club)

  • CAG, CEC, ECs, UPSC: 6 Years OR 65 Years of age (whichever is earlier).
  • SPSC (State PSC): 6 Years OR 62 Years of age.
  • NHRC/Lokpal: 3 Years OR 70 Years of age (NHRC revised recently). Lokpal is 5 Years or 70.
  • Finance Comm: Specified by President (Usually 5 years).

Legislative & Local Tenures

  • Rajya Sabha / MLC: Members have a 6-Year term. (1/3rd retire every 2 years).
  • Lok Sabha / MLA: 5-Year term.
  • Panchayat Mid-Term Dissolution: If dissolved before 5 yrs, the newly elected body serves ONLY the remainder of the term (not a fresh 5 years).

Delays

📅 Days, Notices & Delays

Legislative Delays (Deadlocks)

  • 14 Days Delay: RS / Council delay on Money Bill.
  • 4 Months (3+1): Max delay by State Council on Ordinary Bill.
  • 6 Months Delay: RS delays Ordinary Bill (Triggers Joint Sitting).

Notices & Vacancies

  • 14 Days Notice: Impeach Pres / Remove VP, Speaker.
  • 10 Days: Inform EC if elected to BOTH LS & RS.
  • 14 Days: Resign State Leg if elected to Parl.
  • 60 Days: Seat vacant if absent from all meetings.
Months

📆 The "Months" Matrix

Emergency Approvals

  • 1 Month: Parliament approval for National Emergency (Art 352).
  • 2 Months: Parliament approval for Pres Rule (356) & Financial (360).
  • 2 Months: Notice for Civil suit vs Pres/Gov.

The Almighty "6 Months" Rule

  • 6 Months: Max gap between sessions of Legislature.
  • 6 Months: Hold elections for Pres/VP/Local vacancies.
  • 6 Months: Max time non-member can be Minister/PM/CM.
  • 6 Months: Nominated MP window to safely join a party.
  • 6 Weeks: Time to approve Ordinance after reassembly.
  • 3 Months: Max Preventive Detention without trial.

Quorums & Limits

  • 1/10th: Quorum for LS, RS, Assemblies, & Joint Sittings.
  • 50%: Quorum for GST Council.
  • 15%: Max Cabinet size (Union & State). Min for State = 12.
  • 1/6th: Deposit forfeited if valid votes are below this.

Retirements & Mergers

  • 1/3rd: Members of RS and State Council retiring every 2 years.
  • 1/3rd (33%): Minimum reservation for women in Panchayats.
  • 2/3rd: Defection merger exception (Legislative members needed).

State Legislative Council (MLC) Split

  • 1/3rd: Elected by Local Bodies (Municipalities, etc).
  • 1/3rd: Elected by State MLAs.
  • 1/12th: Elected by Graduates (3 yrs standing).
  • 1/12th: Elected by Teachers (3 yrs standing).
  • 1/6th: Nominated by Governor (Lit, Sci, Art, Co-op, Social Svc).
Majorities

🗳️ THE DEFINITIVE MAJORITIES MATRIX

1. The Standard Majorities

  • Simple: > 50% of members Present & Voting (P&V).
    (Ordinary/Money bills, No-Confidence, Pres Rule, Fin Emergency)
  • Absolute: > 50% of the Total Membership.
    (Used to form Govt. Rarely used alone in Parliament)
  • Effective: > 50% of Effective Strength [Total - Vacancies].
    (Removal of VP in RS, Speaker/Dy Speaker removal)

2. The Four Types of Special Majorities

  • Type 1 (Art 249/312): ≥ 2/3rd Present & Voting.
    (RS exclusive: State list laws, New All-India Services)
  • Type 2 (Art 368 Standard): ≥ 2/3rd P&V + Absolute Maj.
    (Const. Amend, SC/HC judge/CEC/CAG removal, Nat Emergency)
  • Type 3 (Art 61 Toughest): ≥ 2/3rd of TOTAL Membership.
    (ONLY for Impeachment of the President)
  • Type 4 (Art 368 + Federal): Type 2 + 50% State Legislature Ratification.
    (Amendments affecting federal structure: GST, President election)

Thresholds

🗳️ Proposers, Populations & Citizenship

Elections & Proposers

  • President: 50 Proposers + 50 Seconders (₹15k deposit).
  • Vice-President: 20 Proposers + 20 Seconders (₹15k deposit).
  • RS MP Election: 10% of party MLAs or 10 MLAs (lesser).
  • SC/ST Parliament Resv: valid till Jan 25, 2030 (80 years limit).

Population Limits (Local)

  • 20 Lakhs: Min pop required to have Block Panchayat (Middle tier).
  • 3 Lakhs: Min pop for Wards Committee.
  • 10 Lakhs: Min pop for Metropolitan Area.

Citizenship Limits

  • Registration: 7 years residence.
  • Naturalization: 12 years (11 + 1 continuous).
    *Reduced to 5 years for CAA 2019 groups.
