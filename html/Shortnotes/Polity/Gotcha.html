🇮🇳 Indian Polity: The "Gotcha" Facts

📌

⚡ 1-Minute Core Revision

Statutory vs Const: Zonal Councils, CBI, Citizenship, LoP are Acts, NOT Const.
Rules vs Const: No-Confidence, Question Hour, PAC are House Rules.
Convention vs Law: PAC/Deputy Speaker from Opp, Zero Hour are Unwritten.
SC Creations: Basic Structure, Collegium, NOTA, Curative Petition.
Never Defined/Used: Budget, Federation, Basic Structure, Untouchability, Office of Profit.

📜 1. Statutory (Acts of Parliament)

Constitution created the power; Parliament passed the specific Act.

Zonal Councils
States Reorganisation Act, 1956 (Inter-State Council is Const. under Art 263).
Contingency Fund of India
Contingency Fund of India Act, 1950 (Art 267 only says Parliament "may" establish one).
Delimitation Commission
Delimitation Acts (Art 82 says Parliament enacts law after every census).
Inter-State Water Dispute Tribunals
Inter-State River Water Disputes Act, 1956 (Art 262 gave power).
Disqualification for 2+ yrs jail
Representation of the People Act, 1951.
Registration of Political Parties
Representation of the People Act, 1951.
Acquiring/Losing Citizenship
Citizenship Act, 1955.
Leader of the Opposition (LoP)
Salary and Allowances of Leaders of Opposition in Parliament Act, 1977.
Exemptions for "Office of Profit"
Parliament (Prevention of Disqualification) Act, 1959.
Punishment for Untouchability
Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955 (Art 17 only abolishes practice).
Rules for National Flag/Anthem
Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971.

🏛️ 2. Purely House Rules

Lives entirely in the Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha rulebooks.

No-Confidence Motion
Rule 198 of the Lok Sabha Rules of Procedure.
Question Hour
Rules of Procedure of the Houses.
The "Guillotine"
Rules of Procedure (Putting undiscussed demands to vote on last day).
Privilege Motion
Rules of Procedure (Though privileges are in Art 105).
Parliamentary Committees
Rules of Procedure of the Houses (PAC, Estimates, etc.).

🤝 3. Unwritten Conventions

Traditions established over decades.

PAC Chairman from Opposition
Parliamentary Convention (strictly since 1967).
Deputy Speaker from Opposition
Parliamentary Convention (post itself is in Art 93).
Zero Hour
Indian Parliamentary Convention (started 1962).
The "Whip"
Parliamentary Convention (inherited from UK).
Governor outside home state
Political Convention (Sarkaria Comm. recommendation).
Resignation on Money Bill defeat
Parliamentary Convention (Signals loss of majority).
President inviting largest party
Presidential Convention during hung assembly.

⚖️ 4. Supreme Court Inventions

Doctrines created purely by judicial interpretation.

Basic Structure Doctrine
Kesavananda Bharati Judgment (1973).
Collegium System
Second and Third Judges Cases (1993, 1998).
Curative Petition
Rupa Ashok Hurra Case (2002) - Remedy after review petition.
NOTA (None of the Above)
PUCL Case (2013).
"Creamy Layer" in OBCs
Indra Sawhney Case (1992).

✒️ 5. Purely Executive Orders

Zero Constitutional or Statutory backing.

NITI Aayog
Cabinet Resolution (2015).
National Development Council
Cabinet Resolution (1952).
Law Commission of India
Government Order (reconstituted every 3 years).
Central Bureau of Investigation
MHA Resolution (1963) (Powers drawn from DSPE Act, 1946).

🚨 6. The "Gotcha" Facts

Mentioned vs Defined vs Never Used. The biggest traps.

Vice President's Salary
No salary fixed in Constitution. Drawn as ex-officio Chairman RS.
"Office of Profit"
Mentioned as disqualification ground (Art 102/191), NEVER defined.
"Contempt of Court"
SC/HC power given (Art 129/215), NEVER defined in Const. (Defined in Act of 1971).
Number of Election Commissioners
Not specified. Left to the President (Art 324).
"Budget"
Word NEVER used. It is "Annual Financial Statement" (Art 112).
"Federation"
Word NEVER used. India is a "Union of States" (Art 1).
"Basic Structure"
Phrase NEVER used in the Constitution.
"Cabinet"
Not in original text. Added to Art 352 via 44th Amendment (1978).
"Minority"
Used in Arts 29/30, NEVER defined.
"Untouchability"
Abolished in Art 17, NEVER defined.
"Martial Law"
Mentioned in Art 34, NEVER defined.

🏛️ 7. What the Constitution Left to Parliament

Matters where the Constitution explicitly delegated law-making to Parliament.

States & Territories (Arts 2 & 3)
Admission, formation, or altering boundaries. 👉 Various Reorganisation Acts (e.g., States Reorganisation Act, 1956)
Citizenship (Art 11)
Complete regulation after Jan 26, 1950. 👉 The Citizenship Act, 1955
Preventive Detention (Art 22(7))
Maximum period & procedures. 👉 National Security Act, 1980 (NSA); COFEPOSA, 1974
Armed Forces Rights (Art 33)
Restricting fundamental rights for discipline. 👉 Army Act, 1950; Navy Act, 1957
Fundamental Right Penalties (Art 35)
Criminalizing untouchability & trafficking. 👉 Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955
Executive Emoluments (Arts 59(3) & 158(3))
Salaries of President and Governors. 👉 President's Emoluments and Pension Act, 1951
Presidential Elections (Art 71(3))
Regulating election matters. 👉 Presidential & Vice-Presidential Elections Act, 1952
Ministers' Salaries (Arts 75(6) & 164(5))
Union and State Ministers' pay. 👉 Salaries and Allowances of Ministers Act, 1952
Delimitation (Arts 82 & 170(3))
Readjusting constituencies after census. 👉 Delimitation Act, 2002
Rajya Sabha Tenure (Art 83)
Duration and retirement rotation. 👉 Representation of the People Act, 1951
Legislators' Qualifications (Arts 84(c) & 191(1)(e))
Additional criteria for MPs/MLAs. 👉 Representation of the People Act, 1951
Presiding Officers' Salaries (Arts 97 & 186)
Pay for Speaker, Chairman, etc. 👉 Salaries and Allowances of Officers of Parliament Act, 1953
Legislative Privileges (Arts 105 & 194)
Powers and immunities. 👉 (Governed largely by rules, no comprehensive Act)
Legislators' Salaries (Arts 106 & 195)
Salaries of MPs and MLAs. 👉 Salary, Allowances and Pension of Members of Parliament Act, 1954
Official Language (Arts 120 & 343)
Continuation of English for Union. 👉 Official Languages Act, 1963
Supreme Court Strength (Art 124(1))
Increasing SC judges beyond original 7. 👉 Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Act, 1956
Judges' Service Conditions (Arts 125 & 221)
Privileges/leave for SC & HC judges. 👉 Supreme Court Judges Act, 1958
Supreme Court Jurisdiction (Arts 135 & 138)
Enlarging appellate powers. 👉 Supreme Court (Enlargement of Criminal Appellate Jurisdiction) Act, 1970
CAG Duties (Art 149)
Powers and service conditions of CAG. 👉 CAG's Duties, Powers and Conditions of Service Act, 1971
High Courts for UTs (Art 241)
Constitution of UT High Courts. 👉 Delhi High Court Act, 1966
Inter-State Water Disputes (Art 262)
Adjudication of river disputes. 👉 Inter-State River Water Disputes Act, 1956
Consolidated Funds (Art 283)
Custody/withdrawal of money. 👉 Contingency Fund of India Act, 1950
Trade & Commerce (Art 302)
Restrictions in public interest. 👉 Essential Commodities Act, 1955
All India Services (Art 312)
Creation and regulation of recruitment. 👉 All India Services Act, 1951
Election Commission (Art 324(2))
Number and tenure of Commissioners. 👉 CEC and other ECs Act, 2023
Elections to Legislatures (Art 327)
Electoral rolls and delimitation. 👉 Representation of the People Acts (1950 & 1951)
SC/ST Lists (Arts 341(2) & 342(2))
Inclusion or exclusion from lists. 👉 Various Constitution Order (Amendment) Acts
