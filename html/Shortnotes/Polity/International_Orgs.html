NDB (BRICS Bank)

HQ: Shanghai, China

Focus: 1 equal vote per founding member; NO veto power. Lends in local currencies. 1st Prez: K.V. Kamath.

Finance

AIIB

HQ: Beijing, China

Focus: Voting based on GDP (China #1, India #2). Trap: USA & Japan are NOT members. India is the largest beneficiary.

Finance

World Bank Group

HQ: Washington, D.C.

Focus: India is in IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA but NOT ICSID. IDA = "soft loan window" (zero-interest).

Finance

IMF

HQ: Washington, D.C.

Focus: SDR is a reserve asset, not currency. Basket: $, €, RMB, ¥, £. India quota: ~2.75%.

WTO

HQ: Geneva, Switzerland

Focus: Agreement on Agriculture (AoA). Green/Blue/Amber boxes. India fights for "Peace Clause" (MSP).
