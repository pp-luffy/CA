Votes

🗳️ Parliamentary Majorities

  • Simple Majority: >50% present & voting. (Ordinary bills, Money bills, No-Confidence, President's Rule).
  • Absolute Majority: >50% of Total Membership. (Form government).
  • Effective Majority: >50% of Effective Strength [Total - Vacancies]. (Removal of VP in RS, Speaker, Dy Speaker).
  • Special Maj (Art 368): 2/3rd present & voting + Absolute Maj. (Const. Amend, SC/HC judge removal).
  • Special Maj (Art 61): 2/3rd of TOTAL Membership. (Impeaching President ONLY).
Funds

💰 Government Funds

  • Consolidated (Art 266): All revenues/loans. Parl approval mandatory to withdraw a single rupee.
  • Public Account (Art 266): PF, judicial deposits, savings. Executive action (No parl approval needed).
  • Contingency (Art 267): ₹30,000 cr for emergencies. Held by Fin Sec for the Pres. Needs post-facto parl approval.
Sessions

⏳ Veto Powers & Sessions

  • Adjournment: Suspends sitting (Done by Presiding Officer).
  • Prorogation: Ends the session (Done by Pres/Gov).
  • Dissolution: Ends LS/Assembly life (Done by Pres/Gov).
  • Veto Exceptions:
    • President has no pocket veto for Const. Amendments.
    • Governor cannot pocket veto a bill; must act ASAP or reserve for President (Art 200).

Committees

🏛️ DRSCs & "Gotcha" Committees

Departmentally Related Standing Committees (DRSCs)

  • Total: 24 Committees (16 under LS Speaker, 8 under RS Chairman).
  • Composition: 31 members each (21 from LS + 10 from RS).
  • Leadership: Appointed by presiding officer of the respective House.
  • Reporting: Submit reports to both Houses simultaneously.
  • Task: Heavy lifting—examine bills, demands for grants, annual reports.

Presiding Officer Chaired (Ex-Officio)

  • Business Advisory: Regulates timetable. (LS: 15, RS: 11)
  • Rules Committee: Procedure/conduct matters. (LS: 15, RS: 16)
  • General Purposes: Affairs not covered elsewhere. (Presiding officers, panel, party leaders)

✅ Compulsory (Mandatory):

  • Establishing Gram Sabha
  • 3-tier system (< 20L pop can skip middle tier)
  • Direct elections at all levels
  • Min Age: 21 years
  • Reservation: SC/ST (proportionate) & Women (Min 33% or 1/3rd)

  • Reservation for OBCs
  • Financial powers to levy taxes, tolls, fees

10th Sch

⚖️ Anti-Defection Law "Gotchas"

  • The Nominated Member Trap: Disqualified if they join a party AFTER 6 months of taking seat. (They are safe if they join within first 6 months).
  • The Independent Member Trap: Disqualified if they join ANY political party at ANY time after election.
  • The Merger Exception: Disqualification bypassed if 2/3rd of legislative members agree to merge with another party.
  • Deciding Authority: The Presiding Officer (Speaker/Chairman).
    🔥 Kihoto Hollohan case (1992): Decision is NOT immune; it is subject to Judicial Review!
