🗳️ Parliamentary Majorities

  • Simple Majority: >50% present & voting.
  • Absolute Majority: >50% of Total Membership.
  • Effective Majority: >50% of Effective Strength. (VP in RS, Speaker)
  • Special Maj (Art 368): 2/3rd present & voting + Absolute Maj.
  • Special Maj (Art 61): 2/3rd of TOTAL Membership. (Impeaching President ONLY)

🚨 5. The "Gotcha" Facts

Mentioned vs Defined vs Never Used. The biggest traps.

Vice President's Salary
No salary fixed in Constitution. Drawn as ex-officio Chairman RS.
"Office of Profit" & "Untouchability"
Mentioned as disqualification/abolished, NEVER defined in Const.
"Contempt of Court"
SC/HC power given (Art 129/215), NEVER defined in Const.
"Budget" & "Federation"
Words NEVER used. It is "Annual Financial Statement" & "Union of States".
"Cabinet"
Not in original text. Added to Art 352 via 44th Amendment (1978).

🏛️ 6. Left to Parliament

Explicitly delegated law-making.

Citizenship (Art 11)
Complete regulation after Jan 26, 1950.
Armed Forces Rights (Art 33)
Restricting fundamental rights for discipline.
SC/ST Lists (Arts 341(2) & 342(2))
Inclusion or exclusion from lists.
