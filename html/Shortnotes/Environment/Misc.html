🔄 Biogeochemical Cycles & Indian Forest Types

  • Gaseous Cycles: Carbon, Nitrogen, Water (reservoir = atmosphere/hydrosphere; fast).
  • Sedimentary Cycles: Phosphorus, Sulphur (reservoir = Earth's crust; slow).
    ⚠️ Trap: Phosphorus cycle has NO atmospheric/gaseous phase.
  • Nitrogen Cycle Bacteria:
    • Fixation: Rhizobium (legumes), Azotobacter, Anabaena
    • Nitrification: Nitrosomonas (NH₃→NO₂⁻), Nitrobacter (NO₂⁻→NO₃⁻)
    • Denitrification: Pseudomonas (NO₃⁻→N₂ gas)
  • 5 Indian Forest Types:
    1. Tropical Deciduous (Monsoon): Largest area in India (Sal, Teak, Sandalwood).
    2. Tropical Evergreen: Western Ghats, NE India, A&N (>200 cm rain).
    3. Tropical Thorn: Semi-arid Rajasthan, Gujarat (Acacia, Cactus).
    4. Montane: Himalayas (Pine, Deodar, Alpine).
    5. Littoral & Swamp: Mangroves (Sundarbans, Bhitarkanika — Sundari).
