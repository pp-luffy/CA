🇮🇳 Presidents of India Timeline

⚡ Quick Revision Cheat-Sheet

Dr. Rajendra Prasad: 1st Prez, Only one to serve 2 full terms.
Dr. Zakir Husain: 1st Muslim Prez, 1st to die in office.
V.V. Giri: Only person to be both Acting & Elected Prez.
Justice M. Hidayatullah: Chief Justice who stepped in as Acting Prez.
Fakhruddin Ali Ahmed: Emergency (1975), 2nd to die in office.
Neelam Sanjiva Reddy: Elected unopposed.
Giani Zail Singh: 1st Sikh Prez.
K. R. Narayanan: 1st Dalit Prez.
Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam: "Missile Man", "People's President".
Pratibha Patil: 1st Woman Prez.
Droupadi Murmu: 1st Tribal Woman, Youngest Prez.
1950 - 1962

Dr. Rajendra Prasad

The first President of independent India and the only one to serve two full terms. He played a central role in the Constituent Assembly and the freedom struggle.
First President 2 Full Terms
1962 - 1967

Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan

A renowned scholar and philosopher. He was the first Vice President to later be elected to the presidency.
1st VP to President Scholar/Philosopher
1967 - 1969

Dr. Zakir Husain

The first Muslim President of India and a noted educationist. He was the first President to pass away while in office.
First Muslim Died in Office
May 1969 - Aug 1969

Acting: V.V. Giri & Justice M. Hidayatullah

After Dr. Husain's death, Vice President V.V. Giri served as Acting President but resigned shortly after to contest the upcoming election. Justice Mohammad Hidayatullah, the sitting Chief Justice of India, then stepped in as Acting President for just over a month until the elections concluded.
Acting President Chief Justice
1969 - 1974

Varahagiri Venkata Giri (V.V. Giri)

The only person in Indian history to have served as both an Acting President and a fully elected President.
Acting + Elected
1974 - 1977

Fakhruddin Ali Ahmed

His tenure was marked by the imposition of the Emergency in 1975. He was the second Indian President to die during his term in office.
1975 Emergency Died in Office
Feb 1977 - Jul 1977

Acting: Basappa Danappa Jatti

Following Ahmed's sudden death, B.D. Jatti, the Vice President and former Chief Minister of Mysore, served as Acting President.
Acting President
1977 - 1982

Neelam Sanjiva Reddy

He holds the distinction of being elected unopposed. At 64, he was the youngest President at the time.
Unopposed Youngest (at the time)
1982 - 1987

Giani Zail Singh

The first Sikh President of India. Before his presidency, he had served as the Chief Minister of Punjab and the Union Home Minister.
First Sikh Former Home Minister
1987 - 1992

Ramaswamy Venkataraman

A lawyer and freedom fighter who was jailed during the independence movement in 1942. He held key ministerial positions before his presidency.
Freedom Fighter
1992 - 1997

Dr. Shankar Dayal Sharma

An academician and lawyer from Madhya Pradesh who previously served as Vice President. His tenure focused strongly on constitutional values and the rule of law.
Academician
1997 - 2002

K. R. Narayanan

The first Dalit President of India. Before taking office, he was a distinguished career diplomat who advocated for social justice.
First Dalit Career Diplomat
2002 - 2007

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Popularly known as the "Missile Man of India" and the "People's President". He was a pioneering aerospace scientist who led crucial developments at ISRO and DRDO.
Missile Man ISRO / DRDO
2007 - 2012

Pratibha Devisingh Patil

The first woman to hold the office of the President of India.
First Woman
2012 - 2017

Pranab Mukherjee

A veteran political leader who managed several heavyweight portfolios including Finance, Defence, and External Affairs before his elevation to the presidency.
Veteran Politician
2017 - 2022

Ram Nath Kovind

The second Dalit leader to become President. He previously served as the Governor of Bihar.
Former Governor
2022 - Present

Droupadi Murmu

The current President. She is the first tribal woman to hold the highest constitutional office, and she also became the youngest person to assume the presidency.
First Tribal Woman Youngest President
