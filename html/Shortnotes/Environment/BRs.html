🗺️ Spatial Memory Map — 6 Regional Clusters (All 18 Reserves)

1. The Himalayas (2)

  • Cold Desert — Himachal Pradesh (Lahaul-Spiti valley, includes Pin Valley NP) UNESCO 2025
  • Nanda Devi — Uttarakhand (named after Himalayan peak) UNESCO 2004

Hook: Only HP & UK — there is NO biosphere reserve in J&K or Ladakh.

2. The Northeast (5) — "S-A-A-A-M"

  • Khangchendzonga — Sikkim (mountain) UNESCO 2018
  • Dihang-Dibang — Arunachal Pradesh (2 Brahmaputra tributaries) National
  • Manas — Assam (Brahmaputra valley) National
  • Dibru-Saikhowa — Assam (Dibru = Dibrugarh; smallest ~765 sq km) National
  • Nokrek — Meghalaya (highest peak of Garo Hills) UNESCO 2009

3. Central India — MP Belt (3) — "P-P-A"

  • Panna — Madhya Pradesh (diamonds & tigers) UNESCO 2020
  • Pachmarhi — Madhya Pradesh (MP's only hill station, Satpuras) UNESCO 2009
  • Achanakmar-Amarkantak — MP + Chhattisgarh (Amarkantak = MP, Narmada origin; Achanakmar = CG) UNESCO 2012

4. West & East Coasts (3)

  • Great Rann of Kutch (Kachchh) — Gujarat (far West; largest ~12,454 sq km) National
  • Sundarbans — West Bengal (East; Ganga-Brahmaputra delta) UNESCO 2001
  • Simlipal — Odisha (East; Mayurbhanj) UNESCO 2009

5. South India (4) — North to South

  • Seshachalam Hills — Andhra Pradesh (Tirupati temple) National
  • Nilgiri — Karnataka + Kerala + Tamil Nadu (tri-junction; first BR 1986) UNESCO 2000
  • Agasthyamalai — Kerala + Tamil Nadu (further south in Western Ghats) UNESCO 2016
  • Gulf of Mannar — Tamil Nadu (marine reserve facing Sri Lanka) UNESCO 2001

6. The Islands (1) & Classic Exam Traps

  • Great Nicobar — Andaman & Nicobar Islands (southernmost tip of India) UNESCO 2013

🏛️ 3-Zone Architecture & Protected Area Hierarchy

Concentric 3-Zone Structure

Transition / Manipulation Zone
Communities live, farm, sustainable use
Buffer Zone
Research, education, eco-tourism
CORE ZONE
Zero interference • NPs & WLS sit here
  • Umbrella Concept: Largest in geographical size & scope; encloses National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries & Tiger Reserves.
  • Example (Nilgiri BR): Encompasses Bandipur NP, Nagarhole NP, Mudumalai NP, Silent Valley NP, and Wayanad WLS.

BR vs National Park vs Wildlife Sanctuary

  • Biosphere Reserve:
    • Scale: Largest (landscape-level, includes human settlements).
    • Legal Backing: Notified by Central Govt (MoEFCC); NOT under WPA 1972.
    • Human Activity: Allowed in Transition Zone; restricted in Core.
  • National Park (Strictest Legal Protection):
    • Scale: Medium to large (ecosystem-level).
    • Legal Backing: Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972 (Section 35).
    • Human Activity: Strictly prohibited (no grazing or private rights).
  • Wildlife Sanctuary:
    • Scale: Small to large (often species-focused).
    • Legal Backing: Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972 (Section 18 / 26A).
    • Human Activity: Limited activity/grazing may be permitted by the Warden.
