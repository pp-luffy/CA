1. Global Financial & Trade Institutions

Finance

NDB (BRICS Bank)

HQ: Shanghai, China

Focus: 1 equal vote per founding member; NO veto power. Lends in local currencies. 1st Prez: K.V. Kamath.

Finance

AIIB

HQ: Beijing, China

Focus: Voting based on GDP (China #1, India #2). Trap: USA & Japan are NOT members. India is the largest beneficiary.

Finance

World Bank Group

HQ: Washington, D.C.

Focus: India is in IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA but NOT ICSID. IDA = "soft loan window" (zero-interest loans to poorest nations).

Finance

IMF

HQ: Washington, D.C.

Focus: SDR is a reserve asset, not currency. Basket: USD, Euro, RMB, Yen, Pound. India quota dictates voting: ~2.75%.

Finance

ADB

HQ: Manila, Philippines

Focus: Proportional voting. USA & Japan largest voters. India is largest borrowing member.

Trade

WTO

HQ: Geneva, Switzerland

Focus: Agreement on Agriculture (AoA). Green/Blue/Amber boxes. India fights for "Peace Clause" (MSP).

2. Strategic, Security & Export Control Regimes

Security

UN Security Council (UNSC)

HQ: New York, USA

Focus: 15 members (5 perm, 10 non-perm). G4 (Ind, Jap, Ger, Bra) push for reform vs. "Coffee Club" (Italy, Pak).

Security

FATF

HQ: Paris, France

Focus: Created by G7. Grey/Black Lists. Grey-listing restricts IMF/WB funding (Terror financing / Money laundering).

Security

SCO

HQ: Beijing, China

Focus: Ind & Pak joined 2017. Key organ: RATS (Tashkent) for anti-terror intelligence sharing.

Defense

NATO

HQ: Brussels, Belgium

Focus: 32 members. Article 5 (Collective defense). Recent highly tested additions: Finland & Sweden.

Export Control

Export Regimes

MTCR: India IN, China OUT (Missiles: 300km/500kg limit).

Wassenaar: India IN, China OUT (Conventional arms).

Australia Group: India IN, China OUT (Chem/Bio weapons).

NSG: India OUT (Blocked by China over NPT).
