8. Omega Block & European Heatwave

  • Europe heatwave >42°C (France) caused by an Omega Block (jet stream bends into Greek 'Omega' shape Ω).
  • High-pressure system traps hot air forming a heat dome. Causes heatwaves in one area, floods in another.
  • Climate change makes blocks 2-4°C hotter. Arctic warming weakens jet stream.
