Biofortified Coloured Potatoes (ICAR-CPRI) 🥔
  • Developer ICAR and Central Potato Research Institute (CPRI) developed nutrient-rich coloured potato varieties to combat Hidden Hunger in India.
  • 3 Key Varieties Kufri Neelkanth (2018, purple-skinned, antioxidant-rich), Kufri Manik (2021, red potato, rich in anthocyanins, Zinc & Iron), and Kufri Jamunia (newly developed purple-fleshed variety).
  • Kufri Jamunia Specs Packed with Vitamin C, Anthocyanins, Carotenoids, Iron, and Zinc; yields 32–35 tonnes/hectare; recommended for Northern Indian Plains.

Core Concepts: Hidden Hunger & Biofortification

Hidden Hunger: A chronic deficiency of essential micronutrients (vitamins, minerals like Fe/Zn, and protein) even when a person consumes sufficient calories/staple food (e.g., adequate rice or chapatis).
Biofortification: Improving the nutritional content of food crops through conventional plant breeding, agronomic practices, or biotechnology. Anthocyanin is the natural water-soluble pigment responsible for the bluish/purplish colour and high antioxidant properties.

Revision Hooks

  • Mains Links directly to GS-II (Malnutrition/POSHAN Abhiyaan) & GS-III (Crop Diversification, Farmer Income Augmentation, Post-Harvest Processing).
  • Agronomy High storage quality (low rotting) and suitable for both traditional dishes (e.g., aloo paratha) and processed foods.
  • Trap Alert: Do not confuse Kufri Neelkanth (purple-skinned, 2018) with Kufri Jamunia (purple-fleshed, rich in carotenoids + Vit C + Fe + Zn).

Key Foreign Trade Instruments Monitored

The NSTCC rests on three pillars — Integrated Monitoring, Performance Assessment (of Export Promotion Schemes & DGFT regional offices), and Evidence-Based Policy Input. It tracks five critical trade mechanisms:

  • IEC (Importer-Exporter Code): Mandatory unique identification code for businesses importing/exporting goods.
  • Advance Authorization: Permits duty-free import of raw materials/inputs that are physically incorporated into export products.
  • EPCG (Export Promotion Capital Goods): Allows import of machinery/capital goods at zero customs duty for export production.
  • Certificate of Origin (CoO): Certifies the country of manufacture to claim tariff concessions under Free Trade Agreements (FTAs).
  • SCOMET: Special Chemicals, Organisms, Materials, Equipment and Technologies — strict export control regime for dual-use items (both civilian and military applications).
  • Mains Future expansion includes automated bottleneck alerts and linking State Governments & Export Promotion Councils (EPCs).

NSAP Revamped: PM Samajik Sahayata Yojana 🤝
  • Rebranding & Tenure National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP) (launched 15 Aug 1995) renamed as Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana (PMSSY) and extended for 5 years (2026–27 to 2030–31).
  • Nodal Ministry & Funding Administered by Ministry of Rural Development (MoRD) with 100% Central Assistance; covers 3.20 crore beneficiaries across all 36 States/UTs.
  • Big Disability Reform Disability eligibility threshold slashed from 80% to 40%, minimum age made eligible from birth, and monthly pension raised to ₹375 (below 80 yrs) and ₹625 (80+ yrs).

4 Renamed Sub-Schemes & Constitutional Backing

The revamped umbrella has four components: (1) PM Vriddhavastha Pension Yojana (elderly), (2) PM Vidhwa Pension Yojana (widows), (3) PM Divyangjan Pension Yojana (PwDs), and (4) PM Parivar Labh Yojana (one-time lump-sum grant on death of primary breadwinner).

Revision Hooks

  • Constitution Directly operationalizes Article 41 (DPSP) (Right to public assistance in old age, sickness, and disablement) and harmonizes with the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act, 2016 (which defines benchmark disability at 40%).
  • Trap Alert (HIGH PROBABILITY): Pension amounts under Old Age Pension, Widow Pension, and Family Benefit Scheme remain unchanged — ONLY the Divyangjan (Disability) pension rates and eligibility criteria were revised!

Ramsar Convention (1971) & Ecological Value

Wetlands designated under the Ramsar Convention (1971, Iran) are recognized as wetlands of international importance for conserving biological diversity, migratory waterbirds, hydrology, and sustainable livelihoods.

Revision Hooks

  • Map Work Pinpoint Sukhna Lake/Wetland (Shivalik foothills, Chandigarh) and Sauj Jheel (Mainpuri, Upper Ganga-Yamuna Doab region of UP).
  • Mains Supports water security, flood buffering, carbon sequestration, climate resilience, and sustainable wetland tourism (Amrit Dharohar initiative).
  • Trap Alert: Sukhna Wetland is in Chandigarh, while Sauj Jheel is in Uttar Pradesh (do not mark both in Chandigarh!).

Towards a TB-Free India: JOSH & BPaLM 🫁
  • 2015–2024 Progress India's TB incidence fell by 21% (vs. 12% global average), TB deaths dropped by 28%, and treatment coverage reached 92% (vs. 78% globally).
  • JOSH Campaign Joint Squad for Health (JOSH) launched on 13th October 2026 across 11 States and 55 Districts to mobilize youth for TB awareness and early detection.
  • BPaLM Regimen Shorter 6-month treatment course for Drug-Resistant TB (MDR-TB) comprising Bedaquiline, Pretomanid, Linezolid, and Moxifloxacin.

Programmatic & Technological Ecosystem

Causative Pathogen: Mycobacterium tuberculosis (an airborne bacterium primarily affecting lungs; symptoms include prolonged cough, fever, night sweats, and weight loss).
NTEP (National TB Elimination Programme): Renamed from RNTCP in 2020; operates on 4 pillars: Detect – Treat – Prevent – Build.

Revision Hooks

  • PM TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyan Launched 9 Sept 2022; screened 28.1+ crore people, gave preventive therapy to 20 lakh people, and certified 46,018 Gram Panchayats TB-free.
  • Nikshay Poshan & Tech Provides ₹1,000/month nutritional support to notified TB patients; deploys AI-enabled portable X-rays, TB Mukt Bharat App, and the "Khushi" AI Chatbot to achieve UN SDG 3.
