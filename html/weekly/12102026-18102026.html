Topic 01

ICAR & Central Potato Research Institute (CPRI): Nutrient-Rich Colored Potatoes

Core Objective: Developed to address hidden hunger in India through biofortification.

Key Varieties & Characteristics:

  • Kufri Neel (2018): Purple-skinned potato rich in antioxidants. ("Neel" = bluish).
  • Kufri Manik (2021): Red potato rich in anthocyanins, zinc, and iron.
  • Kufri Jamuni: Purple-fleshed potato with Vitamin C, zinc, iron, carotenoids, and anthocyanins (natural pigment providing color). Average yield: 32-35 tons/hectare. Recommended for Northern Indian plains.

Significance: Enhances nutritional security, dietary diversity, and increases farmer income opportunities while maintaining good storage quality (low rotting) suitable for traditional and processed foods (e.g., Aloo Paratha).

⚠️ Prelims Trap: Biofortification is achieved through plant breeding, NOT genetic modification (GMO). Anthocyanin is a natural pigment, not an artificial additive.
Topic 02

Mahila Kisan Diwas 2026 (15th October 2026)

Organized By: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.

Venue: Dr. Ambedkar International Center, New Delhi.

Theme: "Role of women farmers in building developed agriculture and a developed India".

Major Highlights & Focus Points:

  • Honoring outstanding women farmers for achievements in crop production, animal husbandry, and seed saving.
  • Release of book titled "Women Farmers of India: Journey from Challenges to Success".
  • Special emphasis on empowering women farmers from tribal regions.
  • Key focus on affordable credit, technology, skill development, farm mechanization, and market access.
✍️ Mains Analytical Hook: Women constitute over 40% of agricultural labor in India ("feminization of agriculture"), yet own less land titles. Empowering women with credit and mechanization is vital for Viksit Bharat 2047.
Topic 03

National Strategic Trade Command Center (NSTCC)

Launched By: Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry in New Delhi.

What is it? A digital monitoring center tracking India's imports, exports, and international trade activities in one place using interactive dashboards.

Key Integrations & Areas Covered:

  • Integrated with DGFT (Directorate General of Foreign Trade) and Trade Connect E-Platform.
  • IEC: Importer-Exporter Code (unique ID for trade).
  • Advanced Authorization & EPCG: Duty-free import of raw materials/machineries for export production.
  • SCOMET: Special Chemicals, Organisms, Materials, Equipment, and Technologies (sensitive dual-use items).

Significance: Enables evidence-based policymaking, faster trade decisions, reduced delays, greater transparency, and enhanced export competitiveness.

Topic 04

PM Gati Shakti National Master Plan (Completed 5 Years)

Launched: October 2021 by PM. Developed by BISAG-N (Bhaskaracharya National Institute for Space Applications and Geo-informatics) using geospatial technology.

Objective: Integrate infrastructure planning, multimodal connectivity (roads, railways, ports, airports), and reduce logistics costs.

Major Achievements & Features:

  • Integrated over 1,800 data layers (railways, forests, highways, industrial zones).
  • Covers 58 central ministries/departments and all 36 States/UTs.
  • Network Planning Group evaluated 421 infrastructure projects worth ₹20.22 Lakh Crores.
  • PM Gati Shakti Public Platform launched via UGIF (Unified Geospatial Interface).

Significance: Prevents duplication of work across departments, eliminates project delays, and lowers logistics costs.

Topic 05

National Social Assistance Program (Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana)

Nodal Ministry: Ministry of Rural Development. Funding: 100% Central Assistance.

Constitutional Link: Article 41 of DPSP (Right to work, to education, and to public assistance in certain cases).

Key Reforms & Sub-Schemes:

  • Renamed to Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana.
  • Sub-schemes: Old Age Pension, Widow Pension (Vidhwa), Disability Pension (Divyangjan), and Family Benefit (Parivar Labh).
  • Disability Eligibility Reduced: From 80% down to 40%. Minimum age for disability pension is now from birth.
  • Disability pension rates: ₹375/month (<80 yrs) and ₹625/month (>80 yrs).
Topic 06

India's Ramsar Sites Reaches 103 Wetlands

Recent Additions: Two new wetlands added, taking India's total Ramsar count to 103 sites.

1. Sukna Wetland: Located in Chandigarh.

2. Saw Jheel: Located in Mainpuri district, Uttar Pradesh.

Background: Ramsar Convention was signed in 1971 (Iran) for the conservation and sustainable use of wetlands, protecting biodiversity, water security, and climate resilience.

Topic 07

International Big Cat Alliance (IBCA)

Headquarters: 55 Lodi/Lii Estate, New Delhi, India. Treaty-based intergovernmental organization.

The 7 Big Cats: Lion, Tiger, Leopard, Snow Leopard, Cheetah, Jaguar, and Puma.

Key Highlights:

  • India's financial contribution: ₹150 Crores for 5 years (2023-24 to 2027-28).
  • Membership: 29 countries + 5 observer states + 11 prospective countries.
  • PRIME Program: Big cat health training for veterinary doctors across 20 countries.
  • Integrated with CITES and UNFCCC frameworks.
Topic 08

Towards a TB-Free India (Tuberculosis Elimination)

Causative Organism: Mycobacterium tuberculosis (Bacterial disease affecting lungs and other body parts).

Key Stats: India's TB incidence declined by 21% (2015-2024) (Global decline: 12%). Treatment coverage reached 92% (Global average: 78%).

Innovations & Schemes:

  • BPaL Regimen: Shorter 6-month treatment for drug-resistant TB (Bedaquiline, Pretomanid, Linezolid, Moxifloxacin).
  • Ni-kshay Poshan Yojana: ₹1,000/month nutritional support for notified TB patients.
  • JoSH Campaign: Joint Squad for Health launched to mobilize youth for TB awareness.
  • Tech Support: AI-enabled portable X-rays, TB Mukt Bharat app, and AI chatbot 'Kushi'.

SDG Alignment: Directly supports Sustainable Development Goal 3 (Good Health and Well-being).
