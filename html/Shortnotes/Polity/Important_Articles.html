Part I: Union & Territory

  • Art 1: Name & territory ("India, that is Bharat").
  • Art 3: Formation of new states & alteration of boundaries/names.

Part III: Fundamental Rights

  • Art 14: Equality before the law.
  • Art 15: Prohibition of discrimination (religion, race, caste, sex, birth place).
  • Art 16: Equality of opportunity in public employment.
  • Art 17: Abolition of Untouchability.
  • Art 19: 6 Freedoms (Speech, assembly, association, movement, residence, profession).
  • Art 21: Protection of life & personal liberty.
  • Art 21A: Right to Education (6-14 yrs).
  • Art 22: Protection against arrest & preventive detention.
  • Art 23: Prohibition of human trafficking & forced labor.
  • Art 24: Prohibition of child labor in factories.
  • Art 25: Freedom of conscience & practice of religion.
  • Art 29 & 30: Protection of minorities (Cultural, educational rights, minority institutions).
  • Art 32: Right to Constitutional Remedies (SC Writs).

Part IV & IVA: DPSP & Duties

  • Art 39A: Equal justice & free legal aid.
  • Art 40: Organization of Village Panchayats.
  • Art 44: Uniform Civil Code.
  • Art 45: Early childhood care & education (< 6 yrs).
  • Art 48A: Protection of environment, forests, wildlife.
  • Art 50: Separation of judiciary from executive.
  • Art 51: Promote international peace & security.
  • Art 51A: Fundamental Duties (11 duties).

Part V: Union Executive & Parl

  • Art 52: President of India.
  • Art 61: Procedure for President's impeachment.
  • Art 63: Vice-President of India.
  • Art 72: Pardoning powers of President.
  • Art 74: Council of Ministers to advise President.
  • Art 76: Attorney General of India.
  • Art 108: Joint sitting of both Houses.
  • Art 110: Definition of "Money Bills".
  • Art 112: Annual Financial Statement (Budget).
  • Art 123: Prez power to promulgate Ordinances.
  • Art 124: Establishment of Supreme Court.
  • Art 143: Prez power to consult Supreme Court.
  • Art 148: CAG of India.

Part VI: State Exec & HC

  • Art 153: Governors of States.
  • Art 161: Pardoning powers of Governor.
  • Art 165: Advocate General for State.
  • Art 213: Gov's power to promulgate Ordinances.
  • Art 214: High Courts for States.
  • Art 226: Power of High Courts to issue Writs.

UTs, Center-State, Finance

  • Art 239AA: Special provisions for Delhi (NCT).
  • Art 249: Parl's power to legislate State List in national interest.
  • Art 262: Inter-state river water disputes.
  • Art 263: Inter-State Council.
  • Art 279A: GST Council.
  • Art 280: Finance Commission.
  • Art 300A: Right to Property (Legal right).

Services, Elections & SC/ST

  • Art 312: Creation of All-India Services.
  • Art 315: Public Service Commissions (UPSC/SPSC).
  • Art 324: Election Commission of India.
  • Art 330 & 332: Reservation for SC/ST in Lok Sabha & State Assemblies.
  • Art 338: NC for Scheduled Castes (SCs).
  • Art 338A: NC for Scheduled Tribes (STs).
  • Art 338B: NC for Backward Classes (NCBC).

Emergency, Temp, & Amend

  • Art 352: National Emergency (War, aggression, armed rebellion).
  • Art 356: State Emergency (President's Rule).
  • Art 360: Financial Emergency.
  • Art 370: Temp provisions for J&K (largely abrogated '19).
  • Art 368: Power of Parliament to amend the Constitution.
