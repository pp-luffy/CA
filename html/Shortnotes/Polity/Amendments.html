📜 Key Constitutional Amendments

⚡ Quick Revision Cheat-Sheet

1st (1951): 9th Schedule (Land Reforms).
7th (1956): State Reorg (14 States, 6 UTs).
24th (1971): Parl can amend FRs.
26th (1971): Abolished Privy Purses.
36th (1975): Sikkim = 22nd State.
42nd (1976): "Mini-Constitution" (Soc, Sec, Int), Fund. Duties.
44th (1978): Removed Right to Property from FRs.
52nd (1985): Anti-Defection (10th Schedule).
61st (1989): Voting age 21 → 18.
69th (1991): Delhi = NCT.
73rd & 74th (1992): Panchayats (11th Sch) & Municipalities (12th Sch).
86th (2002): Right to Education (Art 21A).
89th (2003): NC for SCs & STs separated.
91st (2003): CoM size capped at 15%.
92nd (2003): Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali added.
97th (2011): Co-operative Societies.
99th (2014): NJAC (Later struck down).
100th (2015): India-B'desh Land Boundary Agreement.
101st (2016): GST.
102nd (2018): NCBC gets constitutional status.
103rd (2019): 10% EWS Quota.
104th (2020): SC/ST quota extended, Anglo-Indian seats abolished.
105th (2021): States can identify own OBCs.
106th (2023): 33% Women's Reservation.
1951
J. Nehru (INC)

1st Amendment

Added the 9th Schedule to protect land reform laws from judicial review. Introduced reasonable restrictions on Freedom of Speech and Expression. Empowered the state to make special provisions for socially and educationally backward classes.
9th Schedule Speech Restrictions
1956
J. Nehru (INC)

7th Amendment

Implemented the States Reorganisation Commission's recommendations. Abolished the classification of states and reorganized India into 14 States and 6 Union Territories on linguistic lines.
State Reorganization
1971
Indira Gandhi (INC)

24th Amendment

Affirmed the Parliament's power to amend any part of the Constitution, including Fundamental Rights. Made it mandatory for the President to give assent to Constitutional Amendment Bills.
Amend Fundamental Rights
1971
Indira Gandhi (INC)

26th Amendment

Abolished the privy purses and privileges of the former rulers of princely states.
End of Privy Purses
1975
Indira Gandhi (INC)

36th Amendment

Made Sikkim a full-fledged State of the Indian Union (the 22nd state).
Sikkim added
1976
Indira Gandhi (INC)

42nd Amendment (Mini-Constitution)

Enacted during the Emergency. Added "Socialist, Secular, and Integrity" to the Preamble. Added Fundamental Duties (Article 51A). Made the President bound by Cabinet advice. Shifted five subjects (including Education) to the Concurrent List.
Emergency Era Mini-Constitution Fundamental Duties
1978
Morarji Desai (Janata)

44th Amendment

Reversed many excesses of the 42nd Amendment. Removed the Right to Property from Fundamental Rights (made it a legal right). Replaced "internal disturbance" with "armed rebellion" for National Emergency. Allowed the President to return Cabinet advice once.
Prop Right Removed Armed Rebellion
1985
Rajiv Gandhi (INC)

52nd Amendment

Introduced the Anti-Defection Law by adding the 10th Schedule to disqualify MPs and MLAs for defection.
Anti-Defection (10th Sch)
1989
Rajiv Gandhi (INC)

61st Amendment

Reduced the voting age for Lok Sabha and Legislative Assembly elections from 21 years to 18 years.
Voting Age 18
1991
P.V. Narasimha Rao (INC)

69th Amendment

Granted special status to Delhi, designating it as the National Capital Territory (NCT), and provided it with a legislative assembly and a council of ministers.
Delhi = NCT
1992
P.V. Narasimha Rao (INC)

73rd & 74th Amendments

73rd: Granted constitutional status to Panchayati Raj Institutions (Part IX, 11th Sch).
74th: Granted constitutional status to Municipalities (Part IXA, 12th Sch).
Panchayats (11th) Municipalities (12th)
2002
A.B. Vajpayee (NDA)

86th Amendment

Made the Right to Education a Fundamental Right (Article 21A) for children aged 6-14 years. Added a related Fundamental Duty.
Right to Education
2003
A.B. Vajpayee (NDA)

89th Amendment

Bifurcated the National Commission for SCs and STs into two separate bodies: NC for SCs and NC for STs.
NC SC/ST Bifurcated
2003
A.B. Vajpayee (NDA)

91st Amendment

Limited the size of the Council of Ministers at the Center and States to a maximum of 15% of the total strength of the lower house. Strengthened the Anti-Defection Law.
15% CoM Cap
2003
A.B. Vajpayee (NDA)

92nd Amendment

Added four new languages—Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, and Santhali—to the 8th Schedule.
4 Languages Added
2011
Manmohan Singh (UPA)

97th Amendment

Granted constitutional status and protection to Co-operative Societies.
Co-operatives
2014
Narendra Modi (NDA)

99th Amendment

Established the National Judicial Appointments Commission (NJAC) to replace the collegium system (Later struck down by the Supreme Court in 2015).
Struck Down NJAC
2015
Narendra Modi (NDA)

100th Amendment

Operationalized the Land Boundary Agreement (LBA) between India and Bangladesh for the exchange of enclaves.
LBA with Bangladesh
2016
Narendra Modi (NDA)

101st Amendment

Introduced the Goods and Services Tax (GST) and established the GST Council.
GST Introduced
2018
Narendra Modi (NDA)

102nd Amendment

Conferred constitutional status on the National Commission for Backward Classes (NCBC).
NCBC Status
2019
Narendra Modi (NDA)

103rd Amendment

Introduced a 10% reservation for Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) in government jobs and education.
10% EWS Quota
2020
Narendra Modi (NDA)

104th Amendment

Extended the reservation of seats for SCs and STs in the Lok Sabha and State Assemblies to 2030. Abolished reserved seats for the Anglo-Indian community.
SC/ST Quota Extended Anglo-Indian Seats Removed
2021
Narendra Modi (NDA)

105th Amendment

Restored the power of State governments to independently identify and maintain their own lists of Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBCs) / OBCs.
State OBC Lists Restored
2023
Narendra Modi (NDA)

106th Amendment

Passed as the Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam. Mandates a 33% reservation for women in the Lok Sabha, State Legislative Assemblies, and the NCT of Delhi Assembly.
33% Women's Quota
