📝 Proposers & Supporters

  • President of India: 50 Proposers + 50 Seconders.
    (Deposit: ₹15,000 in RBI)
  • Vice-President: 20 Proposers + 20 Seconders.
    (Deposit: ₹15,000 in RBI)
  • Rajya Sabha MP: 10% of the party's MLAs or 10 MLAs, whichever is less.
  • State Leg. Council (MLC): Minimum 10 persons.

⚠️ Forfeiture of Deposit: If a candidate fails to secure at least 1/6th of the total valid votes polled.

Fractions

🍕 The "Fraction" Rules in Constitution

Legislative Council (MLC) Composition

  • 1/3rd: Elected by Local Bodies (Municipalities, District Boards).
  • 1/3rd: Elected by State MLAs.
  • 1/12th: Elected by Graduates (3 yrs standing).
  • 1/12th: Elected by Teachers (3 yrs standing).
  • 1/6th: Nominated by Governor (Lit, Science, Art, Co-op, Social Svc).

Retirement & Tenures

  • Rajya Sabha / State Council: 1/3rd members retire every 2 years. (They enjoy a 6-year term).
  • Panchayat Women Quota: Minimum 1/3rd (33%) total seats & chairperson offices.

Anti-Defection (10th Schedule)

  • Merger Valid if: 2/3rd of legislature party members agree to merge.
Tenures

⏳ Unique Tenures & Bench Sizes

  • Panchayat Mid-Term Dissolution: If a Panchayat is dissolved before 5 years, the newly elected body serves ONLY the remainder of the term (not a fresh 5 years).
  • Constitution Bench (SC): Requires a minimum of 5 Judges (Art 145(3)).
  • Advisory Jurisdiction: When President consults SC (Art 143), minimum 5 Judges must sit.
  • Caretaker / Non-Member Minister: Maximum 6 Months without being an MP/MLA.
Terms

📖 Parliamentary Jargon Dictionary

Term Definition
Lame-Duck Session The last session of the existing Lok Sabha, after a new Lok Sabha has been elected. "Lame-ducks" are MPs who failed to get re-elected.
Guillotine Putting all undiscussed demands for grants to vote on the last day allotted for budget discussion, due to lack of time.
Casting Vote The vote cast by the Presiding Officer (Speaker/Chairman) ONLY to resolve a tie (They do not vote in the first instance).
Whip An official instruction issued by a political party to its MPs/MLAs to vote a certain way. Defying it triggers the Anti-Defection Law.
Sine Die (Adjournment) Terminating a sitting of Parliament for an indefinite period (without naming a day for reassembly). Done by Presiding Officer.
Question Hour Usually the first hour of a sitting. MPs ask questions (Starred = oral answer; Unstarred = written answer; Short-notice = <10 days notice).
Zero Hour Starts immediately after Question Hour. An Indian innovation (since 1962). MPs can raise matters of urgent public importance without prior notice.
