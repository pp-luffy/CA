Technology

Odisha Partners with Intel & 3DGS

  • MoU signed by Odisha Govt, Intel Corporation, and US-based 3D Glass Solutions (3DGS) for a semiconductor substrate manufacturing facility[cite: 1].
  • Estimated Investment: $3.3 billion, making it one of India's largest high-tech investments[cite: 1].
  • Location: Bhubaneswar-Khurda region[cite: 1].
  • Focus: Manufactures advanced packaging glass-core substrates and high-density interconnect substrates[cite: 1].
  • Alignment: Supports the India Semiconductor Mission for domestic ecosystem development[cite: 1].
  • Implementation Timeline: Phased over 5 to 6 years[cite: 1].
Infrastructure

Paradip Port Tops LPPI 2024-25

  • Paradip Port ranked 1st among major ports in the dry bulk cargo category under the Logistics Port Performance Index (LPPI) 2024-25[cite: 1].
  • Liquid Bulk: Sikka Port and Terminals (Gujarat) ranked 1st[cite: 1].
  • Container Cargo: Mundra Port (1st), followed by JNPA (2nd)[cite: 1].
  • Index launched by the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways[cite: 1].
  • Digital Reforms launched simultaneously: e-Navik (seafarer grievance redressal), e-Samudra (ship registration)[cite: 1].
  • Additional modules include the Medical Practitioner Module and Ship Recycling Credit Note Module (redeemable under ₹70,000-crore scheme)[cite: 1, 2].
Infrastructure

Cabinet Approves Coastal Highway Project

  • CCEA (chaired by PM Modi) approved a new coastal highway from Rameshwar to Paradeep[cite: 2].
  • Development Model: Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM)[cite: 2].
  • Project Length & Cost: 163.180 km in two packages at a capital cost of ₹8,300.79 crore[cite: 2].
  • Districts Covered: Khurda, Puri, Kendrapada, and Jagatsinghpur[cite: 2].
  • Need: Bypasses poor geometry and heavy traffic on existing Puri-Satapada & Puri-Konark stretches[cite: 2].
  • Integration: Aligns with the PM GatiShakti National Master Plan (launched Oct 13, 2021; ₹100 lakh crore initiative)[cite: 2, 3].
Government

BRICS Disaster Risk Reduction Meeting

  • The first in-person technical meeting of the BRICS DRR Working Group under India's Presidency commenced in Puri[cite: 3].
  • Odisha was chosen to host due to its global expertise in cyclone management and community-based early warning[cite: 3].
  • Participants: Delegates from 11 BRICS member and partner countries[cite: 3].
  • Focus Areas: Sustainable financing, resilient infrastructure, and integration of traditional knowledge[cite: 3].
  • BRICS Trivia: "BRIC" coined by Jim O'Neill (2001); 1st Summit in Russia (2009); Expanded in 2024 (Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, Saudi Arabia, UAE) and 2025 (Indonesia)[cite: 3].
Culture

Raja Festival Begins Across Odisha

  • A 3-day traditional festival marking Raja Sankranti[cite: 3].
  • Significance: Celebrates womanhood, Mother Earth, and the onset of the monsoon season[cite: 3].
  • Belief: Mother Earth undergoes her yearly menstrual cycle and is given rest[cite: 3].
  • Respect for Nature: Agricultural activities remain suspended during this period[cite: 3].
  • Key Attractions: Traditional swings and delicacies like Poda Pitha, Arisa Pitha, Manda, Kakera, and Raja Paan[cite: 3, 4].
Environment

AI-Based Crocodile Warning System

  • Location: Kusunpur village in Kendrapara district[cite: 4].
  • Initiative: Model village selected under the CSIR Smart Village programme (Lab to Land approach)[cite: 4].
  • Technology: An AI-enabled wildlife detection system installed at key entry points for real-time alerts[cite: 4].
  • Objective: Reduce human-crocodile conflicts near the Bhitarkanika ecosystem[cite: 4].
  • CSIR Facts: Established in September 1942, HQ New Delhi; PM acts as Ex-officio President[cite: 4].
Culture

Nilachakra Logo Secures Trademark

  • The Shree Jagannath Temple Administration obtained official logomark registration for the Nilachakra under the Trade Marks Act[cite: 4].
  • Wordmarks Granted: "Ananda Bajara" and "Patitapabana"[cite: 4].
  • Scale: Applications filed for 29 names, terms, and symbols associated with the Jagannath tradition[cite: 4].
  • Key Terms Defined: Jagannath Dham (pilgrimage centre), Mahaprasad (sacred food), Patitapaban (Lord's form visible from Lion's Gate)[cite: 4, 5].
  • Objective: Protect heritage symbols from unauthorized commercial misuse[cite: 4].
Technology

Coal-to-Ammonium Nitrate Project

  • PM Modi laid the foundation for India's first commercial-scale Coal-to-Ammonium Nitrate Project in June 2026[cite: 5].
  • Location: Lakhanpur, Jharsuguda district[cite: 5].
  • Project Cost: ₹25,016 crore[cite: 5].
  • Implementing Agency: BCGCL (a joint venture of BHEL and Coal India Limited) using indigenous coal gasification tech[cite: 5].
  • Land: ~350 acres possessed by Mahanadi Coalfields Limited (MCL)[cite: 5].
  • Support: Ministry of Coal provided ₹1,350 crore assistance[cite: 5].
Economy

Dried Whole Egg Powder Export to Austria

  • First-ever commercial export of dried whole egg powder from Odisha to Austria[cite: 5].
  • Facilitator: APEDA (Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority)[cite: 5].
  • Exporter: OVO Farm Pvt. Ltd., Balangir[cite: 5].
  • Consignment Size: 22.6 metric tonnes[cite: 5].
  • Facility Specs: Certified under FSSC 22000 Version 6, HALAL, and KOSHER[cite: 5].
  • APEDA Facts: Statutory body established in Dec 1985 under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry[cite: 5].
Government

Odisha Launched 'Go East' Initiative

  • Launched by CM Mohan Charan Majhi during the CII Eastern Regional Council Meeting in Bhubaneswar[cite: 5].
  • Stands for: Government of Odisha Eastern Investment Accelerator and Special Task Force[cite: 5].
  • Implementation: A dedicated Go-East Cell established within IPICOL[cite: 5].
  • Tracking: A separate Go-Swift module enables real-time monitoring of approvals[cite: 5].
  • Target Regions: Priority handling and dedicated help desk for investors from West Bengal, Bihar, Jharkhand, and Chhattisgarh[cite: 5].
