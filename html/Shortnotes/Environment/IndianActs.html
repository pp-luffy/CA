⚖️ All 12 Major Indian Biodiversity, Forest & Environmental Acts
Act Year & Amendments Core Scope, Statutory Bodies & Exam Traps
Indian Forest Act 1927 Consolidates forest laws; classifies forests into Reserved, Protected, and Village Forests; regulates timber transit.
Wild Life (Protection) Act (WPA) 1972 Amended 2022 Creates National Parks, WLS, Conservation & Community Reserves, Tiger Reserves. Establishes NTCA, WCCB, CZA, NBWL. 2022 Amendment: Rationalised Schedules from 6 to 4 & aligned with CITES.
Water (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act 1974 Amended 2024 Established CPCB and SPCBs to control water pollution.
Forest (Conservation) Act (FCA) 1980 Amended 2023 Restricts diversion of forest land for non-forest purposes without Central Govt approval. 2023 Amendment (Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam): added exemptions for strategic linear projects near borders.
Air (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act 1981 Empowers CPCB / SPCBs to control air (and later noise) pollution.
Environment (Protection) Act (EPA) 1986 Umbrella Legislation (enacted after Bhopal Gas Tragedy). Notifications like EIA, CRZ (Coastal Regulation Zone), ESZ (Eco-Sensitive Zones), and Wetlands Rules are issued under this Act.
Public Liability Insurance Act 1991 Mandatory insurance for immediate relief to victims of accidents involving hazardous substances.
PPV&FR Act 2001 Protection of Plant Varieties & Farmers' Rights Act — protects plant breeders' & farmers' rights over crop varieties.
Biological Diversity Act (BDA) 2002 Amended 2023 Implements CBD; 3-tier structure: NBA (National), SBBs (State), BMCs (Local — maintain People's Biodiversity Registers). 2023 Amendment: Decriminalised offences & eased compliance for AYUSH practitioners.
Forest Rights Act (FRA) 2006 Recognises Individual & Community Forest Rights (incl. Minor Forest Produce) of STs & OTFDs; empowers the Gram Sabha.
National Green Tribunal (NGT) Act 2010 Dedicated environmental court for speedy disposal under 7 scheduled laws. Trap: WPA 1972 & FRA 2006 are NOT under NGT jurisdiction!
CAF / CAMPA Act 2016 Manages Compensatory Afforestation Funds when forest land is diverted: 90% to States, 10% to Centre.
☀️ India's Climate Action Plan (NAPCC — 2008) & Recent Schemes

NAPCC (2008): Missions 1 to 4

  • 1. Jawaharlal Nehru National Solar Mission (JNNSM): Promote solar power generation & make solar competitive with fossil fuels.
  • 2. National Mission for Enhanced Energy Efficiency (NMEEE): Market-based energy efficiency via PAT Scheme (Perform, Achieve and Trade).
  • 3. National Mission on Sustainable Habitat (NMSH): Energy efficiency in buildings, urban waste management & public transport.
  • 4. National Water Mission (NWM): Integrated water resource management & 20% improvement in water use efficiency.

NAPCC (2008): Missions 5 to 8

  • 5. National Mission for Sustaining the Himalayan Ecosystem (NMSHE): Conserve Himalayan glaciers, biodiversity & mountain ecosystems.
  • 6. National Mission for a Green India (GIM): Afforestation & restoration of degraded forest ecosystems.
  • 7. National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA): Climate-resilient crops, soil health & micro-irrigation.
  • 8. National Mission on Strategic Knowledge for Climate Change (NMSKCC): Research, climate modelling & data networks.

Recent High-Yield National Schemes (2019–2023)

  • India Cooling Action Plan (ICAP) 2019: India was the first country in the world to develop a comprehensive cooling action plan.
  • National Clean Air Programme (NCAP) 2019: Target of 40% reduction in PM2.5 & PM10 levels in 131 non-attainment cities by 2025–26.
  • National Green Hydrogen Mission 2023: Make India a global hub for green hydrogen (target: 5 MMT/year by 2030).
  • MISHTI 2023: Mangrove plantation along India's coastlines.
  • Amrit Dharohar 2023: Optimal use & conservation of Ramsar Wetlands.
🐅 India's 9 Flagship Species Conservation Projects

1970s Classics (Hangul, Tiger, Crocodile)

  • Project Hangul 1970: Conservation of the Kashmir Stag (Hangul) in Dachigam NP, J&K.
  • Project Tiger 1973: Flagship tiger conservation scheme; administered by NTCA.
  • Project Crocodile 1975: Conservation of Gharial, Mugger & Saltwater Crocodile (notably in Bhitarkanika & Satkosia, Odisha).

1990s–2000s (Elephant, Vulture, Snow Leopard)

  • Project Elephant 1992: Protects elephants, habitats & corridors (linked with MIKE programme under CITES).
  • Vulture Conservation (Diclofenac Ban) 2006: Veterinary Diclofenac banned to save critically endangered vultures; Vulture Safe Zones created.
  • Project Snow Leopard 2009: Conservation of high-altitude Himalayan wildlife & ecosystems.

2020s Projects (Dolphin, Lion, Cheetah)

  • Project Dolphin 2020: Conservation of both Gangetic/river & marine dolphins.
  • Project Lion 2020: Conservation & landscape management of Asiatic Lions (Gir, Gujarat).
  • Project Cheetah 2022: World's first intercontinental large wild carnivore translocation (Namibia / South Africa → Kuno NP, MP).
