May 2026

Important Dates (May)

  • World Metrology Day (20 May): Theme 2026 is "Metrology: Building Trust in Policy Making"[cite: 2, 3]. It celebrates the signature of the Metre Convention on 20 May 1875 by representatives of 17 nations[cite: 3]. India became the 13th country worldwide to issue OIML approval certificates[cite: 3].
  • National Dengue Day (16 May): Led by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare to raise awareness[cite: 4, 5]. Dengue is caused by the dengue virus (Flaviviridae family) and spread by infected Aedes mosquitoes[cite: 5].
  • International Leopard Day (3 May): Officialized in 2023 by the Cape Leopard Trust (CLT)[cite: 6, 7]. Indian Leopards inhabit regions excluding mangroves and deserts[cite: 7]. The India-headquartered IBCA aims to conserve 7 big cats; India natively inhabits 5 (excluding Puma and Jaguar)[cite: 7].
🎯 OPSC ASO Alert: India issues OIML certificates (13th globally). IBCA covers 7 big cats, but India lacks Puma and Jaguar!
June 2026

Important Dates (June)

  • World Ocean Day (8 June): Highlights oceans' role in climate regulation, oxygen production, and carbon absorption[cite: 8, 9]. Theme 2026: "Strong Marine Protected Areas for Our Blue Planet"[cite: 9].
  • World Day Against Child Labour (12 June): Highlights eliminating exploitative practices[cite: 10, 11]. Theme 2026: "Red Card to Child Labour: Fair Play for Children, Decent Work for Adults"[cite: 11].
  • World Dengue Day (15 June): Theme 2026: "One World Against Dengue"[cite: 12, 13]. Dengue affects >130 countries and has become a year-round challenge due to climate change[cite: 13].
🎯 OPSC ASO Alert: Note the distinction: National Dengue Day is May 16, whereas World Dengue Day is June 15!

September 2026
