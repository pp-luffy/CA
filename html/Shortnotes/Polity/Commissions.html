Centre-State

A. Sarkaria Commission (1983)

Chairperson: Justice R.S. Sarkaria (Indira Gandhi govt; report 1987)

Core Mandate: Review working arrangements between Centre & States.

Top Exam Recommendations:

  • Article 356 (President’s Rule): Use very sparingly as last resort. Warning must be issued prior to imposition.
  • Inter-State Council: Set up a permanent body under Article 263 (established in 1990).
  • Governor's Appt: Eminent persons outside state, NOT active politicians of Centre's ruling party. Consult State CM. 5-year tenure undisturbed arbitrarily.
  • Residuary Powers: Legislative stays with Parliament, but taxation residuary stays with states (not fully accepted).
Centre-State

B. Punchhi Commission (2007)

Chairperson: Justice Madan Mohan Punchhi (Former CJI; UPA govt; report 2010)

Core Mandate: Fresh look at Centre-State relations mapping 2 decades of economic/polity changes.

Top Exam Recommendations:

  • Localizing President’s Rule: Localize Art 356 to specific troubled districts instead of dissolving entire state assembly.
  • Governor’s Removal: Drop the "Doctrine of Pleasure". State legislature must have a role/consultative mechanism.
  • Concurrent List: Consult states prior to Centre introducing legislation on subjects.
  • Internal Security: Create a specialized federal agency for terror crimes ➔ led to NIA.
Panchayati Raj

A. Balwant Rai Mehta (1957)

Context: Appointed to examine Community Dev Programme (1952) & National Extension Service.

Core Rec: Democratic Decentralization

➔ Three-Tier System

  1. Gram Panchayat (Village level)
  2. Panchayat Samiti (Block level) ➔ Executive body
  3. Zila Parishad (District level) ➔ Advisory, coordinating & supervisory body
Panchayati Raj

B. Ashok Mehta (1977)

Context: Janata Party govt to revive declining PRIs.

➔ Two-Tier System

  1. Zila Parishad (District level)
  2. Mandal Panchayat (Group of villages)

➔ Official participation of political parties in elections + Regular constitutional recognition.

C. L.M. Singhvi (1986)

Context: Rajiv Gandhi govt.

  • Constitutional Status: Add a separate chapter in Constitution for PRIs.
  • Nyaya Panchayats: Establish village courts for speedy justice.

Note: Directly laid groundwork for 73rd & 74th Amendments (1992).

Other Polity Committees

Swaran Singh (1976)

  • Recommended inclusion of Fundamental Rights & Duties.
  • ➔ Resulted in addition of Fundamental Duties via 42nd Amendment Act.

Sarkaria vs. Rajamannar (1969)

  • Rajamannar (TN): Radical. Demanded TOTAL abolition of Art 356, All-India Services (IAS/IPS), and Planning Commission.
  • Sarkaria (Centre): Moderate. Favored a strong Centre paired with cooperative state safeguards.
