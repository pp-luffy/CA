Basics

📚 River Concepts & Regimes

  • Catchment Area: Specific area from which a river collects water.
  • Watershed / Water Divide: Elevated boundary separating two drainage basins.
  • River Regime: The seasonal rise and fall of water volume.
Himalayan Rivers Peninsular Rivers
Perennial (Rain + Glaciers) Seasonal (Monsoon dependent)
Antecedent / Dendritic pattern Concordant / Super-imposed pattern
Youthful, high erosion, shifting courses, forms large Deltas Mature, graded profiles, stable courses, forms smaller deltas or Estuaries

Drainage Patterns (Crucial)
  • Antecedent: Existed before Himalayan uplift. Deep gorges. (Indus, Sutlej, Brahmaputra).
  • Super-Imposed: River cuts through and ignores underlying rock structure (Damodar, Chambal, Banas).
  • Dendritic: Tree branch shape (Ganga, Northern Plains).
  • Radial: Flowing outwards from a central dome (Amarkantak sending Narmada West and Son North).
  • Centripetal: Flowing inwards to a central depression (Loktak Lake, Sambhar Lake, Chilika Lake).
⚠️ Prelims Trap Alert: You might see "Teesta" listed as a Major Basin in MCQs. It is a minor basin! The major Himalayan ones are Ganga, Indus, and Brahmaputra.
OPSC Special

🔥 Odisha Drainage Master

Odisha rivers are primarily Peninsular, non-perennial, and flow East into the Bay of Bengal. Crucial for ASO!
Mahanadi (The Lifeline)
Origin: Sihawa Range, Bastar hills (Chhattisgarh). Enters Odisha at Jharsuguda.
Total Length: 851 km (494 km in Odisha).
🧱 Major Dam: Hirakud (Sambalpur) - Longest earthen dam.
Distributaries (Forms delta): Kathjodi, Kuakhai, Birupa, Devi, Daya (flows into Chilika).
Tributaries: Seonath, Jonk, Hasdeo, Ib, Mand, Tel, Ong.
Brahmani & Baitarani
Brahmani: 2nd largest in Odisha. Formed by the confluence of Sankh and South Koel rivers at Vedvyas (Rourkela).
🧱 Dam: Rengali Dam.
Baitarani: Originates from Gonasika hills (Keonjhar). Often called the 'Ganga of Odisha' mythologically. Flows into Bay of Bengal via Dhamra mouth (along with Brahmani).
Other Key Rivers
Subarnarekha: Originates in Chhotanagpur plateau. Forms boundary between West Bengal and Odisha.
Rushikulya: Originates from Daringbadi hills (Kandhamal). ⚠️ Trap: Does NOT form a delta! Famous for Olive Ridley turtle mass nesting (Arribada) at Purunabandh.
Budhabalanga: Originates in Similipal massif. Forms the spectacular 💦 Barehipani Falls.
Vansadhara: Originates in Kalahandi. Shared with Andhra Pradesh.
Odisha Lakes & Lagoons
Chilika: Largest brackish water lagoon in Asia. Spread over Puri, Khurda, Ganjam. Receives water from Daya and Bhargavi rivers. Nalabana Bird Sanctuary is located here.
Ansupa: Largest freshwater lake in Odisha. Horseshoe-shaped. Left bank of Mahanadi (Banki, Cuttack).
Kanjia: Freshwater lake inside Nandankanan (Bhubaneswar).

Ganga System

🙏 The Ganga Basin

  • India's Largest Basin. Total length: 2,525 km.
  • Formation: Confluence of Bhagirathi (from Gangotri) and Alaknanda (from Satopanth) at Devprayag.
  • 🧱 Major Dams: Tehri Dam (Highest in India). Farakka Barrage.
🌊 The Panch Prayag
1. Vishnuprayag: Alaknanda + Dhauli Ganga
2. Nandprayag: Alaknanda + Nandakini
3. Karnaprayag: Alaknanda + Pindar
4. Rudraprayag: Alaknanda + Mandakini
5. Devprayag: Alaknanda + Bhagirathi (Becomes Ganga)
⬅️ Left Bank Tributaries
Ramganga, Gomati.
Ghaghara: Mapchachungo Glacier.
Gandak & Kosi: Kosi is the Sorrow of Bihar (shifting courses).
Mahananda: Last left-hand tributary in India.
➡️ Right Bank Tributaries
Yamuna (Longest Trib - 1376km)
Source: Yamunotri Glacier (Bandarpunch). Joins Ganga at Prayagraj.
Tribs: Chambal, Sindh, Betwa, Ken.
Chambal (Super-imposed)
Famous for Badland topography (Ravines).
🧱 Dams: Gandhi Sagar, Jawahar Sagar, Rana Pratap Sagar.
Son & Damodar
Son: Originates at Amarkantak.
Damodar: Flows through rift valley. Sorrow of Bengal.
Indus System

🏔️ River Indus (Sindhu)

  • Source: Bokhar Chu Glacier (Tibet). Known as Singi Khamban (Lion's mouth).
  • Course: Antecedent. Flows NW between Ladakh and Zaskar ranges. Deep gorge at Nanga Parbat.
  • Mithankot: Receives the Panjnad (Satluj, Beas, Ravi, Chenab, Jhelum).
⬅️ Left Bank Tributaries (Panjnad)
Jhelum
Source: Verinag, Pir Panjal. Flows through Wular Lake (Tectonic). 🧱 Uri Dam.
Chenab (Longest Tributary)
Source: Bara Lacha Pass. Formed by Chandra + Bhaga rivers.
🧱 Dams: Baglihar, Dulhasti, Rattle (J&K).
Ravi & Beas
Ravi: Kullu Hills.
Beas: Flows entirely within India! 🧱 Pong Dam, Pandoh Dam.
Sutlej (Antecedent)
Source: Rakas Lake (Mansarovar). Enters India via Shipki La Pass.
🧱 Dams: Bhakra Nangal, Nathpa Jhakri.
Brahma System

🐉 River Brahmaputra

  • Source: Chemayungdung Glacier (Tibet). Known as Yarlung Tsangpo.
  • Course: Antecedent. U-turn at Namcha Barwa syntaxial bend. Enters Arunachal as Siang/Dihang.
  • Majuli: World's largest riverine island (Assam).
⬅️ Left Bank Tributaries
Dibang, Lohit, Burhi Dihing, Dhansari.
➡️ Right Bank Tributaries
Teesta: Source: Too Lhamo Lake (Fed by Zemu Glacier).
Subansiri: Gold River. 🧱 Subansari Dam.
Others: Kameng, Manas, Sankosh.
West Flowing

🌅 Arabian Sea Drainage (23%)

✍️ Mains Analytical Hook: Why no deltas on the west coast? Short, swift-flowing rivers over hard gradient rock carry minimal silt. They drop directly into the deep Arabian sea, forming Estuaries instead of deltas.
Narmada & Tapi (Rift Valley Twins)
Narmada (1,312km): Amarkantak (Radial). Flows through rift valley between Vindhya and Satpura.
🧱 Dams: Sardar Sarovar. 💦 Dhuandhar Falls.
Tapi: Betul Plateau. Flows south of Satpura range in parallel rift valley.
🏙️ Surat. 🧱 Ukai Dam.
Western Ghats Coastals
Sharavati (KA): 💦 Jog Falls.
Mandovi & Zuari (Goa).
Periyar (KL): 🧱 Idduki, Mulaperiyar.
East Flowing

🌄 Bay of Bengal Drainage (77%)

Godavari (1,465 km) - Dakshin Ganga
Source: Trimbakeshwar (Nashik, MH). Largest peninsular basin.
🧱 Dams: Jayakwadi, Polavaram, Sriram Sagar.
Tribs: Penganga, Wardha, Wainganga, Indravati, Sabari, Manjra.
Krishna (1,400 km)
Source: Mahabaleshwar (MH).
🧱 Dams: Srisailam, Nagarjuna Sagar (Largest Masonry).
Tribs: Tungabhadra (Largest), Koyna, Bhima, Musi.
Kaveri (The Regime Exception)
Source: Brahmagiri Hills (KA).
⚠️ Unique Regime: Unlike other peninsular rivers, Kaveri is nearly perennial. Gets rain from South-West monsoon (summer) in upper catchment, and North-East monsoon (winter) in lower catchment.
🧱 Dams: Krishnarajsagar, Mettur, Kallanai (Oldest).
